# ADR-008 — The MVP is the YC demo plus a live invite-first beta

Date: 2026-10-29. Owner: GenUI. Status: accepted founder direction ([disposition record](../../evidence/APP-006/founder-disposition-2026-10-29.md)); two budget and transport choices remain Victor's and are listed below. This amends the sequencing of [ADR-005](ADR-005-PHASE-1-SEQUENCING.md) and the acceptance ownership of ADR-003's hosted items; it changes no behavior contract in ADR-003, ADR-004, ADR-006 or ADR-007.

## Decision

1. **The MVP is two things on one build.** The Northstar demo of the demo design (six acts, S01–S07, one image digest, the packet) recorded for YC, and the same build deployed at `https://beta.getforma.dev` as an invite-first beta that invited viewers can sign up to and use on synthetic data, labeled beta, with no SLA. Phases 1–4 deliver it; the platform-v2 design remains the code's design and the demo design its reduction.
2. **Ceremony deferred, not waived.** The joint six-hour identity rehearsal, the three-browser certification runs, the deployed negative matrix, the KMS rotation campaign and the Canary-versus-Northstar resource check move to **APP-079** (PH06, consumer of FORMA-365). GenUI's snapshot/restore drill, two-process race coordinator on RDS and drift/replica trials move to **APP-080** (PH05). Both carry the moved sentences verbatim and block any real organization.
3. **Beta-critical hosted items stay in Phase 1.** Real invited signup with mailbox verification (ADR-006 routes against FormaStack's mail image, FORMA-366), the admitted query → receipt → command → reopen journey in one browser on the live deployment, canary 404s over public HTTPS, and the migrations, seed, qualification-entry run and HTTP surface on RDS. "Fail forward" means a found bug is fixed and redeployed, not that the item is skipped.
4. **Gates.** FORMA-247 and FORMA-248 are cleared by disposition in the external register with the disposition record as evidence; FORMA-366 (the qualified mail image deployed for the beta) blocks APP-076, the go-live trial; FORMA-365 (deferred hosted acceptance) blocks APP-058 and APP-079. APP-006 proceeds on its local consumer qualification and the hash-bound merged contract (gatewasm PR18/19 at `dfba787`, PR20 at `1bf5d3b`), with its hosted items moved verbatim to APP-079.
5. **Re-scoped tickets.** APP-078 keeps readiness comparison, migrations, seed, role bindings, the grant/RLS isolation matrix, the HTTPS surface and cleanup; APP-076 becomes the beta go-live trial (installed Chrome, real mail, private-route and canary refusals, migrations applied, rollback as a redeploy, service left running for the beta) and takes APP-074's hosted items verbatim; APP-077 keeps the operator package and hands the coordinator to APP-080; APP-074 proves the public invite entry, limits, trusted proxy and admission races on the local harness against the test-only synthetic issuer. APP-079 carries native Safari and Firefox, the deployed negatives, the rotation campaign and the canary resource check; APP-080 the recovery drill, pool-lifecycle, two-order concurrency and drift rows on RDS.
6. **Phase 1 exit criteria** are amended in `Tickets/plan.json` to name these owners; the gate record lists each deferral with its ticket, per QUALITY-GATES.
7. **Repositories.** This repository stays public under its all-rights-reserved notice until the beta deployment is complete, so GitHub Actions run (Victor, 2026-10-29); gatewasm is public under BUSL-1.1 (FormaStack, PR 21). Both return to private only by Victor's decision.

## Victor's pending choices (defaults in bold)

- **Hosting budget for a live beta.** [SYNTHETIC-ENVIRONMENT-COST.md](../planning/SYNTHETIC-ENVIRONMENT-COST.md) prices the always-on synthetic environment (one 2 vCPU/8 GiB Fargate task, RDS, ALB, addresses, keys and a modest usage allowance) at about $150–170/month and says the steady-state plan must be requalified before inviting external beta users; FormaStack estimates a lean always-on fAuth at about $33/month. The default is **about $185–205/month while the beta is advertised**; the alternative is a scheduled daily service window to stay nearer the $100 cap. ADR-003's $100/month was set for a bounded 48-hour qualification window and the earlier $200 always-on option was not approved, so an explicit new figure is needed.
- **Mail transport.** **Amazon SES SMTP credentials in the same account with a verified `getforma.dev` sender, configured through fAuth's existing SMTP secret hook by FormaStack, DNS records by GenUI**; the alternative is Resend. Without a transport, invited outsiders cannot verify and the beta cannot admit them.

## Consequences

The execution plan's Track C becomes the beta go-live path: FormaStack deploys fAuth lean (about 1–1.5 hours of its work) once the budget and transport are approved; GenUI deploys the final reviewed build with migrations applied (APP-078), provisions invite codes, and runs APP-076 against it in installed Chrome. The deferred ceremony is recorded in the PH01 gate record as a named limitation, not omitted. Phase 2 (imports, interpretation, canvas, forms) and Phases 3–4 (authoring, AI, publication, two-app proof) follow for the demo's acts 2–6.
