## Context

ADR-010 cut the November 2 MVP to Phase 1 live: invite, verified sign-in, governed query with receipt, explicit command, restart/reopen. That is live at `https://beta.getforma.dev` since 2026-10-30 (APP-078 window, APP-076 trial; records in `evidence/APP-078/window/` and `evidence/APP-076/trial/`). It proves the platform's backbone, but it does not show the product: a company describing an app, seeing it appear on a canvas, running it on its data and sharing it with a teammate. For a YC investment conversation, the backbone alone reads as a course project. Working prototypes of the missing experience exist beside this repository: Forma Vector Studio (describe an interface; Claude generates reusable Forma modules), Forma Generative UI (an intent-driven infinite canvas routing through a vector index of published modules) and Forma GenUI Runtime (the reusable canvas layer).

## Decision

1. **The November 2 MVP is the pitch demo**: on the live beta, a signed-in member of an organization describes an app; it appears on a canvas assembled from reusable, admitted components (retrieved, adapted or generated, and labeled as such); its views run on the organization's data through the governed query path with visible receipts; the member shares it with a teammate, who opens it under the teammate's own permissions; a non-member cannot see it.
2. **The join happens in a clone.** The integration of the canvas and composer prototypes with the backbone is built in `FormaBuiltApps/forma-pitch-mvp` on branch `pivot/pitch-mvp` (cloned from `phase-one-spikes` at `21199f4`, pushed). `forma-app-platform`'s planned track, its board and the live beta's evidence stay intact. What the clone proves is landed back into the platform only through reviewed tickets after November 2 (section 5).
3. **Invariants that still hold in the clone** (ADR-010 §2, unchanged): verified identity; tenant isolation (RLS, uniform 404); receipts on every governed number; transactional, idempotent, audited commands; model output is data, never code, SQL, markup or grants — a generated component enters only as a typed definition through admission, and every AI result is labeled; no credential, address or code in public evidence.
4. **What is relaxed for November 2 only**: client-side canvas islands beyond APP-003's qualification (the GenUI runtime canvas, measured but not budget-gated); a model call for composition (Claude, through Vector Studio's path, with the key from a secret, never argv or logs); review depth is risk-tiered as in ADR-010 §4 (Tier A only for auth, isolation and admission seams).
5. **Planning is paused, not abandoned.** Every unfinished ADR-010 item is carried by a ticket in Backlog, with nothing deleted or rewritten: the APP-076 trial remainder (record reopen, canary replay, T5 revocation, T9 attachment clearing and FormaStack's key rotation, the laptop retry/conflict rows), the PH01 gate record, APP-078's qualification row (APP-086), APP-089 image hardening, APP-090 UI fixes and the designer handoff, the review residuals (APP-014, APP-084), and PH02–PH07 as planned. The ADR-010 §6 pick-up order remains the order.
6. **Revisit on November 3** (ticket APP-097): decide what the pitch clone keeps and how it lands in the platform (ticket by ticket, through the normal review tiers), close the APP-076 remainder and the PH01 gate, and resume the ADR-010 §6 order with the pitch's lessons applied to PH02 Track C and PH03.

**Amendment, 2026-10-30 (Victor: "we want to wow the YC panel... the AI picking and or creating the apps... the apps being created on a canvas... sharing and real data and real time updates... pre-built components... switch as fast as the GenUI does").** The pitch is library-first: a prebuilt library of admitted business apps on realistic synthetic data, picked by a fast model router (Claude Haiku, about a second) and placed on a multi-app workspace canvas; generation (Claude Sonnet, typed definitions through admission) is the fallback when nothing fits, labeled as generated; sharing with a teammate under the sharer's own permissions; and real-time updates (a server-sent event stream re-authorized at delivery) when an app is shared or a record is committed. Forma Generative UI's local token-hash router and its consumer widgets are not used (they misroute the pitch's own phrasing); its canvas geometry and interaction are (GenUI Runtime). The query engine gains table, grouped and filtered views within the existing operator contract. Built on sub-branches `pivot/canvas`, `pivot/composer`, `pivot/library`, `pivot/realtime` and `pivot/preflight` of the clone. (Recorded on `phase-one-spikes` of `forma-app-platform` at `21159f5`; mirrored into this clone on 2026-10-30 after the APP-095 review, finding M3.)

**Amendment, 2026-10-30 (APP-095 independent review, M3): what "sharing" grants.** A share **copies** onto the teammate the subset of the sharer's own current permissions that opening the work requires (`discover` and `run` on the app, `record_read` on the work, `record_write` only when asked for and held, and `source_read` on each of the work's source dependencies the sharer holds), through the definer `genui_private.share_work` (migration 0008). §1's "opens it under the teammate's own permissions" therefore reads: the teammate's permissions **after** the share, which are the sharer's, copied, and the clause every reader passes (creator-or-grant, `run`, live `source_read` on every dependency) is then evaluated against the teammate; the teammate holds nothing the sharer did not hold and nothing the definitions do not carry. `source_read` is copied because the teammate needs it to open the work, and by the 0002 model (`source_permissions` keyed `(organization, principal, source)`) it is **organization-wide**: the teammate may afterwards read that source through any app it can `run`, and reversing the share does not take it back. Every share that wrote a row leaves one `genui.work_shares` row naming exactly the inserted permissions, referenced by its `permission.granted` event (whose `outcome_digest` recomputes from the row), so a share is reconstructable and reversible row by row; the unshare route itself is not built (debt item 7).

## Consequences

- The live beta keeps running the Phase 1 build until the pitch build is deployed; the pitch deploys to the same environment only through a reviewed change set, and the Phase 1 rollback revision stays available.
- Two lines of work run in parallel until November 3: the pitch clone (APP-091–096) and nothing else on the platform board except Victor's pending trial checks.
- The pitch's shortcuts are visible debt: each is named in its ticket and in APP-097, so the revisit can pay it or carry it.
- **Debt recorded by `pivot/realtime` (APP-095, 2026-10-30):** (1) migration 0008 adds a seventh `genui_admit` definer, `genui_private.share_work`, that writes permission rows (ADR-007 D7.1 names six; D7.3 says permission rows are written only by `admit_redemption` and the operator channel) and extends A4 with column SELECTs on `memberships.status`, `saved_work`, `work_source_dependencies` and the three permission tables; (2) the admission chain carries `permission.granted` (ADR-007 D7.8 lists it on the operator chain only; the kind is reused rather than a new kind invented, so `verify` needs no change); (3) the members list shows a neutral label derived from the principal id because 0002 principals carry no display name; (4) the event hub is in-process, correct for D7.18's single task and a gap for a multi-task deployment (APP-061); (5) an open event stream is ended after one hour and on every epoch change, but it does not itself refresh the session's idle clock and a session's absolute expiry does not close it before that hour. **Added 2026-10-30 after the independent review (`evidence/APP-095/independent-review.md`):** (6) the event stream's residual amplification on the ADR-004 data pool is one lock-free scoped read per announced event per organization with at least one subscriber (`Data::openable_principals`, judged once and shared by every stream of that organization; review M2 removed the per-subscriber transaction) plus one unfenced currency read per open stream per 30 s re-check, so a burst of commits still costs one data-pool lease per commit alongside the command itself; a verdict cache or a database notification channel is the APP-061 shape; (7) there is no unshare route: a share is reversible only through the operator's `revoke-permission`, row by row, guided by the `work_shares` record, and the copied organization-wide `source_read` is not taken back by reversing the share (M3 amendment above). APP-097 decides whether ADR-007 is amended or the path is reworked.
- The PH01 gate is not marked passed by the pivot; it passes only when its checklist is complete (`evidence/phases/PH01/GATE.md`).
