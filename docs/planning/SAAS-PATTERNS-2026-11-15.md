# How mature workspace SaaS structures its outside pages and its tenancy, compared with Forma Canvas

Written 2026-11-15. Sources were fetched live that day by three research passes; repo facts were read at `forma-ops` (`origin/main`) and the live beta. "(inferred)" marks what I concluded rather than read.

## Summary

1. Before the class, change nothing structural: invite-by-code, a notice, sign-in by workspace and `/admin` is how Linear, HEY and Bluesky ran their betas. Pre-class items are small risk reducers only: spare codes in the Nov 28 batch, the post-verification "continue" link, a contact line and a stable notice URL in the footer release, and plain words about the `auth-staging` host in the invite email.
2. Brand: framework-plus-product companies put the product on a section of the company site (laravel.com/cloud, deno.com/deploy, expo.dev/services) and the app on a path or a `console.`/`app.` subdomain. Recommend `getforma.dev/canvas` after the class and `app.getforma.dev` at public launch, `beta.` redirecting.
3. Sign-in: no peer sends the browser to an `auth.`/`accounts.` host for the login page. Keep `/signin` on the app; rename `auth-staging` to `auth.getforma.dev` after the class, since the callback is registered exactly.
4. Workspace model: the leaders converge on one identity across many workspaces, a top-left switcher, path slugs, an owner/admin/member/guest ladder, free viewers and paid editors, owner-only deletion, self-serve export, and audit logs and SSO/SCIM at the top tiers. Forma has one fAuth tenant per organization (Slack's old model), no switcher or discovery, opaque handles, Admin/Member plus share guests, compiled limits and no export.
5. Keep per-tenant identity through the tiers work and make it livable as Slack did: find my workspaces (APP-163, blocked on FORMA-368) and the switcher (APP-164) after the class. Revisit a Forma-wide identity at public launch.
6. Invites: email invites with a 7-day expiry, a pending list, cancellation and an admins-only default are universal; links and domain auto-join are a collaboration-tool extra. APP-165 should deliver those four after the class; codes stay the operator's tool.
7. Guests: item-scoped guests (Notion pages, Figma files, Slack channels) are the norm; ADR-014's named, expiring shares already fit.
8. Pages outside the app: all seven peers publish terms, privacy, DPA, sub-processors, a security page, `security.txt`, an outsourced status page, a changelog, pricing and support. Forma has none on either domain; the notice and `feedback@` do that work today.
9. After the class: counsel-reviewed terms and privacy, `security.txt`, a hosted status page fed by APP-218's probes, and APP-114's landing with request access, not a waitlist. Security page, DPA, changelog and pricing at public launch.
10. Cookies: only a `__Host-` session cookie exists, so state "no non-essential cookies, no banner" in the privacy page, as Linear and Vercel do.

## 1. Domain and brand architecture

**Convergent pattern.** Marketing on the apex; the login page on the same host (slack.com/signin, linear.app/login, figma.com/login, vercel.com/login, github.com/login, app.notion.com/login); none uses a separate `auth.` host for the browser step. Status always on its own domain (slack-status.com, notion-status.com, linearstatus.com, vercel-status.com, githubstatus.com; Figma alone uses status.figma.com). Changelog and legal as apex paths. The app lives on an apex path (github.com, vercel.com/dashboard, figma.com/files, linear.app/<workspace>), on `app.` (Notion moved to app.notion.com in 2026) or on a per-workspace subdomain (Slack only). Framework-plus-product companies never give the product its own domain: Laravel markets Forge/Cloud/Nightwatch at laravel.com/forge etc. and runs the apps at forge./cloud./nightwatch.laravel.com; Deno at deno.com/deploy with console.deno.com; Expo at expo.dev/services with expo.dev/login; Supabase at supabase.com/dashboard; Prisma at console.prisma.io; Tailwind Plus at tailwindcss.com/plus. The framework keeps its own domain only when it outgrows the company (nextjs.org). Sources: [Slack URL](https://slack.com/help/articles/221769328-Locate-your-Slack-URL), [Notion login](https://www.notion.so/login), [Linear model](https://linear.app/docs/conceptual-model), [Vercel dashboard](https://vercel.com/dashboard), [Laravel Cloud](https://cloud.laravel.com/), [Deno Deploy](https://docs.deno.com/deploy/), [Expo](https://docs.expo.dev/eas/), [Supabase](https://supabase.com/docs/guides/platform), [Prisma Console](https://console.prisma.io/login), [Tailwind Plus](https://tailwindcss.com/plus).

**Forma today.** `getforma.dev` is the Stack site (docs, learn, playground, blog; static). `beta.getforma.dev` is the app with its own landing (`/`, `/about`), `/signin`, `/signin/{handle}` and `/invite`. The credential step is on `auth-staging.getforma.dev` (fAuth, PAR/PKCE, exact callback registered per ADR-003). `getforma.dev/canvas`, `app.`, `canvas.` and `status.` do not exist (checked 2026-11-15). The family brand "forma. / canvas" is set in FORMA-BRAND.md.

**Gap.** Canvas has no home on the company site; "beta" is in the app's hostname; "staging" is in the host students see at password time; the app's landing doubles as the marketing page.

**Recommendation.** Nothing before the class: a rename is a deploy, and a deploy signs everyone out. After the class, a Canvas section at `getforma.dev/canvas` (APP-114's content). At public launch, `app.getforma.dev` with `beta.` redirecting, `auth.getforma.dev` for fAuth, `/signin` staying on the app host. `canvas.getforma.dev` only if Canvas becomes the bigger brand (the Next.js case); not today's decision.

## 2. The landing page in invite-only beta

**Convergent pattern.** Linear ran a waitlist with hand-issued invites from April 2019 to June 2020 ([changelog](https://linear.app/changelog/2020-06-30)); Superhuman "threw up a landing page" collecting an email plus a short survey and kept a 275,000-person waitlist "by design" ([Lenny](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/how-the-biggest-consumer-apps-got), [First Round](https://review.firstround.com/how-superhuman-built-an-engine-to-find-product-market-fit)); Arc and v0 used waitlists; HEY and Bluesky added invite codes ([Bluesky](https://bsky.social/about/blog/02-06-2024-join-bluesky)). Practitioners agree on a short page: "a domain name, your company name, a short description, a contact, and a call to action", no "marketing robot" voice, no six months of polish ([YC](https://ycombinator.com/blog/startup-school-week-4-recap-kat-manalac-and-gustaf-alstromer/)); "waitlists rot", so qualify sign-ups ([a16z speedrun](https://speedrun.substack.com/p/the-growth-meta-how-to-build-a-waitlist)). Pages that are the app host (v0.dev, bsky.app) show Sign in beside the request CTA (inferred).

**Forma today.** `/` on beta has a hero, a "private beta" label, "Enter an invitation code", "Sign in", three value steps and "Built on Forma Stack"; `/invite` carries the notice with the consent checkbox; `/about` links to the Stack site. No contact line, FAQ, security section or request-access path; the footer is in review. APP-114 (backlog) plans the getforma.dev landing with hero, how it works, security, FAQ, a beta list form and a legal footer.

**Gap.** A visitor without a code has nowhere to go; the only contact is inside the notice; the notice has no stable URL after consent (`/notice` is 404 live).

**Recommendation.** Before the class, only the footer release: a contact line and a link to the notice at a stable URL. After the class, APP-114 at `getforma.dev/canvas` with "Request access" (role and use, answered by hand) rather than a waitlist counter, a security section in plain words, an FAQ and the legal footer. No logos, certifications, waitlist count or cookie banner.

## 3. The workspace model

**Convergent pattern.** One identity, many workspaces (Notion, Linear, Figma, Vercel, GitHub); Slack keeps an account per workspace stitched by email, and GitHub's enterprise-managed users are the deliberate one-account-per-tenant exception ([Slack](https://slack.com/help/articles/212675257-Join-a-Slack-workspace), [Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/create-delete-and-switch-workspaces), [Linear](https://linear.app/docs/workspaces), [GitHub EMU](https://docs.github.com/en/enterprise-cloud@latest/admin/managing-iam/understanding-iam-for-enterprises/about-enterprise-managed-users)). Any signed-in user can create a workspace. Discovery by email only after a mailbox proof: Slack emails a code, then lists workspaces ([Slack sign-in](https://slack.com/help/articles/212681477-Sign-in-to-Slack)); nobody offers an anonymous lookup. A top-left switcher everywhere. Path slugs dominate (`linear.app/acme`, `vercel.com/acme`, `github.com/acme`); Slack's subdomain is the legacy outlier ([Linear](https://linear.app/now/how-we-built-multi-region-support-for-linear)). Domain auto-join in Slack, Notion, Linear and Figma, not Vercel or GitHub ([Slack](https://slack.com/help/articles/115004856503-Manage-how-people-join-your-workspace), [Linear](https://linear.app/docs/invite-members)). An owner/admin/member/guest ladder, with one primary owner at Slack and Notion and functional roles (billing, security) at Vercel and GitHub ([Slack](https://slack.com/help/articles/360018112273-Types-of-roles-in-Slack), [Linear](https://linear.app/docs/members-roles), [Vercel](https://vercel.com/docs/rbac/access-roles), [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/managing-peoples-access-to-your-organization-with-roles/roles-in-an-organization)). A paid seat per editing human, viewers and limited guests free; free tiers cap content and history, not headcount ([Figma](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360039960434-Manage-seats-in-Figma), [Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/plan-downgrade)). Deletion is owner-only with the name typed; grace from none (Slack, Notion) to 48 hours (Linear) and 28 days (Figma); GitHub reserves the name 90 days and will not let the last owner leave ([GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/managing-organization-settings/deleting-an-organization-account)). Export is self-serve on content tools ([Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/export-your-content), [Linear](https://linear.app/docs/exporting-data)). Audit logs are enterprise-gated except GitHub's basic org log ([Linear](https://linear.app/docs/audit-log), [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/keeping-your-organization-secure/managing-security-settings-for-your-organization/reviewing-the-audit-log-for-your-organization)). SAML sits one tier below SCIM in most products ([Notion](https://www.notion.com/help/saml-sso-configuration), [Vercel](https://vercel.com/docs/directory-sync)).

**Forma today.** Organization = workspace (ADR-016). One fAuth tenant per organization, so a person in two organizations has two accounts, and guests sign in through the sharing organization's tenant (ADR-014). Workspaces are operator-provisioned (ADR-003). No discovery (APP-163, blocked on FORMA-368), no switcher (APP-164), no organization in app URLs; the sign-in URL carries an opaque 26-character handle or a configured alias (APP-117 step 1). No domain auto-join. Roles Admin and Member through permissions, guests through named shares; `/admin` lists and removes members with `self` and `last_admin` refusals. Entitlement is 30 days from redemption, compiled in (A19); every limit is a compiled constant (A13). No leave, delete or export (ADR-008 accepted "no export" for the class). A hash-chained audit log exists but no admin sees it. No SSO or SCIM.

**Gap.** Identity is Slack-shaped without Slack's two conveniences. Limits and entitlement are constants. Members cannot leave or export. The rest is a tier question.

**Recommendation.** Keep per-tenant identity through the tiers work: it is what fAuth gives, it keeps isolation absolute, and Slack shows it carries a large product. Build the two conveniences after the class. Make limits and entitlement plan data (A13, A19) as the first tiers step. Add self-serve "Leave workspace" and per-member export after the class, because the notice already promises copy-out. Path slugs (`/acme/canvas`, APP-117 step 3) at public launch; subdomains only for enterprise. Owner role, admin-visible audit log, domain auto-join and SSO/SCIM belong with the Team and Business tiers.

## 4. Invites

**Convergent pattern.** Email invites everywhere, expiring (GitHub and Vercel 7 days, Slack 30 days) with a pending list and cancellation ([GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/organizations/managing-membership-in-your-organization/inviting-users-to-join-your-organization), [Vercel](https://vercel.com/docs/rbac/managing-team-members), [Slack](https://slack.com/help/articles/360060363633-Manage-pending-invitations-and-invite-links-for-your-workspace)). Reusable links and domain auto-join in the collaboration tools (Slack links serve up to 400 people for 30 days; Linear and Figma links are reset rather than expired); no links at Vercel or GitHub. Who may invite: owners only at Vercel and GitHub, admins by default with a setting to open it at Linear and Slack ([Linear](https://linear.app/docs/invite-members)). Guests are scoped to an item (Notion pages, Figma files, Slack channels, GitHub repos) or to a team (Linear), and are shown apart from members. Anti-abuse numbers are published only by GitHub (50 invitations per 24 hours, 500 for paid or month-old orgs) and Vercel (100 pending access requests); Slack says it throttles without a number.

**Forma today.** Operator-issued codes with a digest, expiry, maximum redemptions (one by default), revocation and audit; per-address limits of 5 a minute and 100 a day on `/invite`, 2,000 a day globally; the provider invitation is server-only. Victor delivers codes by email. Admins cannot invite (APP-165 backlog). A code works only after the next restart, and a restart signs everyone out (POST-CLASS-FOLLOW-UPS). Guests get named, view-only shares that expire in 1 to 30 days (ADR-014), listed in the share dialog, not in `/admin`.

**Gap.** No admin invites or pending list; a lost code during the class costs a restart (inferred from the restart rule).

**Recommendation.** Before the class: three to five spare codes in the Nov 28 batch, with expiry past the class, so a lost or late code never needs a restart that weekend. After the class, APP-165 as admin-issued email invites with a 7-day expiry, a pending list with cancel, and admins-only by default; codes stay the operator's tool for new organizations. Links and domain auto-join only with the Team tier, after domain verification. Show guests in `/admin` as their own list.

## 5. The pages outside the app

**Convergent pattern.** All seven products checked (the six plus Supabase) publish terms, privacy, a DPA with a sub-processor list, a security page, `/.well-known/security.txt`, a status page, a changelog, pricing and a support entry. Status pages are outsourced everywhere but Slack (Atlassian Statuspage for Figma, Vercel, GitHub; incident.io for Notion, Linear, Supabase). `security.txt` points at HackerOne or a mailbox; Slack and Supabase omit the `Expires` field [RFC 9116](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9116) requires. SOC 2 and ISO reports sit behind a trust center and arrive with enterprise deals. Cookies diverge: Slack and Figma run a consent manager, GitHub a region-gated banner; Linear, Vercel and Supabase describe cookies in the policy with no banner ([Linear](https://linear.app/security), [Vercel](https://vercel.com/legal/cookie-policy), [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-cookies)).

**Forma today.** None of these exist on either domain (all 404 on 2026-11-15). The beta notice v1, consented to on `/invite`, is the only agreement; `feedback@getforma.dev` is the only contact and it lives inside the notice. A status page is planned "at scale" (APP-220); alarms reach Victor by email, and APP-218 adds three-region probes. The only cookie is the `__Host-` session cookie.

**Gap.** Everything, but for 25 consenting students the notice covers what terms and privacy would. The pre-class gaps are a visible contact and a re-readable notice.

**Recommendation.** Before the class: the footer release carries the contact line and the notice link; `security.txt` on `getforma.dev` (a static file, no beta release) with a `mailto:` contact and an `Expires` a year out, on beta only if the footer release is still open. After the class: counsel-reviewed terms and privacy under `getforma.dev/legal/`, stating no non-essential cookies and no banner, and a hosted status page at `status.getforma.dev` fed by APP-218's probes. At public launch: a security page in plain words (tenant isolation, receipts, AI output as data, no training on customer data), the DPA and sub-processor list (AWS, Anthropic), a changelog and pricing.

## Open questions for Victor

1. **Brand architecture.** Should Canvas get `getforma.dev/canvas` as its marketing home after the class, and should the app move to `app.getforma.dev` at public launch? Recommended: yes to both. Every framework-plus-product peer puts the product on a section of the company site and the app on a path or a product subdomain; `canvas.getforma.dev` is only for the day Canvas outgrows Forma Stack, as Next.js did.
2. **Identity.** Keep one fAuth tenant per organization (Slack's and GitHub EMU's model) or move to one Forma identity across workspaces (Notion, Linear, Vercel, GitHub)? Recommended: keep per-tenant through the tiers work and build Slack's two conveniences (find by email, switcher); revisit at public launch when guests in several organizations multiply.
3. **Workspace address.** Path (`/acme/canvas`) or subdomain (`acme.getforma.dev`)? Recommended: path, as at Linear, Notion, Vercel and GitHub; subdomains as an enterprise option per APP-117 step 4.
4. **Who may invite after the class.** Admins only, with an organization setting to allow members (Linear, Slack), or owners only (Vercel, GitHub)? Recommended: admins only by default, the member setting with the Team tier, no reusable links until domain verification exists.
5. **Legal stance before launch.** Publish placeholder terms and privacy now, or let the beta notice stand as the only agreement until counsel reviews real pages after the class? Recommended: the notice stands; no placeholders. Publish real terms and privacy before any non-consenting user, with "no non-essential cookies, no banner" stated plainly.
