## v1 (current)

Built from commit e279953, tag `task/forma-generative-app-platform-v2/v1`. Hand over `pdf/design-investor.pdf`.

- `pdf/design-investor.pdf`: the submission: the investor edition, 18 pages. For the audience named at intake.
- `docx/design-investor.docx`: the investor edition as Word, for editing and handing in. For the author and the audience.
- `html/design-investor.html`: the investor edition as a web page. For the audience, in a browser.
- `md/design-investor.md`: the investor edition as Markdown, the source. For the audience, when Markdown is accepted.
- `pdf/design.pdf`: the record: the full design document, 66 pages. For the head, reviewers, and anyone who wants the detail.
- `docx/design.docx`: the record as Word. For reviewers who edit.
- `html/design.html`: the record as a web page. For reviewers, in a browser.
- `md/design.md`: the record as Markdown, the source. For reviewers who prefer source.
- `notes.md`: presenter notes. For the author, before presenting.
- `claims.md`: the vendor-claims ledger: every claim about a product the design does not own, checked against the vendor's documentation, with what the lead did. For the head, and anyone who asks where a product figure comes from.
- `xref.md`: the reference cross-check: the finished document checked against the research it was built from, one fresh agent per slice, and the lead's triage of every finding. For the head, and anyone who asks whether the document follows its own rules.
- `reception.md`: the reception check: the recipient's read of the submission, by a fresh agent in their role, and the lead's triage of every question and objection. For the head, before handing over; never the recipient.
- `html/design-annotated.html`: the annotated edition: the record with method notes, design notes, a glossary, exercises, flashcards and reading, as one web page. For learners, and anyone being taught the design.
- `md/annotations.md`: the annotated edition's design layer, the source. For the author.
- `md/flashcards.tsv`: the flashcards as tab-separated text (front, back, deck), importable into Anki. For learners.
