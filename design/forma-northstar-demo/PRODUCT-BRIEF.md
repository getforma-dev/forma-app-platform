# A company builds its apps with Forma

## What the demonstration establishes

Forma lets a company create, transform, run and share apps around its data and work. Northstar makes that concrete through an outbound investigation. Spend Review then demonstrates a different purpose and interaction: a queue, a detail view and a persisted decision form. Both use the same authoring, admission, publication, identity, query, record and saved-work capabilities.

The demo should make three things visible: the company platform surrounding the apps; useful generation and precise adaptation within supported capabilities; and work that survives publication, handoff and reuse. It is not a pitch for a standalone security tool, and it does not claim arbitrary software generation.

This recommendation follows the supplied App Platform definition, especially pages 17–21. The earlier definition and security packet supply useful Northstar detail; their security-first launch assumption no longer controls the product.

## People, spaces and responsibility

Northstar is a synthetic organization with Security and Finance spaces. Maya builds the investigation app and is explicitly assigned the additional permissions needed to create the Finance example. Sarah demonstrates independent publication review under Northstar's configured policy. Jordan can open permitted shared work and start an independent case. A separate organization and principal provide an isolation test; an operator account provisions memberships, policies and fixtures.

These are responsibilities, not a requirement that every customer employ three separate people. The platform supports policy-governed publication, including authorized owner publication for an appropriate low-risk scope. The Northstar demonstration uses independent review to make changes and their approval visible. Authors cannot change publication policy themselves. App publication, an internal review decision and an external financial approval are different operations.

## Six acts

### 1. Enter the company workspace

Begin at Forma's company home, with permitted spaces, app owners, published versions and recent work. Clicking Security and entering `/security` resolve to the same stable space. App creation and work resumption are visible choices. Unknown commands offer understandable navigation feedback; they do not create resources or execute actions.

Show the platform before the investigation. A company logo or space theme can personalize it, but neither changes authority. Search and recents must exclude inaccessible names and previews as well as inaccessible data.

### 2. Bring evidence and describe the job

Maya imports connection events and a destination inventory, confirms timestamp units, the half-open time window, byte meaning and matching rules, and describes the investigation for `build-worker-17`. The importer reports accepted and rejected rows and pins source revisions. Use CSV for events and a small tabular XLSX inventory to exercise both input paths.

The first question is what occurred and how the observed destinations relate to the supplied inventory. A large transfer or missing inventory entry does not establish compromise. There is no anomaly claim without a sufficient baseline and a defined comparison. Imported evidence stays within its authorized context; upload does not publish it to the organization.

### 3. Form and transform a useful app

Retrieve compatible permitted components and compose six linked views: case summary, activity timeline, destination breakdown, inventory comparison, evidence table, and findings/handoff. Label retrieval, adaptation and generation accurately. Inspect the defined calculations and provenance.

Make a meaningful change: add a transfer-rate measure with an explicit whole-window denominator and a source-linked observation form. Preview the proposal, inspect its semantic difference, accept it into a new draft revision, undo it and reapply it. Layout and existing work remain stable where the patch does not affect them. Partial model output never becomes executable behavior.

Use synthetic preview data by default. If authorized real-data read preview is enabled, it operates on a validated immutable draft under the viewer's current access. All draft record writes go to an isolated preview collection. Publication does not promote those trial entries into live work.

### 4. Publish and run

Sarah reviews the exact draft and its declared inputs, calculations, schemas and commands. Publication applies Northstar's current policy and binds that exact content. A change after approval invalidates the approval. Publishing a reusable app does not publish Maya's case, source credentials or private notes.

Maya starts live saved work from the published version, binds her permitted source revisions, filters the views and saves an observation with evidence references and stated uncertainty. A reload restores the authorized work and personal view state. Published work continues to operate while a subsequent draft or generation attempt fails. Generation is not required for ordinary navigation, deterministic queries or internal form submission.

### 5. Share the work and reuse the tool

First share the saved investigation with Jordan under current work and source permissions. Jordan can understand its context, observations and remaining questions. If granted write permission, Jordan can continue the same work; concurrent edits produce visible conflicts instead of silent overwrites.

Then choose **Start new work** using the published app. Jordan supplies evidence for `ops-runner-04`. This creates a different work identity and new bindings and records. It does not inherit Maya's evidence, findings, personal filters or credentials. The app can be reused without regenerating its whole interface.

Keep **share app**, **share saved work** and **export** separate. A shared link conveys identity, not bearer authority. Revoking a source or work permission blocks subsequent access to derived findings as well as raw rows; previously delivered information cannot be recalled.

### 6. Create a different app

Return home and enter Finance by click or `/finance`. Starting through the same supported creation interface, Maya imports a small spending table and asks for a review queue, a linked detail view and a decision form. Declare integer currency units and a threshold; add typed status and note fields. Build this definition during the demonstration or reproducible rehearsal, without a per-example source change or deployment.

Preview, publish under the configured policy, start saved work and submit an internal decision. Show the saved record after refresh, a duplicate-safe retry and an understandable stale-edit conflict. This records the team's review; it neither changes the imported file nor pays a vendor. Return to Security and recover the earlier case context without leaking Finance results into it.

## Boundaries and pitch evidence

Use one managed browser deployment and versioned trusted Forma components. The shared contract proposes a typed app definition, bounded queries and declared internal commands. The factory must decide this representation against the historical markup-based approach before either implementation diverges. Domain meaning belongs in app definitions and registered operators, not tenant-name branches.

Northstar needs a supported inventory lookup, not a SIEM or autonomous responder. Spend Review needs a durable form, not an accounting engine. Live connectors, MCP Apps, external actions, scheduled workflows, simultaneous coediting, a marketplace and public websites are later extensions. They are not necessary for this proof.

All data is synthetic and labeled. Every simulated behavior must be identified as such and excluded from working-capability acceptance. Record the platform build, definition digests, query receipts, record outcomes and rehearsal conditions. Measure the full process, including import cleanup and review; do not present generated pixels, fictional users or elapsed model streaming time as customer traction or an approved working app.
