# Categories

Categories describe ownership; sprints describe execution order. A ticket lives once, in its status folder.

| Category | Responsibility | Tickets |
|---|---|---|
| [FND: Foundation](categories/FND.md) | Released framework qualification, typed contracts and reproducible application structure. | 9 |
| [IAM: Identity and access](categories/IAM.md) | Authenticated principals, tenant boundaries and service authority. | 12 |
| [ING: Evidence and imports](categories/ING.md) | Bounded CSV/XLSX ingestion, interpretation and immutable evidence. | 5 |
| [QRY: Queries and provenance](categories/QRY.md) | Deterministic operations, exact semantics, receipts and resource bounds. | 9 |
| [REC: Internal records](categories/REC.md) | Declared mutations, durable outcomes, conflict recovery and concurrency. | 3 |
| [UX: Workspace and accessibility](categories/UX.md) | Native presentation, canvas and equivalent keyboard/focused workflows. | 12 |
| [AUT: App authoring](categories/AUT.md) | Typed definitions, direct composition, patches and isolated preview. | 4 |
| [GOV: Publication and lifecycle](categories/GOV.md) | Policy, exact-content publication, pinned work and maintenance. | 6 |
| [AI: AI and retrieval](categories/AI.md) | Authorized discovery, bounded generation, explanation and evaluation. | 12 |
| [SHR: Sharing and handoff](categories/SHR.md) | Separate grants, derived information, revocation and exports. | 5 |
| [VAL: Validation and demonstrations](categories/VAL.md) | Synthetic fixtures, both app journeys and reproducible release evidence. | 14 |
| [OPS: Operations and security](categories/OPS.md) | Jobs, release controls, recovery, retention, support and pilot operations. | 16 |
