## Why

The [ADR-009 re-review](../../evidence/APP-015/adr-009-review.md) found no blocker but one major (R-M1: lease reclaim stalls the queue under the unique running index) and six minors in the revised text, and the [contract-edition review](../../evidence/APP-015/contract-edition/independent-review.md) kept several decisions "documented here" with open vocabulary. [ADR-010](../../docs/architecture/ADR-010-NOV-2-MVP-CUT.md) defers the doc work past the 2026-11-02 MVP; it must be in ADR-009 before APP-015's migration is written.

## What changes

1. **R-M1:** replace the `claim_next_job` behaviour: a `running` row is the only candidate (unexpired → nothing; expired → reclaim under `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`); only when none exists pick the oldest-cursor organization's queued row; add the two-organization crash/reclaim case to APP-015's tests.
2. **R-m1:** `USAGE ON SCHEMA genui, genui_private` and `SELECT(kind)` for `genui_jobs`; the fence grant to `genui_worker` issued as `genui_lock`; add the tuples to the B10 catalog assertion.
3. **R-m2:** label the remaining amendments (D7.14 upload deadline as B19; `parser-host` versus design D11/§6.4 as B20; D7.8's canonical-bytes rule in B12; the 0002:85 CHECK in B13 or B10; ADR-004:20–21 in B9), mark B8/B12 at their points of change, state the fixture-organization exemption from the 90-day lifecycle.
4. **R-m3:** a refused authority recheck ends the job `failed` with a named code and one `job.failed` event and releases the queue row in the same transaction; `WorkerScope` re-reads the epoch and retakes the fence once on a fence miss with an active membership.
5. **R-m4:** define `queue_position` (organization-local estimate, or `enqueue_job` returns it plus a read-only `job_queue_position`).
6. **R-m5:** job read authorization (principal or `source_read` holder; otherwise the uniform 404).
7. **R-m6:** classify 23505 and 55P03 as "nothing claimed"; write "one job running per deployment (one task under D7.18)" for APP-061.
8. **Contract edition:** record items 5, 6, 7 and 11 (derivable identity lists; `limit_exceeded` and the 429 scope; array framing, row grammar and unknown values to APP-016/018; the open `failure_code` vocabulary) as decisions in the contracts README or the next edition, and run the Docker-backed native campaign that uses the seed on the edition-two tree (the review's unrun limitation).
