## What changes

Demonstrate selected-sheet literal values and a hostile workbook under compressed/expanded/entries/cells/time/memory caps with no file/network/credential interface; record library/license choice.

Keep this a reviewable slice: M means approximately 1–3 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

- [ ] Parse a bounded CSV and multi-sheet XLSX into literal typed values with source coordinates while the WASM module has no network, file, environment or credentials interface.
