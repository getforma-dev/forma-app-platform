## Why

Apps are whole products made of components. The Library and canvas should make that identity clear and prevent overlapping owners of the same DOM components.

## What changes

The separate `/v1/cards` artifacts already have durable card identity and are listed by APP-168. This ticket covers whole saved works: persist a deliberate artifact kind for independently created Cards versus Apps, preserving whole-work identity and authorization. Define migration/backfill for existing ambiguous works with owner review; never guess from number of components. Replace the temporary member-local Library classification only after cross-device behavior is qualified. Audit historical card placement/stack APIs and saved layouts, then separately remove obsolete frontend/backend components after compatibility evidence. The current redesign is a soft frontend removal, not deletion of backend components.

*Split 2026-11-05:* the migration/backfill of existing ambiguous works (owner review) and the retirement of legacy placement moved to [APP-176](../0-backlog/APP-176.md), with their two acceptance tests. This ticket keeps the durable per-member kind.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Admitted durable kind survives new device/login while unauthorized work remains undiscoverable.
- [ ] Each change of kind is one `work.kind_set` workspace-chain event, the route is admitted like the other member writes, and its authorization negatives are tested at route and storage level.
- [ ] An independent review of the final head finds no blocker.

## Verification

Record focused tests, browser evidence and independent review before completion. Planning checks alone do not prove behavior.

## Migration and rollback

Migration 0022 creates `genui.work_artifact_kinds` (ENABLE+FORCE RLS, `tenant_scope` on the organization and a RESTRICTIVE principal policy for `genui_workspace`) and adds `work.kind_set` to the workspace chain's kinds. It rewrites no stored value; absence still means App. It is stacked on APP-084's 0021 and ships with it in one reviewed operator schema release ([APP-175](../0-backlog/APP-175.md)): `pool.rs` requires exactly version 22, so beta auto-promotion refuses this change. Rollback is forward-fix; a snapshot restore with the previous image is a separately reviewed recovery. Browser-local view metadata stays bounded and holds identifiers only.
