## Why

A principal, tenant or service-scope mistake can expose another organization’s work; this boundary must be proven before dependent integration. This ticket delivers: revalidate fauth contract and integration prerequisites.

## What changes

Confirm published/current deployment identity, registered client/PKCE/JWKS/tenant claims, key custody and failure behavior; retain adapter replacement seam; link rather than duplicate FORMA-247/248 follow-ups. [ADR-003](../../docs/architecture/ADR-003-PUBLIC-BETA-ACCESS.md) adds invite-first registration at the beta origin: public visitors cannot create an account without a valid invitation. FormaStack owns tenant-scoped signup, real email-verification delivery, credential/recovery policy and a token-free code-flow handoff. GenUI owns code admission, membership and entitlement in APP-073/009/074. The exact origin/callback are `https://beta.getforma.dev` and `https://beta.getforma.dev/auth/callback`; registration is not covered by the prior login-only source campaign.

[ADR-008](../../docs/architecture/ADR-008-YC-DEMO-AND-BETA-MVP.md) (2026-10-29): Victor deferred the hosted identity ceremony; this ticket now delivers the local consumer qualification of the pinned contract and the hash-bound merged provider contract (gatewasm PR18/19 at `dfba787`, PR20 at `1bf5d3b`), and its hosted items are APP-079's.
