## Why

Internal decisions must survive retries and restarts without duplicate mutations, silent overwrite or stale authority. This ticket delivers: prove command retries, conflicts and authorization race ordering.

## What changes

Deliver the dedicated follow-on scope below, retaining the established typed interfaces and current authorization. Lose the response after commit and retry the same scoped key/payload with now-stale expected version: after current authorization, return the original outcome with one record revision and mutation audit. Race same-key and different-key edits; one intended write commits, changed-payload reuse and stale distinct edits conflict with authorized recovery, and other principals cannot retrieve the key. Pause a new command and race it with membership revocation or work-version adoption in both orders; authoritative change first prevents stale commit, earlier authorized commit remains recorded and current visibility governs response delivery.

Keep this a reviewable slice: M means approximately 1–3 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes production deployment and real customer data; this is a synthetic qualification. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.
