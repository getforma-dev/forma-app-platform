# PH08: PIVOT: November 2 pitch demo

Team: GenUI. Progress: **active**. Exit gate: **pending**.

Predecessor phases: None — start here.

## Outcome

On the live beta, a signed-in member describes an app, sees it on a canvas of labeled retrieved/adapted/generated components, runs it on governed data with receipts, shares it with a teammate under the teammate's own permissions, and a non-member cannot see it; then the November 3 revisit decides what lands in the platform.

## Entry

ADR-011 (2026-10-30) is accepted. The pitch is built in the clone FormaBuiltApps/forma-pitch-mvp on branch pivot/pitch-mvp (from phase-one-spikes 21199f4); the platform's own planned track and the live beta's evidence stay intact.

## Deliverables

- The pitch clone and the integration architecture of the GenUI runtime canvas, the intent planner with module index and the Claude-composed modules on the platform backbone (APP-091).
- Canvas island in the signed-in workspace, composer, governed data with receipts, and sharing under policy (APP-092–095).
- Deployment and rehearsal on beta.getforma.dev through a reviewed change set (APP-096).
- The November 3 revisit that lands the pitch ticket by ticket and closes every deferred item (APP-097).
