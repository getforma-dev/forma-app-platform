# PH08: PIVOT: November 2 pitch demo

Team: GenUI. Progress: **active**. Exit gate: **pending**.

Predecessor phases: None — start here.

## Outcome

On the live beta, a signed-in member describes an app, sees it on a canvas of labeled retrieved/adapted/generated components, runs it on governed data with receipts, shares it with a teammate under the teammate's own permissions, and a non-member cannot see it; then the November 3 revisit decides what lands in the platform.
