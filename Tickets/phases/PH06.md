# PH06: Qualification and pilot learning

Team: GenUI. Progress: **planned**. Exit gate: **pending**.

Predecessor phases: [PH05](PH05.md)

## Outcome

Measured operational evidence supports a controlled pilot, followed by observed customer use and independent reuse.

## Entry

PH05 controls and scope are ready. First qualify the release candidate with APP-067–071; then decide live-data entry at APP-072 entry; complete this phase only after recurring-use learning.

## Deliverables

- Payload-safe telemetry, SLO/cost signals and actual restore/failure drills (APP-067/068).
- Open-loop load/noisy-neighbor/budget qualification and independent security/accessibility review (APP-069/070).
- Exercised operations/release/rollback runbooks and named support ownership (APP-071).
- Final live-data entry record, then recurring customer workflow/second-person reuse evidence (APP-072).
- Deferred hosted identity acceptance: three-browser callback matrix, deployed negatives, rotation campaign and canary resource check on the live deployment (APP-079; ADR-008).

## Exit gate

All phase tickets must be Done, predecessor phase gates must pass, and the following evidence must be accepted. Ticket completion alone does not complete the phase. Follow the [quality gates](../../docs/planning/QUALITY-GATES.md) for checks, release trials and evidence standards.

- [ ] An isolated restore verifies relationships, audit and deletion-ledger replay; measured RPO/RTO meet adopted design targets. Process/role/zone/provider/key-store failures fail safely (APP-068).
- [ ] Cold/warm million-row, six-view peak, hot-record and 10x/noisy-neighbor trials report p50/p95/p99, errors, admitted/rejected load, bounded resources and fair quotas against explicit adopted limits (APP-067/069).
- [ ] Independent release review covers SR1–SR13, actual provider disclosure, revocation, service isolation, audit, deletion after restore and WCAG/keyboard/screen-reader evidence. Blockers are fixed and affected checks rerun (APP-070).
- [ ] Before the first real organization sign-in or live row, record readiness using APP-058 scope/identity, APP-079 hosted identity acceptance, APP-070 qualification and APP-071 operational evidence. This is the entry checkpoint for APP-072, not a requirement to finish customer learning before it starts.
- [ ] Then complete APP-072 recurring-use and second-person reuse observations with baseline effort, corrections, sample size and limits. No demo-only adoption claim. Archive the phase evidence after this learning completes.
