## Why

After the November 2 trial, the operator task's execution role can still read FormaStack's Northstar management key (`fauth-beta/management/northstar`). "GenUI never handles the raw key" was a process boundary under one IAM user, not an IAM boundary (A12). The invitation input secret's older versions can also still hold addresses: a `PutSecretValue {}` only moves the prior version to `AWSPREVIOUS`, and unlabeled versions stay fetchable by version id until Secrets Manager deletes them.

## What changes

1. **Detach, then rotate.** A reviewed stack update clears `ProviderManagementSecretArn` and `ProviderManagementUrl`. That removes the operator task's injection and its execution role's `GetSecretValue` grant on the Northstar secret. Only after GenUI confirms the update completed does FormaStack rotate the Northstar key (rotation runbook `/Users/victorvillacis/dev/Forma/work/FORMA-367/ROTATE-NORTHSTAR-MANAGEMENT-KEY.md`, FormaStack's action).
2. **Invitation input versions.** Remove the address-bearing versions of `OperatorInvitationInput`, either by deleting and recreating the secret or by an audited bound on deprecated versions, and record which.
3. **Rotation and audit of GenUI's three secrets per environment.** The input secret joins the capability store's rotation and audit. The operator's `list` shows each redemption's principal and entitlement ids (APP-076 G5); the listing itself is not audited.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] The stack update completed and its readback shows no management-secret injection and no `GetSecretValue` grant on the Northstar secret for any GenUI role.
