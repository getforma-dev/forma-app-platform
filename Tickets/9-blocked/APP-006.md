## Why

A principal, tenant or service-scope mistake can expose another organization’s work; this boundary must be proven before dependent integration. This ticket delivers: revalidate fauth contract and integration prerequisites.

## What changes

Confirm published/current deployment identity, registered client/PKCE/JWKS/tenant claims, key custody and failure behavior; retain adapter replacement seam; link rather than duplicate FORMA-247/248 follow-ups. [ADR-003](../../docs/architecture/ADR-003-PUBLIC-BETA-ACCESS.md) adds invite-first registration at the beta origin: public visitors cannot create an account without a valid invitation. FormaStack owns tenant-scoped signup, real email-verification delivery, credential/recovery policy and a token-free code-flow handoff. GenUI owns code admission, membership and entitlement in APP-073/009/074. The exact origin/callback are `https://beta.getforma.dev` and `https://beta.getforma.dev/auth/callback`; registration is not covered by the prior login-only source campaign.

Keep this a reviewable slice: M means approximately 1–3 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes production deployment and real customer data; this is a synthetic qualification. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Attach FormaStack-owned FORMA-247 evidence for the deployed login service, environment client registration, tenant branding and redirect/PKCE behavior at a pinned revision.
- [ ] Exercise issuer/audience/signature/expiry/kid/tenant claim verification and key refresh failure against the intended adapter contract; an unknown or invalid principal is refused.
- [ ] Record the deploy identity, bounded key-refresh behavior and live-service rollback/fallback boundary without bypassing authenticated membership or introducing a fake organization picker.

- [ ] Attach the pinned FormaStack hosted invite-only signup contract: absent/invalid/expired/foreign invitations cannot create accounts through direct endpoints; valid signup binds the server-selected tenant and intended admission context. Verify actual email-verification delivery, mailbox activation and recovery policy; controlled synthetic inboxes are permitted but fabricated delivery, bypassed verification and invented JWT claims are not.
- [ ] Qualify exact beta origin/callback and token-free signup/login code handoff; wrong tenant, wildcard/changed redirect, replay and provider signup-limit failures refuse. Record signup/login/IP/account/global/pending-account limits and the GenUI pre-access integration seam; no invitation grants workspace authority.
