## Why

Victor's disposition of 2026-10-29 ([record](../../evidence/APP-006/founder-disposition-2026-10-29.md)) deferred the hosted identity ceremony so the demo and beta could proceed on reviewed source. Before any real organization signs in, the deferred proof must exist. This ticket carries APP-006's five hosted acceptance items verbatim, APP-074's items 1 and 8 verbatim for native Safari and Firefox (their installed-Chrome text is edited in APP-076 items 3 and 4), and three items new by ADR-008 that carry no "Moved" marker.

## What changes

Run, against the live fAuth and GenUI deployments: existing-member login in installed Chrome, native Safari and Firefox through the real callback; one fresh invited signup; the deployed negative matrix (wrong verifier, client, redirect, cross-tenant token, replay, 60-second code expiry); the key qualifier's unknown-kid campaign; the Canary-versus-Northstar application-resource refusal; and receipt of FormaStack's custody/rotation evidence.
