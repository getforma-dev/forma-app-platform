## Why

On 2026-10-30 Victor asked to "add the ability to stack cards and shuffle through them" (APP-104). The ticket modeled them on macOS Stacks, BumpTop's piles and Fences. Stacks were built in APP-104. Fences were not. APP-104 recorded them as an open box: "Fences (labeled regions cards drop into and move with, roll-up by double-click): not built; `/arrange` covers 'arrange by app' as rows." Since then ADR-018 made each app one canvas object. Whether a fence still adds anything beyond the whole-app App Card, Stack and Open views is an open product question.

## What changes

1. Decide with Victor whether fences are still wanted under ADR-018, and record the decision in `docs/planning` or an ADR amendment.
2. If kept: a fence is a labeled region of the canvas view layout. Whole apps and widgets dropped into it move with it, and a double-click rolls it up. It is presentation only, stored like the rest of the view layout, and changes no grant, query, record or receipt.
3. If dropped: close this ticket with the decision and no code.
