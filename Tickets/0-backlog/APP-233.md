## Why

On 2026-11-15, from about 15:09 UTC, when the Route 53 uptime probes of `forma-beta-observability` started asking `GET /health/ready`, the alarm `forma-beta-trial-alarms-target-5xx` flapped between ALARM and OK 64 times in 12 hours, and `forma-beta-observability-server-errors` 12 times. Every counted 5xx was a `503` on `GET /health/ready` (304 of 304 `http.server_error` events from 21:00 to 03:00 UTC). They arrived in bursts of one to seven, at most 15 s long, exactly 4.0 minutes apart. Users were unaffected.

The cause:
- The runtime supervisor refreshes the database credentials every 240 s (`app/runtime/runtime.py`, the `range(240 if child else 5)` loop), and each refresh writes a new generation.
- `Readiness::ready()` (`app/src/readiness.rs`) accepts its cached success only for the current generation and file digest.
- The background check runs every 15 s. Between a refresh and the next check, readiness answers 503 even though the new credentials work.

The balancer's own health checks are not counted in `HTTPCode_Target_5XX_Count`, and with an interval of 30 s and an unhealthy threshold of 3 they never fail three times inside 15 s. So nothing alarmed until the probes, which are counted as real requests, arrived.

## What changes

- Readiness re-checks as soon as the configuration file's generation changes. One way: `ready()` sees a generation mismatch and wakes the background task, or the task watches the file's digest at a short interval. The 503 window then shrinks to the probe's own duration. Alternatively, a success for the previous generation stays valid for a short grace period while the new generation's check is pending, provided that period stays within that generation's `expires_at`. Choose one and record why.
- `/health/ready`'s 503 is a readiness state, not a server fault. Log it at a level and under an event other than `http.server_error`, or not at all. The filter exclusion of 2026-11-16 can then be removed.
- Afterwards the uptime probe can go back to `/health/ready` if that still adds value over `/health/live` behind the balancer; decide in the runbook §11.8 note.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] A test refreshes the configuration (new generation, same working stores) and shows `ready()` stays true, or false for no longer than one probe, without waiting for the 15-s tick.
- [ ] A test shows a refresh to a generation whose probe fails still turns readiness false (the generation binding is not weakened).
- [ ] After the release: `HTTPCode_Target_5XX_Count` shows no 4-minute bursts over one hour with the uptime probe on `/health/ready` (or the probe stays on `/health/live`, recorded).

## Verification

`cargo test -p forma-app-platform --lib readiness`, then on beta `aws logs filter-log-events` with `{ $.event = "http.server_error" }` over one hour after the release.

## Migration and rollback

No migration. The code change is reverted like any app change. The monitoring change of 2026-11-16 is reverted by setting `AppHealthCheck`'s `ResourcePath` back to `/health/ready` and the `ServerErrorFilter` pattern back to `{ $.event = "http.server_error" }` in `ops/monitoring/observability.json`, then updating the stack.
