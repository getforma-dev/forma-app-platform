## Why

The PH01 exit gate requires tenant isolation and authoritative identity checks on the actual managed path; local substitutes are explicitly insufficient. This ticket carries [ADR-004](../../docs/architecture/ADR-004-TENANT-TRANSACTIONS.md) slice 3's managed obligations verbatim by reference to the [managed plan](../../evidence/APP-008/managed-plan.md) §Ordered managed run and §Required matrix, moved from APP-008 by [ADR-005](../../docs/architecture/ADR-005-PHASE-1-SEQUENCING.md).

## What changes

Inside one Victor-approved reserved window outside any auth rehearsal, with the service stopped and the consumer observed stopped: publish the new immutable production image from the final reviewed source, update the stack through a reviewed TemplateURL change set (new `ImageDigest`, new future stop schedule, optional task definitions, stack policy read back), observe and reserve, compare the live catalog and replication mode before any mutation, take and restore the pre-migration snapshot, apply every landed migration once and once again, seed the two fixture organizations, run the full matrix through the qualification task and the operator task, qualify the production HTTPS surface from the exact managed image, stop every task, clean up temporary resources and reconcile the ledger.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Pre-window decisions are recorded and reviewed: ledger basis reconciled, window UUID reserved with `extra_task_hours` at least 2, TemplateURL destination and change-set files reviewed, the ten-minute campaign cap reviewed against image startup and reserved task-hours, and the drift-trial target chosen (a temporary restored instance with reviewed `rds-db:connect` bindings, or a recorded limitation). If a required managed administrator operation is unavailable, record that limitation and obtain an equivalent reviewed real-RDS trial; do not silently replace it with a local result.
- [ ] Before any mutation, the live foundation manifest, replication mode and canonical catalog are compared read-only against the reviewed logical baseline under the approved creator-grant normalization; any unexpected difference stops for review and is never normalized automatically. The pre-migration snapshot is created, restored to an exact temporary private identifier and compared before any restored migration or reseed.
- [ ] Every landed migration applies once from the exact candidate image and once again unchanged, adding exactly one history row per migration; the two organizations are seeded with validated envelopes and exact-match idempotence; four actual IAM-token connections bind exactly to their roles with exact PostgreSQL users, flags, memberships and origin mode; wrong user/token, expired material, wrong hostname/CA and plaintext refuse for the intended reason; the schema 1 image/source pairing is incompatible with the new version's readiness.
- [ ] Two organizations and grants on RDS: seeded publication/record positives, same-local-ID and Finance-only canary negatives, missing/malformed scope and wrong parent IDs, cross-tenant composite FKs, all granted relations under all four roles, no direct workspace publication/record DML or privileged `SET ROLE`, worker's limited organization read, and denial for unknown actions, revoked/old-epoch/missing grants, registry/DB mismatches, missing source visibility and tombstones.
- [ ] Pool lifecycle on RDS with private backend PIDs and public equality/difference assertions: same-backend reuse after success and error rollback across organizations with no prior scope or metadata and cleared role, settings, listeners and advisory locks; cancellation during connect, recycle, begin, query, finish and reset; ambiguous commit; cleanup timeout; credential generation and expiry; capacity and waiter limits; transport destruction; poisoned session context. Successful cleanup reuses; uncertain cleanup replaces the backend without business retry.
- [ ] Concurrency with real row locks and observed wait states: both orders of membership revocation, organization suspension and saved-work version change, holding organization before member then work; the application operation first completes only under its held fence; the writer first causes a current-state or stale-pin refusal after waiting; operator mutations advance epochs rather than resetting them.
- [ ] Drift and readiness: canonical drift, unsafe all-role defaults, replica mode and unauthorized parameter `SET` refuse; catalog mismatch precedes any unsafe metadata-view evaluation; clean state is restored and readiness recovers.
- [ ] Production HTTPS from the exact managed image over public `0.0.0.0/0` ingress: landing, liveness and readiness succeed and unauthenticated or nonexistent paths receive the uniform refusal with the same status, body and security headers and no provider or queue effect; ingress is never left limited to one operator IP. The private-resource refusal matrix over real routes is APP-076's (moved by ADR-005).
- [ ] Every task is observed STOPPED, temporary database/snapshot/task-definition cleanup is verified, the protected source identity is unchanged, the ledger is reconciled, and an independent review binds all receipts; a partial matrix or cleanup uncertainty keeps this ticket open.

## Verification

Execute the [managed plan](../../evidence/APP-008/managed-plan.md) steps 1–7 exactly, reusing `infra/scripts/preflight.py`, the stack policy and the retained APP-007 restore and review patterns; follow `infra/README.md` for activation and the window/ledger rules. Coordinate the window with the FormaStack owner so no auth consumer is running. Record commit, file hashes, image digests and architecture, task-definition revisions, role ARNs, UID/modes, RDS identifier/resource ID/engine, catalog/ledger/fixture hashes, registry identity and run UUID under `evidence/APP-078/`; private request files may hold endpoints, never credentials or raw environment.

Source obligations: C01, C11; design §§8, 10.5; SR1, SR3; ADR-004 request/operation states, fence ownership, pool lifecycle and migration/recovery.

## Migration and rollback

The pre-migration snapshot is schema 1; the known compatible service candidate is the qualified `8f8c34b` image. Switching the image back while the source remains at a later version is not rollback. After a failed transactional migration, prove the source stayed at the exact version 1 baseline; after a committed upgrade, use a reviewed forward fix or the snapshot restore with an explicitly compatible image and a separately reviewed cutover. Never widen IAM grants or bypass the RDS replacement/deletion guard.
