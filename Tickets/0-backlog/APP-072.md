At entry, record the final live-data readiness decision using APP-058 scope/identity, APP-070 qualification and APP-071 operator evidence. Then capture baseline and full preparation/create/review/use/handoff/reuse/maintenance effort, return without founder help, another app without platform edit, correction rate and willingness to pay; separate evidence from demo success.
