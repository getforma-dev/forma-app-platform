## Why

## What changes

Sandbox from APP-004; bound archive-wide work, literal/formula-cache policy, no macros/external links, stable CSV logical records/spans and XLSX coordinates, accepted/rejected counts; complete or fail visibly.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Actual CSV and selected-sheet XLSX files pass through the sandbox and produce accepted/rejected counts, typed rows and original row/cell provenance.

- [ ] Macros, links and unsupported formulas never execute; cancellation and parser crash leave no admitted partial revision and the worker can handle the next job.
