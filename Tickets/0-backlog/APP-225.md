## Why

Every Forma organization needs professional, platform-level roles: who runs it, who manages its members, who builds, who only looks. The platform knew one organization-level fact (`admin_policy`, written by the operator) and nothing else, so there was no read-only seat, no delegated roster management and no owner who cannot be locked out. [ADR-021](../../docs/architecture/ADR-021-ORGANIZATION-ROLES.md) records the decision; this ticket builds the roles, their management and the migration. [APP-226](APP-226.md) enforces the viewer ceiling on every write path.

## What changes

1. **Migration 0025** (`app/migrations/0025_organization_roles.sql`, schema 24 → 25, `catalog-v25.json`, every pin). `genui.memberships.role` text NOT NULL DEFAULT `member`, CHECK over the five values; the deterministic data migration (in each organization the earliest-admitted active `admin_policy` holder, by `code_redemptions.committed_at` NULLS LAST then the smallest principal id, becomes `owner`; every other active holder `admin`; everyone else, revoked included, `member`); `organization_permissions` dropped (it held nothing else); `access_codes.role` (`member` or `viewer`, default `member`) and `admit_redemption` admitting with it; `steward_card` naming an owner or admin; both audit chains admit `membership.role_changed`; the RESTRICTIVE `viewer_read_only` policies (APP-226).
2. **Definers.** `genui_private.set_member_role(p_org, p_caller, p_principal, p_role)`: both parties active members, the ADR-021 ladder, the last-owner rule, a no-op for the same role, otherwise the role, the target's epoch advanced and one `membership.role_changed` event with a recomputable digest (target, from, to). `admin_remove_member` under the same ladder, `last_owner` for `last_admin`, `not_allowed` added. `org_admin_overview` answers the role per member (recreated: `is_admin` became `role`) and is callable by owner, admin and manager. Refusal codes: `not_member`, `not_allowed`, `last_owner`, `self`, `unknown_role`.
3. **One capability function.** `storage::OrgRole::can(Capability)` with `Build`, `Read`, `Write`, `Share`, `Connect`, `Admin`; `manages`, `may_set`, `may_remove`, `assignable`. `TrustedRequest::role()` is resolved with the session, the Slack link and the agent token. `Action::AdminPolicy` is `Action::OrgAdmin` (the three administering roles on the member's own active row). `labels::OWNER_OR_ADMIN` names an owner or admin.
4. **`/admin`.** A Role column; per active row a select offering only the roles the caller may set (`OrgRole::assignable`), a Change role form with its own synchronizer token (`csrf::Form::OrgAdminRole`, fields `csrf`, `role`, `from`, `confirm`) and a confirmation box for owner and admin changes; the Rust ladder refuses a forged form before storage and the definer judges again; notices in plain words (`role_changed`, `role_unchanged`, `role_unconfirmed`, `role_not_allowed`, `role_last_owner`, `role_self`, `role_not_member`, `role_unknown`; Remove gains `not_allowed` and `last_owner`); the legend (Owner, Admin, Manager, Member, Viewer) and the helper line. Server-rendered, script-free. A member or viewer keeps the uniform 404.
5. **Operator.** `admission set-role --organization --principal --role` (audited on the operator chain, `last_owner` and `role_unsupported` mapped); `grant-admin` and `revoke-admin` kept as `ops/promotion/admission.py` aliases for `set-role admin` and `set-role member`; `issue --role member|viewer`; `list` shows each redeemer's `membership_role`; `app/operator/overrides.json` gains `admission-set-role` and `admission-issue-trial-viewer-invitation` in place of the two `admin_policy` overrides. The runbook's sections that named `grant-admin` are rewritten.
6. **Seed and qualifier.** `tenant_seed.py` writes the fixture owner's role instead of the permission row; `qualify_tenant_schema.py` expects the new definer, grants, chain kinds and the viewer policies.

## Migration and rollback

Migration 0025 (schema 25). Not additive: `organization_permissions` is dropped and `org_admin_overview` recreated (recorded in the migration header and ADR-021 §8). Rolling back means restoring the 24 snapshot (the runbook's reviewed recovery), since the dropped table's rows live on only as roles. The deployment contract digest changes (new migration, catalog, campaign file and allowlists), so promotion needs the schema release record.

  - `organization_permissions` is dropped in 0025: `admin_policy` was its only value, and a second source of admin standing would drift from the role.

  - The data migration lifts FORCE RLS on three tables for its one UPDATE (the migration role has no organization scope and no BYPASSRLS) and restores it; the catalog is unchanged by it.
