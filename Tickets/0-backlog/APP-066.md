## Why

The managed workflow must remain bounded, recoverable and auditable under real failure conditions before it handles customer data. This ticket delivers: build admin and operator workflows.

## What changes

Membership, create/edit/retire spaces, policy, aliases, ownership, app retirement and controlled deletion; quota/job/budget/restore views without customer payload, clear audit and permissions, manually provisioned account workflow documented.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes unapproved paid provisioning or real-data processing; completing preparation does not itself authorize either. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Authorized administrators can manage membership, create/edit/retire spaces, policy, aliases, ownership, app retirement and the existing confirmed deletion workflows with current policy checks and high-impact audit.
- [ ] Operator views expose job/quota/budget/restore health but no organization payload, while an ordinary member cannot access admin/support operations.
- [ ] Manual provisioning and administrative conflict/denial/recovery paths are documented and usable without direct database edits for ordinary workflow.

- [ ] Surface APP-073 code issuance/revocation and separate entitlement suspension/extension through the operator workflow without revealing stored code secrets. Initial isolated-organization invites and later member invites preserve destination/permission bounds; ordinary members cannot mint codes or elevate trial limits.
