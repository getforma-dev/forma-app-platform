## Why

Found while building APP-128 (its log, 2026-10-31): on the clean profile (no library, no sources, demo off) the sentence "Track my team's book club reading list …" routed to generation (`empty_library`); the model replied three times and admission refused every draft `not_runnable`. A new customer cannot build a first app from a sentence; only templates (`just dev clean seed-library`) or a folder with data get them in. Victor, 2026-10-31: "Fix it now", and "in the gap we built too much for MVP and hard coded lots of stuff" (the wider audit is APP-135).

## What changes

A sentence on a workspace with no apps and no data produces a runnable, admitted, governed **records app**: a `records` input whose rows the member enters through the app's create command, records/ledger views, receipts as for every governed record. Admission is not weakened: either generation is steered to the runnable shapes when no source exists, or a wrong admission rule is fixed with its design citation.

## Acceptance tests

- [x] On `just dev clean`, "track my book club's reading list" → a confirm → an app with a create form; adding a record shows it on the records card with a receipt.
- [x] Drafts pass the contract check for new content (`validate(..., new_content=True)`, edition impl-2026-10-31.4) and Rust admission.
- [ ] The demo profile's rehearsed sentences are unchanged.

## Verification

Unit tests for generation and admission; the pitch campaign; a browser walk on `just dev clean`.

## Migration and rollback
