## Why

The managed workflow must remain bounded, recoverable and auditable under real failure conditions before it handles customer data. This ticket delivers: exercise restore and failure drills.

## What changes

Isolated database/object/key restore with relationships/audit/digests and deletion ledger, measured RPO/RTO; process/role/zone failure, provider outage and key-store unavailability fail safely; restore is never fixture reseeding.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes unapproved paid provisioning or real-data processing; completing preparation does not itself authorize either. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Restore a real backup into an isolated environment and verify app/work/source/record/receipt/audit relationships plus deleted-tenant absence before serving.
- [ ] Measure recovery point and elapsed recovery time against explicitly adopted RPO/RTO; reseeding fixtures or showing a backup timestamp cannot pass.
- [ ] Exercise process/role/zone loss, provider outage and key/store unavailability; committed records survive and failures never leak data, duplicate commands or fabricate results.

## Verification

Use persisted operational state, actual resource/failure or restore drills, immutable identifiers and measured outcomes rather than fixture reseeding. Record the exact commands, environment, pins, expected/actual results and remaining limits in this ticket’s Log and `evidence/APP-068/` when implementation runs. The application harness is established by APP-007; do not invent a successful command or substitute the planning-board check for runtime evidence.
