## Why

Sharing goes to one person at a time and only as read or write. An app for a whole organization needs one share to everyone, and a choice of role: viewer, contributor or reviewer.

## What changes

The share grant gains a role (viewer, contributor or reviewer, mapping to `record_read`, `record_write` and `record_review`) and an organization-wide target that covers current and future members and is revoked as one unit. The share dialog offers both.

## Acceptance tests

- [x] Sharing with everyone gives every current and newly admitted member the chosen role, and revoking it removes the access for all of them at once.
- [x] Each role grants exactly its permissions, and a reviewer share is required for reviewer-only commands (APP-191). Only a sharer who holds `record_review` may grant reviewer, and `share_work` is never copied, so the owner controls who reviews.
- [ ] An independent Tier A review finds no blocker.
