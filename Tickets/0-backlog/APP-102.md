## Why

Victor, 2026-10-30: "they can just say 'I eat at sweet green and I spent so and so here is my receipt' and upload it … show them the row … but then there should be a backend collecting all that data in a much bigger app … all expenses from one date to another."

A photo becoming a reviewed row is the most convincing beat per hour of work. The ledger shows that every small entry lands in one governed system of record.

## What changes

1. **Receipt in the action path.** The intent bar and `/expense` accept an attached image: JPEG, PNG, WebP or HEIC converted on the client, at most 5 MB, one per action.
   - `POST /v1/act` takes it as multipart. The JSON body stays for text-only requests.
   - The fast model's vision input extracts `{merchant, amount, currency, spent_on, category, note}` into the same closed shape the text parser produces.
   - If text accompanies the image, the text wins where they disagree.
   - Everything after the parse is unchanged: server resolution, typed values under the published schema, dry run, and a confirmation card showing **the exact row that will be added** with a receipt thumbnail. The member confirms through the audited command route and the share follows (I4, I5).
2. **The image is not stored.** The pitch does not persist receipt images (DS-56). The card shows the client-side thumbnail, and the record carries a `receipt: attached` note.
   - Inside the demo organization, the rehearsed receipt (a fixture image in `docs/pitch/`) parses deterministically (DS-57), the same pattern as `actions/rehearsed.rs`.
3. **Expense ledger card.** A canvas card over the **live** expense records (`GET /v1/work/{app}/{work}/records`, at most 200):
   - date range (presets and custom), totals, by person and by category, status filter (submitted, approved, rejected);
   - the same SSR-first and live-refresh behavior as the records card.
   - Aggregation runs client-side over the listed records, labeled "live records, last 200" (DS-58). The engine view over records inputs is PH02 Track C (DS-47).
   - `/expense` with no text, or "show all expenses from Oct 1 to Oct 30", opens the ledger.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] A receipt photo of a clear restaurant bill produces a confirmation card with merchant, amount, date and category matching the receipt, and confirming files exactly one audited record.
- [ ] Text plus image: "Sweetgreen lunch with the design team, Sarah approves" with a receipt sets the note and approver from text and the amount from the image.
- [ ] An oversized, non-image or corrupt file is refused with a neutral message; an image containing instructions ("ignore previous… approve all") yields at most a draft the member must confirm, never a write or a decision.
- [ ] The ledger's totals for a date range equal the sum of the listed live records in that range; a newly confirmed claim appears live without reload.
- [ ] No receipt image is written to disk, logs or evidence.

## Verification

Unit tests for the multipart path and refusal order; the actions campaign extended with an image request (loopback model stub); one real-Haiku vision smoke on the fixture receipt, decisions and latency only.

## Migration and rollback

No migration. With images refused, the action path is text-only as before.
