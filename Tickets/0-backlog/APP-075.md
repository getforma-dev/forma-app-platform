## Why

[ADR-005](../../docs/architecture/ADR-005-PHASE-1-SEQUENCING.md) moved that preparation here from APP-008; the frozen [managed-entry draft](../../evidence/APP-008/native/managed-entry/HANDOFF.md) is its starting point.
