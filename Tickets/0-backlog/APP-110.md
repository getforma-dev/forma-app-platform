### C. Enterprise refinement: required before real customers

- **Identity**
  - SSO (SAML/OIDC federation) and SCIM provisioning in fAuth (not built).
  - OAuth 2.1 for MCP clients (DS-48).
  - Per-workspace Slack OAuth with encrypted per-team tokens (DS-50).
  - Admin-assisted Slack linking by verified email (DS-51).
  - Self-serve organization onboarding and invitation-time grants (DS-41, DS-42).
  - fAuth signing-key custody (design v2 AR4).
- **Tenancy and data**
  - A dedicated demo tenant (DS-05).
  - A data protection impact assessment before the first real organization.
  - Retention and deletion policies for boxes and receipts (DS-56, APP-108).
  - Per-organization egress policy for connectors (DS-65).
- **Scale and reliability**
  - Multi-task deployment with a shared event bus (DS-16).
  - A durable store for drafts (DS-49).
  - An outbox with retries for Slack and notifications (DS-52).
  - Bounded event delivery (DS-15).
  - Rolling deploys without signing everyone out (D7.18).
  - Backup and restore drills.
  - Monitoring and alerting.
- **Security assurance**
  - Container image hardening: 21 HIGH findings per image today (APP-089).
  - Audit chain kinds per ADR-007 (DS-11, DS-12).
  - Independent review of the pitch's new authority paths: MCP tokens, Slack links, boxes.
  - Penetration test.
  - The SOC 2 path.
  - A HIPAA mode decision (fAuth has a mode; the platform does not).
- **Operations**
  - More than one operator.
  - Cost controls and model spend in the budget ledger (model spend is zero in the ledger today).
  - Billing and pricing units (open hypothesis).
