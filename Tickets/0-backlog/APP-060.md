## Why

A principal, tenant or service-scope mistake can expose another organization’s work; this boundary must be proven before dependent integration. This ticket delivers: authenticate service calls with constrained role identities.

## What changes

Deliver the dedicated follow-on scope below, retaining the established typed interfaces and current authorization. Internal calls carry short-lived signed tokens naming caller, callee, organization, principal and epoch; wrong audience, expired, forged or replay-inappropriate tokens are refused. The control/data callees independently reread current membership and enforce their own role grants rather than trusting workspace-supplied identity. Rotate service signing credentials and test unavailable key verification; calls fail closed, alert safely and preserve in-process/remote request semantics without granting workspace publication/data-write rights.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes unapproved paid provisioning or real-data processing; completing preparation does not itself authorize either. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.
