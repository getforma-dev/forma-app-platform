## Why

These are the parts of APP-122 that Fable's review (2026-10-31) deferred, to keep the demo build small. They come after the Nov 3 revisit (APP-097).

## What changes

1. **Make it a card.** An Ask answer hands its record set to Shape as a preview of a new card. Nothing is added without a click.
2. **Persistent personal cards.**
   - A card made by Shape belongs to its app, is marked "made by you" and is stored per member (an additive migration under RLS and FORCE RLS).
   - The app owner can add it for everyone (an audited command).
3. **Scope.** `@` mentions in Ask (`@expenses.total`), and a removable scope chip.
4. **Verbs.**
   - `/forma <words>` joins the registry (`/shape` moved back into APP-122).
   - `/<folder> <words>` builds from a folder's files, with `/folder <name> formation …` kept as an alias.
5. **Pinned answers.**

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] **Make it a card** opens a Shape preview of the answer's records; nothing is added without a click.
- [ ] A "made by you" card persists across sessions, is invisible to other members, and a foreign organization gets the uniform 404. The owner adding it for everyone is audited.
- [ ] `@expenses.total` scopes an Ask; removing the chip scopes it to the canvas.
- [ ] `/forma <words>` does exactly what the same plain sentence does; `/q3 <words>` and `/folder:q3 <words>` run the folder's formation, and a verb or an app of the same name answers first.
- [ ] A pinned answer persists, is read-only (it never runs again) and is removable; a receipt storage never issued is refused.
- [ ] A stored card spec naming an unknown field, or carrying `"receipts": false`, is refused server-side.

## Verification

Unit and campaign tests (Docker); a browser walk on the dev stand.

## Migration and rollback

An additive migration (personal cards). Without it, cards stay session-only.
