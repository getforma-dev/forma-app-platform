## Why

Victor's real trial organization must be purged by 2026-12-14. `purge_organization` in `app/runtime/admission_operator.py` counted the organization's `genui_identity.linked_apps` and `share_events` rows and refused `guest_linked_shares_present` at every schema version while any existed, so the purge could not finish once any guest had linked or accepted one of the organization's live shares; revoking the shares did not clear the refusal. Migration 0024 (live on beta since 2026-11-14 18:16 UTC, APP-194) dropped the `share_events` key to `live_work_shares` exactly so that the links can go while each guest's hash chain stays verifiable.

## What changes

Only the operator's purge, in `app/runtime/admission_operator.py`; no migration.

- One declared gate, `GATED_PURGE_DELETIONS = {"genui_identity.linked_apps": 24}`, following the `GATED_PERMISSIONS` pattern; `schema_version(c)` reads `genui_meta.schema_version` after `begin()` has proved the catalog (the same read `stats` and `grant_permission` make).
- At schema 24 or later, the purge deletes the organization's `linked_apps` rows in the block that already deletes `shared_auth_flows` under the lifted FORCE, as `genui_owner`, then restores FORCE and re-checks it. That block runs before `deletion_order`, so the links go before the `live_work_shares` their non-deferrable key reaches. `share_events` has no gate and is never deleted.
- Below 24, a guest's row in either table refuses `guest_linked_shares_present` and the transaction rolls back, as before.
- After every content table is empty, a second lifted read proves that no `linked_apps` row of the organization remains (`purge_guest_links_remain`) and that its guests' `share_events` count is unchanged (`purge_guest_chains_changed`); FORCE is restored and re-checked again. The audit guards (`purge_audit_changed`) are unchanged.
- The result reports `linked_apps_deleted` and `share_events_kept` under `identity`, keeping `shared_auth_flows_deleted` and `guest_rows`; `deleted` carries `genui_identity.linked_apps`.

Grant and policy finding (migrations 0001–0024): `genui_identity` is `AUTHORIZATION genui_owner` and its tables are created under `SET LOCAL ROLE genui_owner` (0017:4–5); no later migration changes their owner or revokes from `genui_owner`. The owner holds DELETE implicitly, and the `account_scope` policy binds the owner only under FORCE, which `lift_identity_rls` already lifts for `linked_apps`. The `shared_auth_flows` DELETE in the same block relies on the same facts. No migration is needed.
