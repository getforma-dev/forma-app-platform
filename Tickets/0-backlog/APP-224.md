## Why

The canvas top bar is one flex row whose items never shrink. With a long organization name, an admin's links and a demo organization's note, it needed 1651 px. So between 761 and about 1650 px it ran out of its row, and the items at its end were cut off with no way to reach them: Help, the name menu and Sign out, at 1024, 1280, 1366 and 1440. Students in the class of Nov 28–30 mostly use 1280–1366 px laptops.

## What changes

CSS and markup only, in `app/admin/src/canvas/app.tsx` and `app/admin/src/styles/canvas.css`. No script changes and no dependency.

1. **The organization label** keeps its ellipsis and now carries its full name as its `title`. The status group no longer shrinks, so the label gives way first.
2. **Low-priority items collapse as the width drops** (desktop and tablet, 761 px and up):
   - at 1800 px and below, the connection state shows only as its dot, and the "No connected apps yet." line hides. Both stay `role="status"` words for assistive technology;
   - at 1536 and below, the demo organization's note hides (before, at 1200 and below);
   - at 1440 and below, the avatar of principal initials hides;
   - at 1366 and below, Workspace and Admin move into the name menu;
   - at 1152 and below, Connected apps moves into the name menu;
   - at 900 and below, the "canvas" product label hides. The organization label already hid at 1024 and below.

   Each step keeps the organization label at about 90 px or more in the worst case.
3. **The name menu** opens a panel: its name form, then the bar's links that moved in (`data-nav`). Each link shows only while its bar copy is hidden. Admin stays behind the admin flag in both places.
4. **Always in the bar, and reachable by keyboard:** Create app, Library, Help, the name menu and Sign out.
5. **Phones** keep their scrolling second row. Both menu panels are `position: fixed` there, so the row no longer clips the name panel. While a menu is open, the first-run name prompt drops below the top bar.
