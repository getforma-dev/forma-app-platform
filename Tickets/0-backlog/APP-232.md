## Why

The beta costs about $210 a month running and about $105 a month stopped (APP-216 "Why" and §3). ADR-013 D13.7 defers production ("not created before the Nov 3 revisit"; a second stack, same `infra/`, its own RDS, secrets, keys and hostname, promoted by digest, its budget Victor's). Keeping members on a free plan after Jan 15 means either the beta runs on, or a production stack takes the beta's `release.json` digests and its data moves, or the beta is parked and members wait. Each has a cost and a date, and the renewal mechanics need two weeks of lead time (APP-228 §5).

## What changes

1. **An ADR-013 amendment, dated, by Jan 1**, choosing one of: **park** (final snapshots; delete the instances, the load balancer and the WAF; keep the keys; the fixed-response "ended" page of APP-216 §3; about $15–20 a month; an "unpark" procedure tested once); **renew again** (the February ledger month, a renewed expiry and one release executed by Jan 14 14:00Z, with the ceilings for February); or **production** (`GenuiProduction` per D13.7: account, budget, hostname, the data move from the beta's database and bucket, DNS cutover, the first `$PRIV/production/` window). With the figures for January and February under each option, and what the members see on Jan 15 under each.
2. **The inputs the amendment cites:** the December `reconcile` total, APP-216's measured costs, the class's real use Dec 1–12 (members active, apps, AI use from `/admin`), and the new app builder's state (POST-CLASS "Before and during the class").
3. **The work that follows is its own ticket** (park, renew or production), opened the same day with its own release plan; this ticket is the decision.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] The ADR-013 amendment exists by Jan 1 with the chosen option, the figures for January and February, the dates, and what members see on Jan 15.
- [ ] If "renew again": the February month and the renewed expiry are applied and a release executes by Jan 14 14:00Z with the packet's stop recorded; if "park": the park procedure is rehearsed on the dev stand and the stop day's checklist is in the runbook; if "production": the production ticket names the account, budget and hostname and ADR-013's D13.7 is updated.
- [ ] Members are told, in the app and by email from the organization owner, what happens on Jan 15 at least a week before.
