## Why

A displayed number must be exact, bounded and traceable to the authorized evidence and record snapshot that produced it. This ticket delivers: enforce query admission, deadlines and cache authority.

## What changes

Per-org/node slots, fair bounded queue, cancel, constrained memory/disk and interrupted engine; safe typed errors never fake totals; optional cache includes effective scope and always reauthorizes.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes production deployment and real customer data; this is a synthetic qualification. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.
