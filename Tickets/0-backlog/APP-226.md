## Why

A viewer ([ADR-021](../../docs/architecture/ADR-021-ORGANIZATION-ROLES.md)) opens and reads what they are allowed to see and does nothing else, whatever share or grant they hold: a viewer with an organization-wide contributor share reads but cannot write. The ceiling has to hold at every write, share, connect and AI route, in Rust and in the database, so that no later route or missed gate reopens it.

## What changes

1. **Rust gates.** Every write and AI route reads `TrustedRequest::can(Capability)` before any body is read and refuses a viewer with 403 `viewer_read_only`: compose and templates, Ask and Shape, `/v1/act`, the assistant, the customizer, the command commit, a note, every folder write and AI route (create, upload plan, upload, sheet, data, attach, build, rename, share, explain, ask), the share routes (the dialog's read, the member list, the person and organization shares), the agent-token mint, the MCP caller (a token minted before a demotion is refused at its next call), the Slack link and the Slack action, confirmation and decision flows.
2. **SQL clauses.** `authorization::not_viewer` (one clause) in `work_clause` for `record_write`, in `reviewer_clause`, and in the `edit_definition`, `review_publish`, `share_work` and `export` branches of `permissions`; migration 0025's RESTRICTIVE `viewer_read_only_{insert,update,delete}` policies on every write table (apps, definitions, publications, saved work, records, commands, shares and grants, sources and folders, agent tokens, Slack links) refuse a viewer's member transaction at the table.
3. **The refusal.** `http::viewer_read_only()`; `Refusal::ViewerReadOnly` in the APP-217 error layer, so a browser navigation gets a themed page with the sentence "You have view-only access in this workspace. Ask an admin for member access to build or edit." (the error page pass now chooses among the refusals sharing a status by the body's code). For `/admin`, non-managers keep the uniform 404.
4. **The canvas.** `CanvasProps.can_build` (with `can_build_mode` and `view_only` for the SSR subset): a viewer sees no Create app, Connect an agent (`agents.ts` reads `data-can-build`), Share, Ask about this or Shape…, and the view-only sentence in place of the composer bar; members and above see no change.
