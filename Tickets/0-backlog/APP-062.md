## Why

The managed workflow must remain bounded, recoverable and auditable under real failure conditions before it handles customer data. This ticket delivers: complete append-only audit chains and immutable anchoring.

## What changes

Implement per-tenant/writer insert-only audit kinds, serialized hash-chain heads, immutable anchoring, daily verification and alarms. Customer support elevation and breakglass are the following dedicated ticket.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes unapproved paid provisioning or real-data processing; completing preparation does not itself authorize either. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Application roles can only append their permitted audit kinds; attempts to update/delete or append another writer’s category are refused.
- [ ] Concurrent events serialize each tenant/writer hash-chain head, and daily verification detects mutation, omission or broken linkage.
- [ ] Anchor heads to immutable storage at the declared cadence, alarm on missed anchors/audit failure, and retain evidence distinguishing attempt logs from committed mutation events.
