## Why

Victor, 2026-10-30, after signing in on beta: the canvas renders every openable app at once. Each demo member holds a saved work of all 14 library apps (`grant-library-access`) plus Spend Review and an authoring app: 16 apps and about 90 cards, and the island ran every view on load. His design: "add like a separate side window or right pane in which one can view all the cards created in categories and app so one can choose to bring to canvas or select them or do a /app card-name or number or something intuitive." Then: "copy the controls that other canvas apps already have, like Figma. Our controls are unorthodox; we want familiarity."

## What changes

1. **A clean board.** The canvas shows only the cards the member brought onto it, what this session places (compose, slash commands, the action path's app, widgets), the run cookie's restored work, and any records card holding a record that waits for the member's decision (Sarah's pending expense always shows; live `record.committed` brings one in too). The placed set is kept per member in this browser (DS-67). An empty board shows "What do you need?", one line, three chips (`/expense`, "Review expenses over $200", `/weather`) and a "Browse your library" link, with the intent bar focused.
2. **The library pane** (top-bar **Library**, `L`; Esc closes; slides over the board on desktop, a bottom sheet at phone width). Category → App → Card: library apps grouped by their first routing capability's prefix (`finance.*` → Finance, `security.*`, `ops.*`/`operations.*` → Operations, Sales, Support, People), in sidecar order; every other work under "My apps"; widgets under "Utilities". Apps are numbered `1…`, cards `3.0` (the app card), `3.1`, …; each card row shows its number, title, kind (number, chart, table, form, records, ledger) and an on-canvas indicator, and toggles Add/Remove; each app has "Add all". Search filters by name or number.
3. **Slash commands.** `/card <name or number>` places one card, `/app <name, slug or number>` an app's cards; the autocomplete lists matching cards and apps with their numbers. Existing commands (`/expense`, the library slugs, widgets, `/apps`) are unchanged.
4. **Performance.** `GET /canvas` builds the pane from metadata only (each work's outline and records listing; no view runs) and renders as groups only the auto works (restored, waiting records). The island fetches the member's placed works through the existing group route and runs only the cards on the board through the existing query route. The re-flow reads every size before it writes, under one deferred visibility pass; far-off (parked) cards skip rendering and keep their height; cards stop animating and taking the pointer while the camera moves.
5. **Figma-style controls.** Scroll or two-finger swipe pans (Shift: sideways); Cmd/Ctrl + scroll and pinch zoom toward the cursor; Space + drag and middle-drag pan; `V` move and `H` hand tools in a bottom-center toolbar with a zoom menu (zoom in/out, 100%, fit, selection, reset layout, shortcuts); Cmd/Ctrl + `=`/`-` zoom (the browser's page zoom is held back), Shift + 0/1/2 zoom to 100%/fit/selection; click a card's header to select, Shift + click to add, drag the header to move, arrows nudge 1 px (Shift 10 px), Delete/Backspace sends the selection back to the library (no data changes), Esc clears; `?` opens the shortcuts sheet. Touch: one finger pans the empty canvas, two fingers pinch. No shortcut fires while typing.
6. **Stacks** (Victor: "add the ability to stack cards and shuffle through them", after macOS Stacks, BumpTop's piles and Fences). Make one by dragging a card onto another and holding ~400 ms (the target lights up), by Cmd/Ctrl + G on a selection, or with "Stack" on an app in the pane. A stack shows its top card in full, two or three offset edges behind it (plain elements), its name (the first card's app; double-click to rename), "+N" and "2/5" with dots. Horizontal scroll, Shift + scroll, ←/→ on the selected stack or a swipe leafs through it (a quick leaf animation). "+N", Enter/Space or a double-click fans the cards out in a grid beside it; Esc or a click outside folds them back; a card dragged out of the fan leaves the stack; Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + G spreads the stack. A record committed in a stacked card's work pulses the stack, and a records card with a record waiting comes to the top. Only the top card's element sits in a stack; the others stay in the hidden board and run when they reach the top. Stacks are kept with the placed set (DS-67). `/arrange` lays the canvas out again, one row per app.

Nothing changes in authorization, RLS, receipts or the command route: the pane is built from the reads the page already made under the work page's and record page's own authorization, and every number still comes from `POST /v1/work/{app}/{work}/queries/{view}` with its receipt.

## Acceptance tests

- [x] A demo member's canvas starts clean (empty state, or only their placed cards and waiting records).
- [x] The pane lists categories, numbered apps and cards from metadata only; Add places a card, animated and centered; Remove takes it back.
- [x] `/card 3.2` and `/app expense` place cards; autocomplete shows numbers.
- [x] A record waiting for the member's approval still shows on a clean board.
- [x] The controls above work with mouse, trackpad and keyboard, and never fire while typing.
- [x] Stacks: made from the pane, Cmd/Ctrl + G and drag-and-hold; leaf through, fan out and fold back, spread; kept across reloads.
- [ ] Fences (labeled regions cards drop into and move with, roll-up by double-click): not built; `/arrange` covers "arrange by app" as rows.

## Verification
