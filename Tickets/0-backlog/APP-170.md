## Why

Apps are whole products made of components. The Library and canvas should make that identity clear and prevent overlapping owners of the same DOM components.

## What changes

Design and admit durable component visibility separately from browser-local personal hiding. Define how sections/actions/modal targets behave when a component is hidden, require recoverable reachable content, and qualify member/guest/connected views. A hidden component is presentation, not security; authorization and shared-data projection stay authoritative. Do not remove required component references or smuggle hidden IDs into today's presentation contract.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Admitted publication contract defines section and action visibility without unreachable required components.
- [ ] Saved design applies across devices and allowed recipients with old-version compatibility.
- [ ] Tests prove visibility never expands grants or exposes data and recover hiding without losing records/drafts.

## Verification

Record focused tests, browser evidence and independent review before completion. Planning checks alone do not prove behavior.

## Migration and rollback

Preserve saved works, grants, records and receipts. Browser-local view metadata is bounded and contains identifiers only. Deploy through the existing protected pipeline after review; no cloud change is part of this ticket's current implementation turn.
