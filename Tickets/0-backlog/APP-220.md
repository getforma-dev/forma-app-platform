## Why

These are what mature multi-tenant SaaS products run. For one beta organization of 25 people they cost more than they return; each becomes worth it at a clear trigger.

## What changes

Each item becomes its own ticket when its trigger arrives.

| Item | What | Trigger | Rough cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Per-route RED metrics | Rate, errors and duration per route template (CloudWatch embedded metric format) | More than one organization, or a latency complaint the dashboard can't place | about $0.30 per metric |
| Distributed tracing | OpenTelemetry spans from request to database and model call (X-Ray or an OTLP backend) | A slow path that logs can't explain, or a second service | X-Ray free tier: 100k traces a month |
| Error tracking | Grouped exceptions with release tags (Sentry, or self-hosted GlitchTip) | Real users beyond the class. It is a third party in the data path, so it needs a privacy decision and a CSP change for any browser SDK. | free developer tier |
| Public status page | "Is it down or is it me?" (Better Stack or Instatus free tier, fed by the APP-218 probes) | Any audience larger than one class | free tier |
| On-call paging | SMS or push for the "unreachable" and error-rate alarms (SNS SMS, or PagerDuty/Opsgenie) | Users who aren't in the same room as the operator | SMS cents each |
| SLO burn-rate alerts | Multi-window burn rates on a stated availability objective | Once an SLO is promised to customers | a few alarms |
| Synthetic journeys | A scripted sign-in, open-app and submit (CloudWatch Synthetics or Playwright on a schedule) | Before public sign-up | about $5 a canary a month |
| Real-user monitoring | Browser performance and errors (CloudWatch RUM) | Public launch. It needs a script under the strict CSP. | per session |
| Tenant usage metering | Per-organization AI calls, storage and members as metrics and records: the input to plans and billing | The tiers work (A13) | part of A13 |
| fAuth metrics | Sign-in success rate, latency and refusals by kind (FORMA-370 follow-up) | More than one tenant on fAuth | small |
