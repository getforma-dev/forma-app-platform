## Why

Victor, 2026-10-31: "I think we got commands mixed up with apps … showing all apps in the commands seems not right … they should go in /apps, not in commands."

A Fable design review (2026-10-31) confirmed the mix:
- `command-registry.ts` lists verbs (`/card`, `/unite`, `/arrange`) and nouns (`/expense`, `/pto`) as peers;
- six apps have hand-written hints in `KNOWN`;
- every catalog app and every saved app becomes a `Command`.

The established pattern keeps verbs apart from things you open or address: Slack (slash commands vs the app directory), Discord (commands grouped under their app), Raycast and VS Code (palette vs quick open), and Figma (actions vs assets).

## What changes

The design is Fable's recommendation.

1. **Verbs: the `/` menu and the `/commands` sheet.** Only things you *do*: `note`, `folder … formation`, `card`, `unite`, `split`, `arrange`, `apps`, `app`, `commands`, `shortcuts`, `agent`, and the widget verbs (`weather`, `calculator`, `clock`). That is about 12 items, and the whole slash menu on a clean canvas.
2. **Nouns: apps are *addresses*.**
   - They live in the Library pane (`/apps [query]`) and in quick open (`/app <name>`).
   - Each Library row shows the app's address (`/expense`), its hint and an example.
   - On a clean canvas, `/apps` shows the catalog to compose from.
3. **Addressing an app with an instruction stays `/slug <words>`, as a second grammar** (like Slack's `/giphy cats`).
   - `/` alone shows verbs only.
   - From the first letter (`/e…`), the dropdown searches verbs, then app addresses, in a separate section: "Apps — type /name and what you want".
   - Addresses never appear in `/commands`. Its footer says "Your apps answer to /name — see /apps".
   - Rejected: `@expense` (`@` is folder mentions) and `/app expense …` (longer with no gain). Plain sentences stay the fallback.
4. **Where addresses come from.**
   - Library apps take theirs from the catalog (`command.slug` and aliases).
   - Saved apps get theirs at "Save as /name".
   - Admin renames come later.
   - Open decision for Victor: list addresses for every app the member can open, or only for apps already on the canvas. Fable recommends canvas-only, with the pitch organization's fixture pre-placing the demo apps.
5. **Hints come from the definition, not from copy in the client.**
   - The server derives each app's hint from its create command's writable fields (status and initial fields excluded, labeled) and its transitions ("… or approve/reject").
   - Examples come from the catalog's `routing.intent_examples`.
   - Non-record apps get "what to show".
   - An optional `command.hint` override in the catalog keeps demo-quality copy.
   - `GET /v1/commands` returns `hint` and `examples`, and the client drops `KNOWN`.

**Implementation.**
- `command-registry.ts`: split into `commands` (verbs) and `addresses`, with `setAddresses`, `addressList` and `matchAddresses`. `findCommand` falls back to `findAddress`, so every typed `/expense …` path is untouched.
- `slash.ts`: the dropdown gets two sections.
- `command-sheet.ts`: verbs, plus the footer.
- `library.ts`: rows show address, hint and example; search covers aliases and hints.
- `app/src/composer/commands.rs`: adds `hint` and `examples` (scoped per decision 4). Parsing stays backward-compatible.

There is no slug or catalog migration.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] `/` alone lists verbs only. The `/commands` sheet has no app sections, and its footer points to `/apps`.
- [ ] `/ex` shows the matching verbs, then an "Apps" section with `/expense` and `/expense-review`, each with its derived hint.
- [ ] `/expense $86 Nobu, Sarah approves`, `/expense` (ledger), `/pto …` and a saved `/big-claims …` behave exactly as before.
- [ ] Server: the expense hint is derived from `writable_fields`. Addresses follow decision 4. A saved work appears after naming. Library rows show address, hint and example, and `/apps expense` filters by slug, alias and hint.

None. Clients that ignore `hint` and `examples` keep working.
