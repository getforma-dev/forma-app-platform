## Why

The running two-app journey and its evidence must establish the capability; a staged recording or hardcoded second dashboard cannot. This ticket delivers: qualify load, noisy neighbor and budgets at declared limits.

## What changes

Cold/warm million-row shapes, six-view peak 60 ops/s and 2 record submissions/s, open-loop overload and 10x configuration; p50/p95/p99 plus errors/admitted/rejected load, per-org fairness, resource caps and model stop.

Keep this a reviewable slice: M means approximately 1–3 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes unapproved paid provisioning or real-data processing; completing preparation does not itself authorize either. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Measure cold/warm million-row and six-view workloads at declared 60-query-ops/s peak and two same-record submissions/s, reporting p50/p95/p99, errors and admitted/rejected load.
- [ ] Apply noisy-neighbor overload and configured 10x capacity; other tenants retain their measured fair-share service and queues/memory/temp disk remain bounded.
- [ ] Exhaust import/generation/model budgets and verify honest visible responses while published deterministic forms/queries continue; adjust documented caps to evidence rather than relaxing authority.

- [ ] Include invitation/login/registration abuse, direct API bypass, many-account and forged-forwarded-IP controls in declared-limit testing. Concurrent trial/provider reservations, stale sessions and expired/revoked entitlements cannot exceed configured global or organization allowances; retain real dispatch/charge evidence and honest refusal states.
