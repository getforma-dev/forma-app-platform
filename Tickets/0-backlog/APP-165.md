## Why

Current member and guest provider management invitations intentionally use `delivery_mode=server_only` and require `email_delivery=suppressed`. Verification/reset email delivery does not prove an invitation was emailed. A recipient needs to distinguish invitations to several workspaces or individual shared apps and return to the right destination later.

## What changes

Send the actual Forma-owned invitation through a qualified transport. Include the workspace/app name, inviter where authorized, whether access is workspace membership or one shared app, the purpose-bound acceptance link and a stable appropriate return/sign-in link. Keep opaque routing available for private workspaces. Preserve provider server-only handoff and Forma's invitation/share authorization; simply enabling provider invitation mail must not bypass product admission. Add durable, idempotent delivery status, safe retry, resend limits, expiration/revocation behavior and transport failure reporting. Do not put access tokens or provider handoffs in email URLs or evidence.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Actual delivered membership and app-share messages identify their distinct scope and open the correct pending acceptance; neither grants authority merely by opening email.
- [ ] Invitations to two workspaces remain distinguishable and route correctly, including already-signed-in and wrong-account cases.
- [ ] Accepted members have a stable reusable workspace entry link; app-only guests return to their app without workspace membership.
- [ ] Duplicate delivery jobs, retries and resend do not duplicate admission, shares or broaden authority; expired/revoked invitations fail safely.
- [ ] Message escaping, link allowlists, recipient binding, delivery abuse controls and privacy are independently reviewed.
- [ ] Genuine delivery evidence is retained separately from synthetic transport tests; no unrequested emails are sent during qualification.

## Verification

Qualify app-owned acceptance links with the current invite/share campaigns and a captured transport first. Then perform explicitly scoped real mailbox delivery and multi-workspace browser acceptance, with secrets and real recipients excluded from public evidence.

## Migration and rollback

Any delivery queue/outbox schema is additive and reviewed. Disabling delivery preserves existing manually shared links, accounts, memberships and shares. Never recreate a tenant or replay admission to retry an email.
