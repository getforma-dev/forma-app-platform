## Why

A displayed number must be exact, bounded and traceable to the authorized evidence and record snapshot that produced it. This ticket delivers: enforce exact arithmetic, units, windows and result-scope semantics.

## What changes

Deliver the dedicated follow-on scope below, retaining the established typed interfaces and current authorization. Compute fixtures with exact integer bytes/currency and half-open UTC windows: five events, 1,140,000 total and 114,000 per full-window minute; upper-bound event excluded. Reject mixed currencies, incompatible units, ambiguous timestamps, unsafe large-int transport and undeclared null/coverage assumptions instead of coercing a successful value. Pagination/top-N never changes the complete authorized filtered total; stable ties, omitted groups, lookup cardinality and empty-bucket semantics produce reproducible results.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes production deployment and real customer data; this is a synthetic qualification. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.
