## Why

The canvas must show the same linked, receipted views as the focused workspace, and any connection it draws must be a real relationship rather than an invented one. Evidence relationships need APP-023's receipts and APP-025's linked views, so this work follows the canvas geometry slice (APP-027) instead of holding it back. This ticket delivers: integrate linked views and evidence connections on the canvas.

## What changes

Linked view cards on the canvas share APP-025's declared filter, context and filter epoch, and re-query through its path regardless of card geometry changes; actual evidence connections are drawn only from declared source/view bindings and receipts; the keyboard and focused view complete the same linked-view task on the same work state.

Keep this a reviewable slice: M means approximately 1–3 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes production deployment and real customer data; this is a synthetic qualification. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Drawn connections correspond to actual source/view/evidence relationships rather than invented causality. (Split from APP-027 by ADR-009 D9.14.)
- [ ] Linked view cards on one canvas share a declared filter and show its scope; a filter change re-queries every linked card at one context and filter epoch through APP-025's path, including after cards are moved, resized, grouped or fit.
- [ ] The linked-view task completes through keyboard navigation and the focused view on the same work state and epoch, and a late response from a prior filter, work or organization never renders in any canvas card.
