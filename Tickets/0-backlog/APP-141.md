## Why

The canvas currently shows an app card and other cards that often do not read as one thing. APP-120 already says an app contains cards and a widget is a one-off. What is missing is the moment the app is created. Victor wants that moment to happen before anything is dropped on the board.

## What changes

A member starts an app from an empty canvas with an explicit modal, not by dragging a finished library card onto the board.

1. The modal offers industries, categories, and favorites. Picking one offers templates. The set should cover the common cases. A fully custom app is named as later work and is not this slice.
2. After the template is chosen, the member describes the app. That description is the second step. It uses the existing composer and admission path. Model output stays data.
3. The result is one app. Its cards are its parts: the views and the form. A widget such as the calculator stays a widget and is not turned into an app by this ticket.
4. Nothing is placed on the canvas until the member confirms.

This ticket does not decide three open questions. They stay open:

- Whether a card can leave the app and become a widget while the data stays.
- Whether a card can be removed from the app and still keep its data.
- Whether the folder, called a box in APP-108, stays. APP-120 already treats a folder as a widget whose formation produces an app. Do not remove folders while that question is open.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] From an empty canvas the member opens a modal, picks an industry or category or a favorite, and then a template, before any card is on the board.
- [ ] Describing the app is the step after the template. The description is admitted through the existing composer path. A custom-from-scratch app is not required for this slice.
- [ ] Confirming creates one app whose cards are its parts. Cancelling places nothing.
- [ ] A calculator, clock, or weather widget placed beside that app stays a widget.

## Verification

Read APP-120 and `choose_authoring` before building. A new member who owns no app is APP-139's hole. This ticket does not invent a second permission model. One fresh-browser check on a worktree port. Ports 4001–4003 and 4010 stay untouched.

## Migration and rollback

No migration. Removing the modal returns creation to today's composer and library placement. Apps already created stay.
