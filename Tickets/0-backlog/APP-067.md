## Why

The managed workflow must remain bounded, recoverable and auditable under real failure conditions before it handles customer data. This ticket delivers: instrument slos, cost and payload-safe telemetry.

## What changes

Client navigation/six-view/commit/revocation timings, role metrics and safe correlation IDs, job age/slots/shed/storage/spend; dashboard plus actionable alerts; raw evidence/token/intent log scan; verified current cost model.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes unapproved paid provisioning or real-data processing; completing preparation does not itself authorize either. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Client timings measure navigation, complete six-view refresh, record acknowledgement and revocation with authorization/network included; role/job/query/slot metrics correlate safely.
- [ ] Dashboards and actionable alerts cover availability/latency, canary, audit anchors, queue age, shedding, storage runway and spend without raw evidence/intent/token logging.
- [ ] Recompute costs from current measured hosts/stores/model/retry/backup/egress use and inspect safe telemetry samples; explain targets versus observed data and budget exhaustion behavior.
