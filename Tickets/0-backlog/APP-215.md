## Why

Five reviews of `origin/main` on 2026-11-15 found no critical and no data-exposing finding in the application, and seven availability and privacy gaps a class of 25 could hit in ordinary use or one student could cause on purpose:

- **Identity H1.** The three service-wide sign-in windows (`SIGNIN_GLOBAL` 600/hour, `CALLBACK_GLOBAL` 800/hour, `INVITE_GLOBAL` 200/day) counted every admitted attempt before the code or handle was judged, so a few source addresses, each under its own per-address window, could fill a service window and refuse every student's sign-in for an hour, or every code for a day, on signup day.
- **Content M1.** `POST /v1/work/{app}/{work}/commands/{command}` had no per-member window: a student could script record creation into a shared app up to the projection bound (proven locally: 15 serial creates, 15 × 200).
- **Content M2.** Every upload preview and data read spawned a parser host (2.5 GiB RSS kill line) with no global concurrency bound on that path; a handful of simultaneous spreadsheet imports could take the single 8 GiB task down.
- **Content M3.** The daily model-call cap (1,000) was one process-wide counter with no per-member share; one or two students within their hourly windows could pause AI for the whole class until 00:00 UTC.
- **Sharing M1.** The records card of a live-shared page labelled every creator with the member's chosen name for an outside guest, against ADR-020 §17.10.
- **Sharing M3.** A guest share required the sponsor's membership epoch to equal the share's pinned epoch; sign-out advances the epoch, so every friend link died the evening its sponsor signed out.
- **Infrastructure M4.** No `Strict-Transport-Security` on `beta.getforma.dev`.

The reviews' remaining findings (fAuth's shared limiter, the operator identity, monitoring, the removed member's organization-wide share, M2) need infrastructure or a migration and are not in this release; sharing M2 gets a runbook interim here.

## What changes

1. **Global windows** (`app/src/http/limits.rs`): `SIGNIN_GLOBAL` 6,000/hour, `CALLBACK_GLOBAL` 8,000/hour (the sum rule), `INVITE_GLOBAL` 2,000/day. Every per-address window is unchanged. The tests pin the values; the ADR-006 amendment records the decision.
2. **Command route window** (`app/src/commands/routes.rs` `submit`): after the request is shaped and before storage, the member's `MEMBER_WRITES` window (120 an hour per member, shared with the folder, notes, share and name routes) is taken; over it, 429 `too_many_requests` with `Retry-After`, nothing written. Only commands that would reach storage count; a replay of the same key counts too. A student answering a quiz, posting or closing spends one each; the refresh herd reads. The MCP and Slack commits keep their own windows.
3. **Parser slots** (`app/src/boxes/inspect.rs`): `run` takes one of `PARSER_SLOTS = 2` before `sandbox::parse_with`, waiting at most `PARSE_QUEUE_WAIT = 10 s`; past that the parse is refused `parse_busy` (the upload is stored and its preview says so; the registration path answers 429 with `Retry-After: 5`; the canvas words it). Every caller of `inspect_file` is covered: upload preview, file preview, registration, the folder's Ask, cards and build.
4. **Per-member daily AI allowance** (`app/src/composer/model.rs`): `MemberDailyCap`, a bounded table (`MAX_MEMBERS` 20,000, emptied at the UTC reset) keyed `organization:principal`, default `DEFAULT_MEMBER_DAILY_CALLS = 80`, `GENUI_MODEL_DAILY_CALLS_PER_MEMBER` overrides it like `GENUI_MODEL_DAILY_CALLS`; the process cap of 1,000 stays the ceiling. `Anthropic::admit`, the one choke point every call passes, takes the member's slot first and the process cap second, giving the member slot back when the process cap refuses. `ModelRequest.member` carries the member; the compiler enforced every call site: the composer route (router, transform, generation), Ask (records and widget), Shape (card, app, widget), the folders (Ask, brief, explain, build from folder), the action path (parse, receipt), the assistant proposals, the widget customizer, MCP and Slack (which act as the member). Calls without a member: none in product code; the demo rehearsals pass `None` and call no model; the test stubs pass `None`. `model::daily_limit_for(member)` answers the same 429 `daily_limit` with `"scope":"member"` when the member's own allowance is what is reached, and `daily_limit_text_for` words it for MCP and Slack; the workspace body and text are unchanged. The canvas (`daily-limit.ts`, `MEMBER_DAILY_LIMIT_MESSAGE`) says: "You've used your AI allowance for today. Building apps and Ask resume for you at 00:00 UTC; your apps and entries keep working."
5. **Neutral guest names** (`app/src/query/canvas.rs`, `app/src/directory.rs`, `app/src/sharing/live.rs`): `records_cards` takes a `guest` flag and labels the `filed by` cell and the row's `creator` through `directory::creator_label(guest, …)`, the function `record_components` already used (moved there; `record_components` delegates). The shared page passes `true`; every member surface passes `false`.
6. **Guest shares survive sign-out** (`app/src/storage/shared_access.rs` `sponsor_clause_for`): the `m.membership_epoch = s.sponsor_epoch` equality is removed. The clause still requires the sponsor's active membership, live entitlement, current `record_read` and share authority (unrevoked creator, or `share_work`), each of which ends on removal; revocation, expiry, a changed publication, digest, work version or policy version, and the suspended-organization pause are unchanged. The pinned epoch stays in the share row and in `check_guest`, binding the access to the share row. **Authorization change: gets a focused independent review.**
7. **HSTS** (`app/src/http/mod.rs` `finish`, the outermost layer): `Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` on every answer of the service, the uniform 404 included; no `preload`. A browser ignores the header on a plain-HTTP answer, so the loopback dev listeners are unaffected.

**Runbook interim for sharing M2** ([TRIAL-RUNBOOK §10](../../docs/operations/TRIAL-RUNBOOK.md#10-the-end-of-trial-purge-by-sunday-dec-14), pointed to from §5): the exact SQL that ends a removed member's organization-wide share, operator with Victor's word, because no operator override touches `work_shares` and the code fix needs a migration.
