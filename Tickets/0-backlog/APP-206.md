## Why

When a member without share authority (a student) files a record naming an approver, the canvas tries to share the work under the filer's rights and shows "The share with X was refused; the record is filed." (APP-189 known issue). The approver named is usually the owner or a reviewer, who already sees the record.

## What changes

Exactly ADR-020 §8, in builder B4's files: `app/src/storage/sharing.rs` (one lock-free read, `Workspace::approver_standing`), `app/src/actions/resolve.rs`, `routes.rs`, `mod.rs`, `tests.rs`, `app/admin/src/canvas/actions.ts`, `app/src/mcp/tools.rs`, `mcp/tests.rs`, `app/src/slack/flows.rs`, `slack/tests.rs`, and the `actions`, `mcp` and `slack` campaigns.
- Per approver the draft carries `standing`: `reviewer` (the app's owner, the work's unrevoked creator, a `record_review` holder, or any active member under a live organization-wide reviewer share: `authorization::reviewer_clause`), `shareable` (not a reviewer, and the filer's `share_standing` allows a contributor share), or `unreachable`.
- The canvas shares only `shareable` through `/share/add` (unchanged); `reviewer` notes "Filed for X's review" with no request; `unreachable` notes "X is not a reviewer of this app; the record is filed and visible to its reviewers." MCP and Slack do the same server-side. The share routes and definers are unchanged.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] A contributor under the organization-wide share files a record naming the owner, then a personal reviewer, then a member covered by an organization-wide reviewer share: each draft says `reviewer`, no share request is sent, the note reads "Filed for X's review", and the named person's listing shows the record; a filer with share authority naming a non-reviewer still shares as today (`shareable`); a contributor naming a non-reviewer gets `unreachable`, no share, and the note; the same through MCP `forma_commit` and the Slack flow.
- [ ] No path writes a grant for a filer without share authority: the `work_permissions` and `work_shares` rows are unchanged after each contributor case, and a forged `standing: shareable` in a replayed draft is ignored because the server recomputes it at commit.
- [ ] The `actions`, `mcp`, `slack`, `sharing`, `share-routes` and `private-entries` campaigns stay green; the "share was refused" message never appears for a reviewer approver.
- [ ] An independent Tier A review (authorization: who can be reached, what the standing discloses) finds no blocker; the review file is in `evidence/APP-206/`.

## Verification

`just check`, `just campaign actions mcp slack sharing share-routes private-entries`, and the review file.
