## Why

Victor wants a click to choose the context, then a question about that thing, the way a chat attaches to the object in front of him. The thing is the app on the board, the app page, the focused part of that page, or a one-off widget.

## What exists today

`POST /v1/ask` already takes a scope. The body is `{"question": "<1..500>", "scope": {"kind": "card" | "app" | "canvas", "address", "works": [...]}}`. A card or app scope names one work. A card scope may name one collection, and then only that collection is read. The answer is exactly `{"text", "receipts", "references"}`. No session, or a work the member cannot read, is the uniform 404. The log never stores the question or a record value (`app/src/composer/talk.rs`).

There is no ask scope whose kind is `widget`. `POST /v1/shape` can target a widget. Ask cannot. A widget question is new work on the ask route, not a reuse of shape.

The canvas, the app page, and focus do not yet attach the intent bar to the clicked object. The member can already ask with an address typed by hand.

## What changes

1. On the board, the member selects an app and asks. The request is `kind: "app"` for that app's saved work. The answer is about that app. Another app on the same board is not in the scope.
2. Inside the APP-142 page, asking uses that same app scope. If focus is open, the request is `kind: "card"` for the focused part, including its collection when the part is a records card. Closing focus does not keep the card scope.
3. On a weather, clock, or calculator widget, asking sends a new widget scope that names that widget and no work id. The answer is about the widget. It does not open an app and it does not read another app's records. The widget scope is rejected, with no number shown, when the widget is not one of those three.
4. A governed number in the answer carries the receipt the ask route already returns. A refusal (`could_not_answer`, or no model and no rehearsed path) shows the refusal and no number. The question does not grant read or write. The scope is only something `Data` would already let this member read.

Demo rehearsed answers stay limited to `demo::is_demo_org`. This ticket does not add a rehearsed widget answer.

## What this ticket does not do

- It does not add a second ask route.
- It does not make Shape the way the member asks.
- It does not let the model return markup or a grant.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Asking from a selected app sends `POST /v1/ask` with `scope.kind` `app` for that work. A second app on the board is not in `works`.
- [ ] Asking inside the app page uses that app scope. With focus open, the scope is `card` for the focused part, and a records part names its collection.
- [ ] Asking from a weather, clock, or calculator widget sends a widget scope and no `work_id`. Any other widget kind is refused with no number.
- [ ] A governed number in the answer includes the receipt the route already returns. `could_not_answer` shows no number. The question text is not in the log.

## Verification

Read the ask route and APP-094 before wiring a new entry. The context is a scope the session may already read. It is not a new permission. One fresh-browser check on a worktree port. Ports 4001–4003 and 4010 stay untouched.

## Migration and rollback

No migration. Removing the three entries leaves Ask on the canvas as it is today.
