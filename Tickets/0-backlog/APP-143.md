## Why

Ask currently sits on the canvas as a whole. Victor wants the question to belong to the thing he clicked, the way a chat attaches to the object in front of him. That thing is the app page, one of its cards, or a one-off widget.

## What changes

1. On the board, choosing an app and asking uses that app as the context.
2. Inside the app page from APP-142, asking uses the page, or the focused part if focus is open.
3. On a widget, asking uses that widget only. A calculator question does not become a question about an app.
4. The answer goes through the existing ask path. A governed number still carries its receipt. Model output stays data. No new grant is taken from the question.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Asking from a selected app on the board answers about that app and not about a different app on the same board.
- [ ] Asking inside the app page answers about that page. With focus open, the answer is about the focused part.
- [ ] Asking from a weather, clock, or calculator widget answers about that widget and does not open an app.
- [ ] A number in the answer carries the existing receipt. A refusal shows no number.

## Verification

Read the ask route and APP-094 before wiring a new entry. The context is a scope the session may already read. It is not a new permission. One fresh-browser check on a worktree port. Ports 4001–4003 and 4010 stay untouched.

## Migration and rollback

No migration. Removing the three entries leaves Ask on the canvas as it is today.
