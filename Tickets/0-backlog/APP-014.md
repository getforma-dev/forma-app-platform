## Why

Internal decisions must survive retries and restarts without duplicate mutations, silent overwrite or stale authority. This ticket delivers: prove command retries, conflicts and authorization race ordering.

## What changes

Deliver the dedicated follow-on scope below, retaining the established typed interfaces and current authorization. Race same-key and different-key edits; one intended write commits, changed-payload reuse and stale distinct edits conflict with authorized recovery, and other principals cannot retrieve the key.
