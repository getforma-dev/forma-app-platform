## Why

[ADR-010](../../docs/architecture/ADR-010-NOV-2-MVP-CUT.md) trimmed APP-074 to the public entry, denials, replay refusals, abuse limits and trusted proxy so the invite-first beta could go live on 2026-11-02. The two moved items repeat APP-073's admission races and provider fault handling through the real GenUI routes against the test-only synthetic issuer. They were judged safe to defer because APP-073's campaign proves the same transactions with observed PostgreSQL lock waits, APP-009 item 5 proves both completion orders at the session boundary, and the MVP's invitations are operator-provisioned SES-verified recipients. They are still required before invitations go beyond the demo principals.

## What changes

Drive the APP-074 routes (`GET /invite`, `POST /invite/validate`, the PAR handoff, the ADR-006 callback and pending routes) on the local real PostgreSQL 16.13/TLS harness with two or more concurrent clients and the loopback fAuth stand-in (`fake_fauth.py`) with its fault injection. No production code change is expected; add the campaign cases and retain receipts under `evidence/APP-082/`. If a defect is found, fix it under Tier A review (ADR-010 §4).

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Concurrent last-slot redemption, identical retry, code/entitlement revocation and requests already waiting in admission preserve APP-073's real transaction results. A late response or existing session cannot revive expired/revoked entitlement. Record both race orders and restart/reopen behavior. (Moved from APP-074 by ADR-010.)
- [ ] Exercise provider create/revoke timeout and retry, remote revocation propagation, pending reconciliation, and signup completion after local code expiry/revocation/exhaustion. No unknown remote outcome causes blind duplicate invite creation, fabricated rollback success or a GenUI grant; retain the partial identity outcome and reconcile it through the accepted operator/provider procedure. (Moved from APP-074 by ADR-010.)
- [ ] The campaign cases run in the tenant-foundation workflow on the exact source and an independent review binds the receipts; any defect found is fixed and the affected APP-074 and APP-076 cases are rerun before the next invitation beyond the demo principals.

## Verification

Reuse APP-074's local harness and evidence layout (`evidence/APP-074/local/`), the admission campaign's observed-lock-wait barrier and the provider slice's `fake_fauth.py` faults. Retain sanitized requests, dispatch counters, database outcomes and both race orders. Source obligations: ADR-003, ADR-006, ADR-007 D7.1/D7.9; platform C01/C11/C15, R15, SR1/SR2/SR7/SR12.

## Migration and rollback

No schema change. Test-only additions; a found defect follows APP-074's migration and rollback rules.
