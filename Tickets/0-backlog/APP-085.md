## Why

The [APP-010 review](../../evidence/APP-010/independent-review.md) accepted three items as Phase 1 limits and its re-check left r1 open. [ADR-010](../../docs/architecture/ADR-010-NOV-2-MVP-CUT.md) deferred them past the 2026-11-02 MVP, whose single `owner_publish` policy per organization makes them unexploitable; they are owed before PH03's publication policy work.

## What changes

1. **m3, server-side policy resolution:** `publish` resolves the applicable policy per organization (or space) server-side and refuses any caller-named policy id, before a second policy row coexists with `owner_publish` outside tests; add a positive and a negative campaign case.
2. **m7, remaining fixture SQL:** add an operator subcommand for publication policy rows (`create-policy`, audited on the operator chain with a D7.8 kind) so the campaign's `independent_review` row stops being fixture SQL; Northstar's expenses source revision moves to the import tickets (PH02) and stays fixture SQL until then, recorded as such.
3. **r1, permission events name nothing:** bind the permission and resource into `outcome_digest` under a `forma:operator-permission:v1` domain over `{{permission, resource kind, app, work, source}}` in the next contract edition; until then the README limit reads "permission events do not name the permission or the source".
4. **Coordinator choices to confirm or revise:** `grant-permission` allows `discover`; `create-app` has no kind of its own (its owner `edit_definition` grant is the record); revocations advance the member epoch and grants do not; `ALTER POLICY` in 0005 rather than editing 0003. Record each as accepted in the README or change it.
5. **Catalog size:** record that the v5 body (219,611 B) is about 52 % of the 524,288 cap against the 419,430 guard and that v7 projects to about 241 KB (review m8), so the D7.12 cap needs no further move in Phase 1.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] `publish` refuses a caller-named policy that is not the organization's applicable one, and an `independent_review` organization refuses owner self-publication through the server-resolved policy; both are campaign cases seen failing before the fix.
- [ ] Publication policy rows are created only through the audited operator subcommand; the definition campaign no longer uses fixture SQL for them, and the remaining fixture SQL (the expenses source revision) is named with its PH02 owner.
- [ ] The `forma:operator-permission:v1` digest domain is in the next contract edition with vectors in Rust and Python, and `permission.granted`/`permission.revoked` events carry it; or the README limit is widened and the edition that will carry it is named.
- [ ] The coordinator choices are each confirmed or revised in the README with a dated note, and an independent review binds the changes.

## Verification

Reuse the definition campaign (`native_definition_campaign`), the schema campaign and the operator tests; record under `evidence/APP-010/` with a dated residuals section. Source obligations: C09 (the server applies the organization's policy), design §5.3; ADR-007 D7.6, D7.8, D7.19; ADR-002 for the edition.

## Migration and rollback

Additive changes only (a policy subcommand, a digest domain in a new edition). Existing publications and their sequences are unchanged; a faulty policy resolution is disabled by refusing publication, never by widening it.
