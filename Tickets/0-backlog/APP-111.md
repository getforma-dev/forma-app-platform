## Why

Victor, 2026-10-30: "We also want the AI to explain the context … like a brief … maybe a pro plan can have users drop huge pages and it creates an app from it as a brief and picker and organized to share with metrics and tables." APP-108 ships the small version (≤ 10-page PDFs, at most 3 files per call). This ticket is the paid tier for large documents.

## What changes

1. **Ingestion.** Large PDFs, DOCX and long CSV/XLSX are chunked by page or section. Each chunk is processed once into typed facts:
   - statements with page citations;
   - candidate tables with their page range;
   - key figures with units.

   Results are stored as immutable derived artifacts linked to the box file's digest.
2. **Retrieval.** Each brief or app request pulls only the relevant chunks from a per-box index: vector plus keyword. That's the vector-native engine applied to documents, and it keeps every model call bounded no matter how big the box is.
3. **Brief at scale.** The brief has an organized outline (sections, key points, risks, open questions). Every statement carries a verified citation (file, page). Numbers come either from governed data (receipts) or as cited quotes labeled "from document".
4. **App from the brief.** The model proposes a typed app:
   - metrics (KPI cards) and tables;
   - a picker to filter by document, section or period;
   - the brief card.

   It goes through admission as always. Candidate tables extracted from pages are labeled "extracted from p. N". A member's one-click confirmation promotes a table to a governed source revision with a receipt that traces to the page.
5. **Plan limits.** Pro gets larger page and file caps, more brief and app generations per month, and priority processing. The free tier stays at APP-108's caps. Usage is metered per organization, and model spend goes into the budget ledger.
6. **Sharing.** Sharing a brief app shares the cited pages it uses, not the whole box.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] A 200-page PDF and two large spreadsheets produce a brief whose every statement cites a real page (spot-checked), with model calls bounded in tokens per request.
- [ ] Promoting an extracted table creates a source revision. Its numbers carry receipts linking to the page.
- [ ] Free-tier caps refuse a large document with an upgrade message. Pro caps allow it. Usage is metered.
- [ ] A shared brief app gives the teammate only the cited pages.

## Verification

An evaluation set of real-format synthetic documents; citation precision measured; cost per brief recorded.

## Migration and rollback

Additive tables for chunks, facts and the index. Feature-flagged per plan.
