## Why

The managed workflow must remain bounded, recoverable and auditable under real failure conditions before it handles customer data. This ticket delivers: control support access and emergency administrative operations.

## What changes

Deliver the dedicated follow-on scope below, retaining the established typed interfaces and current authorization. Normal operator access is just in time and limited to health/quotas/jobs; customer data requires the separately authorized scoped time-limited support session. Breakglass access is sealed, audited and observable by an independent reader; expired or revoked elevated sessions cannot continue. Destructive administrative actions follow the recorded governance policy: test the second-person path, or explicitly carry forward platform AR1’s one-operator fallback with the 24-hour notice/hold and audited reason. Record the independent breakglass reader, or the documented head-alert fallback until one is named. No path bypasses organization deletion hold/cancellation.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes unapproved paid provisioning or real-data processing; completing preparation does not itself authorize either. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.
