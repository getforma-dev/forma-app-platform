## Why

The platform needs a usable spatial workspace and an equivalent accessible path over the same saved work. This ticket delivers: link views by exact context and filter epoch.

## What changes

Six-view refresh shares state; loading/empty/error/denied/stale/partial distinguished; late prior-filter/work/organization responses discarded; receipt inspector and units/denominator visible.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes production deployment and real customer data; this is a synthetic qualification. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Selecting a destination updates all linked views from the same context/filter epoch, with visible scope, units and denominator.
- [ ] Delay an old filter, work or organization response until after a switch; no prior-context payload appears in the new view.
- [ ] Clear the filter and restore exact full-scope totals; loading, empty, error, denied, stale and partial states remain distinguishable with a receipt inspector.
