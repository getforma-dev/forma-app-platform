## Why

The managed workflow must remain bounded, recoverable and auditable under real failure conditions before it handles customer data. This ticket delivers: prove synthetic restart, restore, deletion and quota recovery.

## What changes

Complete the reduced synthetic operational path before the working-demo gate. Back up and restore real persisted app, source, work, record, receipt and audit relationships into an isolated environment; apply bounded retention/deletion and fair quota controls. Fixture reseeding is not restoration. Implement the demo-required insert-only audit chains and Object Lock anchors in this slice; later APP-062 expands their managed operations and monitoring. Demo §§10.4 TH7/M13–M14 and 10.7 require these controls before the demonstration.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes production deployment and real customer data; this is a synthetic qualification. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Verify insert-only chained audit, preserve the chain across reset, anchor each chain head to Object Lock every 15 minutes, and verify the restored chain against retained anchors. Record pre-rehearsal snapshot identity, anchor identities and the timed isolated restore; audit/anchor loss cannot be replaced by reseeding.
- [ ] Back up actual persisted synthetic app/work/source/record/receipt/audit relationships, restore into isolation and compare identities/digests at a declared recovery point; do not reseed fixtures.
- [ ] Apply synthetic retention/deletion and restore the older backup; tombstones/deletion ledger prevent deleted originals, normalized data, derived work, preview/export/cache contents from becoming available again.
- [ ] Exhaust one organization’s import/generation/model allowance and cancel/crash work; visible outcomes remain correct and the other tenant continues, with restart preserving each committed record and idempotency outcome.

- [ ] Restore/reset/cleanup cannot revive a revoked or expired invitation/entitlement or reset consumed trial allowance. Prove bounded abandoned-signup/reservation retention, reconciled outstanding usage and admission recovery without cross-tenant disclosure.
