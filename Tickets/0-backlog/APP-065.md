## Why

The managed workflow must remain bounded, recoverable and auditable under real failure conditions before it handles customer data. This ticket delivers: execute reversible deletion holds and complete organization erasure.

## What changes

Deliver the dedicated follow-on scope below, retaining the established typed interfaces and current authorization. Organization deletion starts a visible 24-hour hold, notifies administrators, allows cancellation and requires the approved second confirmer before irreversible work. After the hold, disable the organization key and schedule its deletion while tracking rows, originals, Parquet, derived content, caches/indexes and backup expiry under the adopted policy. Restore a pre-deletion backup into isolation and prove the deletion ledger/key state prevents served data before environment readiness; retained tombstones contain no deleted payload.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes unapproved paid provisioning or real-data processing; completing preparation does not itself authorize either. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.
