## Why

The managed plan's recovery drill, two-order race proofs with real RDS row locks and drift/replica refusals prove operational safety the synthetic beta does not need on day one but a real organization does. [ADR-008](../../docs/architecture/ADR-008-YC-DEMO-AND-BETA-MVP.md) moved them here from APP-076/077/078: sentences marked "Moved" are verbatim; split, reworded or new text is labelled as such and named in ADR-008 §5.

## What changes

Build and prove the two-process race coordinator (run-scoped advisory beacons plus `pg_blocking_pids`/`pg_locks` observations) locally, then run it on RDS; take and restore the pre-migration snapshot to an exact temporary identifier and compare manifests; run the drift, unsafe-defaults, replica-mode and unauthorized-`SET` refusals on a reviewed target; run the pool-lifecycle cancellation matrix on RDS. Follow [managed-plan.md](../../evidence/APP-008/managed-plan.md) steps 3 and 5 and §Required matrix rows for pool lifecycle, concurrency and drift, and [managed-harness-preparation](../../evidence/APP-008/managed-harness-preparation.md) slice 3's coordinator proposal.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] On disposable local PostgreSQL 16.13 with TLS, the coordinator proves real blocking relationships for both orders of membership revocation, organization suspension and saved-work version change (application first completes only under its held fence; writer first causes a current-state or stale-pin refusal after waiting), monotonic epoch restoration (operator mutations advance epochs, never reset them) and peer-loss cleanup; a sleep or process start is never the barrier. (Moved from APP-077 by ADR-008.) Build it as the preparation record proposes: run-scoped session advisory-lock phase beacons plus `pg_blocking_pids`/`pg_locks` observations, the application actor reserving one workspace lease, the operator actor using two migration connections (writer and observer), never extending the three-second application transaction, and aborting on beacon loss, unexpected owner or timeout.
- [ ] The pre-migration snapshot is created, restored to an exact temporary private identifier and compared before any restored migration or reseed. (Moved from APP-078 by ADR-008.) A reviewed cutover or forward-fix procedure is recorded. (New by ADR-008.)
- [ ] Pool lifecycle on RDS with private backend PIDs and public equality/difference assertions: same-backend reuse after success and error rollback across organizations with no prior scope or metadata and cleared role, settings, listeners and advisory locks; cancellation during connect, recycle, begin, query, finish and reset; ambiguous commit; cleanup timeout; credential generation and expiry; capacity and waiter limits; transport destruction; poisoned session context. Successful cleanup reuses; uncertain cleanup replaces the backend without business retry. (Moved from APP-078 by ADR-008.)
- [ ] Concurrency with real row locks and observed wait states: both orders of membership revocation, organization suspension and saved-work version change, holding organization before member then work; the application operation first completes only under its held fence; the writer first causes a current-state or stale-pin refusal after waiting; operator mutations advance epochs rather than resetting them. (Moved from APP-078 by ADR-008.) Both revocation/adoption-versus-commit orders, concurrent last-slot redemption, identical retry and code/entitlement revocation preserve their declared transaction boundaries on RDS with real lock observations; a late response or existing session cannot revive expired/revoked entitlement. (Moved from APP-076 by ADR-008.)
- [ ] Drift and readiness: canonical drift, unsafe all-role defaults, replica mode and unauthorized parameter `SET` refuse; catalog mismatch precedes any unsafe metadata-view evaluation; clean state is restored and readiness recovers. (Moved from APP-078 by ADR-008.) The drift-trial target is chosen before the window (a temporary restored instance with reviewed `rds-db:connect` bindings, or a recorded limitation). (Reworded from APP-078 item 1 by ADR-008.) If a required managed administrator operation is unavailable, record that limitation and obtain an equivalent reviewed real-RDS trial; do not silently replace it with a local result. (Copied from APP-078 item 1 by ADR-008; APP-078 keeps it.)
- [ ] An independent review binds the coordinator evidence and the exact binaries with no blocking finding; the preparation record's coordinator items (cross-process timing under the transaction deadlines, lock visibility under the approved RDS roles, the drift-trial target) are each answered or recorded as a limitation for the PH05 gate record. (Split from APP-077 by ADR-008.)
