## Why

The managed workflow must remain bounded, recoverable and auditable under real failure conditions before it handles customer data. This ticket delivers: publish operator/run/release/rollback and incident runbooks.

## What changes

Named ownership/support, alerts/escalation, data-access and deletion procedure, recovery evidence freshness, client/version compatibility, key/provider rotation, cost/budget and known limitations; no unsupported contractual availability promise.

Keep this a reviewable slice: M means approximately 1–3 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes unapproved paid provisioning or real-data processing; completing preparation does not itself authorize either. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Named operator/support ownership and escalation cover measured alerts, incidents, degraded provider/key/store conditions, access requests and recovery.
- [ ] Runbooks identify exact release/rollback, compatible client/assets, migration, key/provider rotation, backup/restore and deletion procedures with recent exercised evidence.
- [ ] Publish actual measured limits, cost/budget policy and known limitations without a contractual availability claim unsupported by hosting/support/failure evidence.

## Verification

Use persisted operational state, actual resource/failure or restore drills, immutable identifiers and measured outcomes rather than fixture reseeding. Record the exact commands, environment, pins, expected/actual results and remaining limits in this ticket’s Log and `evidence/APP-071/` when implementation runs. The application harness is established by APP-007; do not invent a successful command or substitute the planning-board check for runtime evidence.

Prepare and exercise rollback on isolated synthetic state before live use. Retain backups, audit and deletion ledger; irreversible key deletion requires its explicit hold/confirmation process and cannot be undone by deployment rollback.
