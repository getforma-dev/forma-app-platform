## Why

The running two-app journey and its evidence must establish the capability; a staged recording or hardcoded second dashboard cannot. This ticket delivers: package reproducible synthetic sources and expected outcomes.

## What changes

Create a portable synthetic fixture package for Northstar, Spend Review and the isolation canary, with original CSV and bounded XLSX inputs, expected arithmetic and deterministic reset instructions. Preserve provenance and label every seeded object; no real customer data.

Keep this a reviewable slice: M means approximately 1–3 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes production deployment and real customer data; this is a synthetic qualification. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.
