## Why

The managed workflow must remain bounded, recoverable and auditable under real failure conditions before it handles customer data. This ticket delivers: prepare immutable role-separated hosted infrastructure.

## What changes

Prepare the chosen hosted topology, network/store/key/secret configuration, role IAM/database grants, constrained egress, readiness and rollback without implicitly provisioning paid infrastructure. A separate ticket implements signed service-call identity.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes unapproved paid provisioning or real-data processing; completing preparation does not itself authorize either. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Prepare versioned edge/network/store/key/secret/four-role deployment configuration for the chosen environment without implicit paid provisioning.
- [ ] IAM/database grants and egress separate workspace/control/data/worker responsibilities; object prefixes/keys and constrained query/parser environments preserve tenant boundaries.
- [ ] Compare in-process and deployed-service receipt results for the canary and document ordered cold start, readiness and rollback with soft dependencies correctly classified.
