## Why

A reviewer must control exactly what ships, while saved work retains its meaning and current access. This ticket delivers: commit exact publications atomically under current policy.

## What changes

Deliver the dedicated follow-on scope below, retaining the established typed interfaces and current authorization. One SERIALIZABLE transaction verifies current digest, policy version, approver authority/epoch and author relationship, then writes publication, compatibility/adoption rows, audit and pointer. Race draft edits, policy change, reviewer revocation and publication retries; stale or replayed approvals cannot publish changed bytes or create duplicate effects. Only the control role writes publications; exact input-contract/operator/renderer/record-schema bindings survive reload and previous allowed publications remain usable during draft failure.
