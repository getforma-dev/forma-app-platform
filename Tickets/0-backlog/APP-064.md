## Why

The managed workflow must remain bounded, recoverable and auditable under real failure conditions before it handles customer data. This ticket delivers: apply complete retention and source deletion policy.

## What changes

Implement adopted retention horizons across every original/normalized/record/derived/cache/preview/export/history copy, source/space soft-delete and undo, minimal tombstones and deletion-ledger replay during restore. Organization-wide irreversible key deletion is the following dedicated ticket.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes unapproved paid provisioning or real-data processing; completing preparation does not itself authorize either. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Configured source/record/history/preview/export/index/cache/audit/idempotency retention horizons are explicit and applied to every copy under the chosen data policy.
- [ ] Source/space deletion uses its defined soft-delete/undo period and tombstones retain identifiers/digests without removed sensitive payload.
- [ ] Restore a synthetic retained/deleted dataset and prove deletion-ledger replay plus cache invalidation occurs before serving; after retry-outcome expiry use reconciliation rather than pretending an old key is safe.
