## Decision

The rules are in [docs/pitch/CANVAS-RULES.md](../../docs/pitch/CANVAS-RULES.md) ("Talking to things").

- **The address is the scope; the words go to the AI.** This holds for `/forma` or a plain sentence (the canvas), `/<app>`, `/<app>.<card>`, `/<folder>` and `/<widget>`. With something selected, a plain sentence goes to the selection, and the bar shows a removable chip.
- **Three layers, separated by what changes:**
  - Records change only through the app's own commands, or through formation into a new app; both are confirmed.
  - Cards are views. They store no data and are computed live, with receipts.
  - Answers are read-only.
- **Four typed outcomes**, chosen by the AI and shown before they take effect:
  - **Answer:** immediate, with receipts, and **Make it a card**.
  - **New view:** a preview with **Add as new card** (default) or **Replace this card** (labeled *adapted*, with **Revert**).
  - **Command:** the existing confirm card.
  - **Forma:** a new card set, or an app from a folder.
- **Switching:** chips switch the outcome when the guess is wrong. A leading `?` forces Answer.
- **Ownership:**
  - A card made from an app's data belongs to that app, marked "made by you", and is personal until the owner adds it for everyone.
  - Widgets only restyle.
  - Cards that combine two apps are a new app: formation, deferred.

## What changes

1. **The scope resolver.** It turns an address or the selection into a scope (canvas, app, card, folder or widget), using the APP-120 registry and the APP-121 labels and aliases.
2. **The outcome contract.** It is one typed union, `answer | view | command | forma`, validated server-side.
   - A `view` is a card spec over the scope app's records (the APP-101 transform mechanism, generalized to card scope).
   - A `command` must name a command the app defines.
   - The model never returns code, SQL, markup or grants.
3. **The answer surface.** A popover attached to the item, with receipts and **Make it a card**.
4. **The view preview.** Add as new card or replace; the *adapted* label and Revert; personal "made by you" cards stored per member (an additive migration under RLS and FORCE RLS).
5. **Outcome chips and `?`.** The chips re-ask with a forced outcome; `?` forces Answer.
6. **The verbs.** `/forma <words>` joins the registry (Make group). `/folder <name> formation …` stays as an alias of `/<folder> …`.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] `/expenses.total add the math for me` previews a new card with a computed figure. Its receipts trace to the source records, and no records are written.
- [ ] `/expenses why did spend jump in March?` returns an Answer with receipts and changes nothing. **Make it a card** pins it.
- [ ] **Replace this card** marks the card *adapted*; **Revert** restores it.
- [ ] `/expenses.total ? make it a pie chart` can only answer.
- [ ] A Command outcome naming a command the app does not define is refused server-side.
- [ ] A "made by you" card is invisible to other members, and a foreign organization gets the uniform 404.
- [ ] With a card selected, a plain sentence shows the "to: …" chip and is scoped to that card. Removing the chip scopes it to the canvas.

## Verification

Unit and campaign tests (Docker); a browser walk on the dev stand.

## Migration and rollback

An additive migration (personal cards). Without it, Answer and Command still work; New view falls back to session-only cards.
