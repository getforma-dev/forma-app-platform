## Decision

Victor, 2026-10-31, superseding the first draft (one AI entry with four guessed outcomes): "I want a clear separation from changing the look and theme of the app and cards to answering questions about the data. I'm scared a question can be like 'where are all the red so and so' and the agent will make the card red. There are two jobs."

The rules are in [docs/pitch/CANVAS-RULES.md](../../docs/pitch/CANVAS-RULES.md) ("Two jobs: Ask and Shape").

- **Where you type decides the job; the AI never chooses.**
  - **Ask:** the Ask tab, or `?` in the bar. A read-only conversation, answered in text with receipts.
  - **Shape:** `/<card or widget> <words>`, `/shape …`, or the Shape field. Changes look or view through a preview and **Apply**. It never answers in words.
- **Structural, not prompt-based.** These are two endpoints with two output schemas.
  - Ask returns only `{text, receipts, references}`. It runs in a read-only transaction and has no write path.
  - Shape returns only a view spec, applied by the client after the user clicks.
- **The overlap is two explicit buttons.** **Make it a card** on an answer opens a Shape preview. **Ask about this** on a card opens a scoped Ask.
- **The rest of the bar is unchanged.**
  - Do: `/<app> <words>` runs the app's commands, behind a confirm card.
  - Forma: a plain sentence or `/forma` picks or builds an app; folder formation builds from files.
- **Layers.**
  - Records change only through Do or Forma, always confirmed.
  - Cards and widgets change only through Shape: *adapted*, with **Revert**.
  - Answers change nothing.
- **Ownership.**
  - A card made by Shape from an app's data belongs to that app, marked "made by you", and is personal until the owner adds it for everyone.
  - A card that combines two apps is Forma, deferred.

## What changes

1. **Ask endpoint and tab.**
   - `POST /v1/ask` takes `{question, scope}` and returns text, receipts and record links.
   - It runs in a read-only database transaction; the tools it gets are read-only queries over the scoped app's records.
   - The pane gets an Ask tab with its own input and thread. `?` as the bar's first character opens it with the text.
   - Scope: the selection, an `@` mention, or the canvas. A removable chip shows which.
2. **Shape endpoint.**
   - `POST /v1/shape` takes `{address, words}` and returns a view spec: chart type, colors, columns, filters, computed fields over the app's records. This generalizes the APP-101 transform to cards and widgets.
   - The schema has no field for prose or records.
   - The client previews the result; **Apply** replaces the card or **Add as new card**. Then the *adapted* label and **Revert**.
3. **Bridges.** **Make it a card** (Ask → Shape preview) and ⋯ → **Ask about this** (card → scoped Ask).
4. **Personal cards.** "Made by you" cards are stored per member (an additive migration under RLS and FORCE RLS).
5. **Verbs.** `/shape` and `/forma` join the registry. `/<card or widget> <words>` means Shape. `/folder <name> formation …` stays as an alias of `/<folder> …`.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] In Ask, "where are all the red invoices?" lists them with receipts. No card, view or record changes: the canvas state and database row counts are identical before and after.
- [ ] The Ask endpoint cannot write. Its transaction is read-only, and a forced write attempt fails.
- [ ] `/expenses.ledger highlight overdue in red` previews a highlighted ledger. Nothing changes until **Apply**; **Revert** restores it. No records are written.
- [ ] Shape's response schema rejects a prose answer, and Ask's rejects a view spec (validated server-side).
- [ ] **Make it a card** from an answer opens a Shape preview; nothing is added without a click.
- [ ] `/expenses.total by month` adds a computed card whose receipts trace to the source records.
- [ ] A "made by you" card is invisible to other members, and a foreign organization gets the uniform 404 on ask, shape and personal-card routes.

## Verification

Unit and campaign tests (Docker); a browser walk on the dev stand.

## Migration and rollback

An additive migration (personal cards). Without it, Ask still works, and Shape falls back to session-only cards.
