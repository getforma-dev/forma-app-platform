## Why

Continuing shared work and starting independent work require separate grants, current source access and explicit data audiences. This ticket delivers: protect source-dependent saved and derived information.

## What changes

Deliver the dedicated follow-on scope below, retaining the established typed interfaces and current authorization. Saved findings, notes, narratives, derived records and retained previews carry relevant source dependencies as information restrictions rather than citation-only metadata. Revoke a source grant while work access remains: raw and derived disclosures are denied/redacted with a reason, including record history, cache and shared preview. Opening shared work or exporting never declassifies inaccessible source content; a recipient with all required grants sees the supported result without copying credentials or authority.
