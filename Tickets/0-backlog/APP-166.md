## Why

Shape can recolor an app, but the colors are three different greens and a handful of overrides on `--cv-*`. The platform itself is the getforma.dev paper and lime palette, written as raw custom properties on `:root`. There is no named theme a subtree can wear. DaisyUI shows the useful mechanism, nested `data-theme`, and also a Tailwind component system this app cannot take. Native pages ship no JavaScript, the content-security policy serves styles from this origin, and a model must not emit class names or CSS.

## What changes

Add one role sheet. `site` and `site-dark` hold the current paper, charcoal and lime values and remain the default: `:root` is site, and a dark operating system selects site-dark only when no `data-theme` is set. `ink` and `ink-dark` sit beside them as a slate and teal pair. Nothing applies `ink` yet.

Existing `--cv-*` and the native `--bg`, `--fg`, `--accent`, `--border` and `--bad` aliases point at the roles. Component rules for the mark, selection and the intent button stop hardcoding the lime and charcoal hexes. The vendored board keeps its four aliases.

Shape's closed `mode`, `accent` and `density` lists stay the API. Apply sets `data-theme` on the unite frame and the cards of that saved work. Accent `default` with a mode selects site or site-dark. An accent follows the operating system unless the mode is explicit. Highlights use the same mark hex as that accent. `comfortable` gets a spacing rule. A published composed app (`data-app-mode`) is left to APP-157.

Widget skins stay structure. A widget with accent `default` inside an accent theme takes that theme's glow, or the dark ink when the skin is paper or retro. An explicit widget accent still wins. `site` and `ink` do not recolor a widget's own kind color.

No admission change, no grant change, no server-stored session theme, and no model-authored CSS.

## Migration and rollback

No schema or authority change. Removing the role sheet and the `data-theme` attribute restores the previous presentation. Saved works, receipts and published appearance are unchanged. Session themes still disappear on reload.
