## Why

At 26 open pages in the release build, the only criterion the refresh herd missed was first-try 200 (83–91%).

- **The cause:** almost all the misses are 429s from the record snapshot cap. `app/src/storage/record_query.rs` allows four full snapshots at once (`Semaphore::new(4)`, `try_acquire_owned` then `Error::Busy`). The answer is a 429 with a 2 s `Retry-After`, and the card shows "Busy. Trying again in a moment…".
- **Where the 4 came from:** APP-168 (`6f7f7d39`) introduced it, and only a code comment names it. `docs/architecture/CSV-APP-DATA.md` says only "a concurrency ceiling".

## What changes

- A named constant replaces `Semaphore::new(4)`, derived from the query worker's `TASK_RUNNING` (4) plus `QUEUE` (20), which is 24. One organization's views are then refused first by the worker's frozen admission (D7.10), never by this cap. `try_acquire` and the 429 stay.
- The comment in `record_query.rs` and the message in `storage/mod.rs` follow.
- `docs/architecture/CSV-APP-DATA.md` gains a dated line naming the ceiling, its derivation, and the worst case: 24 × the 64 MiB per-snapshot bound, about 1.5 GiB in the 8 GiB task. The data pool still bounds database reads.
