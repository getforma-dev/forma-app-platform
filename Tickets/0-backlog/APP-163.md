## Why

A person may belong to several workspaces and not remember their names or URLs. Typing an email must not expose their memberships to strangers.

## What changes

Find my workspaces verifies mailbox control before returning eligible workspace destinations. Use a bounded, authoritative discovery mapping with explicit verification/email-change and membership lifecycle rules. A short-lived single-use code/link, generic pre-verification responses, delivery/rate limits and replay protections guard discovery. Opening a result still requires the destination's tenant authentication and current membership; discovering a link never creates authority. Keep member invitations, accepted memberships and app-only shares distinct. APP-165 owns actual app-owned invitation delivery and workspace-aware message content; preserve those destinations through discovery and do not send unrequested invitations while testing.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Unknown/known email requests reveal no memberships before proof and have reviewed status/timing/delivery-abuse behavior.
- [ ] Expired, replayed, brute-forced and cross-browser/cross-purpose proofs refuse; no credentials/codes appear in logs or evidence.
- [ ] One/multiple/zero eligible workspace results follow authoritative current mapping; email changes, revocation and disabled workspaces remove stale results.
- [ ] Tenant authentication, SSO requirements and current membership still apply; email matches do not link accounts or grant membership.
- [ ] Invitation and shared-app destinations survive discovery with their original acceptance semantics and clear email copy.
- [ ] Independent security review and genuine delivery/browser tests pass, including provider outage and recovery.

## Verification

Inspect the provider's actual supported verification and invitation contracts first. Qualify actual persistence, proof lifecycle, neutral pre-verification responses and cross-tenant negatives. Record real hosted mail delivery separately from synthetic qualification.

## Migration and rollback

Review a minimal discovery mapping and retention policy before adding schema. Disabling discovery leaves APP-162's workspace links available and changes no membership, credential or existing trial data.
