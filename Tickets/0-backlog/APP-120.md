## Why

Victor, 2026-10-31: "we have apps and each app can have a card, so cards belong to apps … widgets are one-off … is a folder a widget too? … we need clear relationships and boundaries … pick a mental model and go with it." A Fable design decision (2026-10-31) settled it.

The model in one line: **the canvas holds things; apps are the only governed thing; a slash verb does something, a slash name talks to an app.**

## What changes

1. **Objects.** There are three kinds.
   - **App** (governed). It contains **cards**, its views. A card never exists without its app and is addressed `app.card` (`expense.ledger`) or by pane number (`3.2`).
   - **Widget** (ad hoc, per member): Weather, Calculator, Clock, **Note**, **Folder**.
     - A folder holds files. Its one power, `formation`, produces an app, and that app links back to it.
     - A note keeps its private storage but is a widget to the member.
   - **Arrangement** (layout only): stack, unite frame, focus. Arrangements are never listed in the Library.
   - "Utilities" is renamed **Widgets** everywhere.
2. **Right pane: Library | Commands | Shortcuts.**
   - Library has two sections, Apps (categories → apps → cards) and Widgets (five rows; the Folder row expands to the member's folders).
   - Each row shows an on-canvas dot (filled or hollow), "n of m cards" for apps, an Add/Remove toggle, and its `/name` in grey.
   - The pane is a pure projection of the board through one `canvas:changed` event.
3. **Grammar.** One sentence to learn: "Type `/` and a verb to change the canvas, `/` and an app's name to tell that app something, or just a sentence."
   - **Verbs** (12, grouped):
     - Place: `/app`, `/card`, `/widget <kind>`.
     - Arrange: `/arrange`, `/unite`, `/split`, `/focus`.
     - Make: `/folder <name> formation <instruction>`.
     - Help: `/apps [query]`, `/commands`, `/shortcuts`, `/agent`.
   - **There is no `/remove`.** Removal is the card's ×, Delete on a selection, or the Library's Remove toggle (as in Figma and Miro).
   - **`/<app-slug> <instruction>` stays** (the `/giphy cats` pattern). A bare `/<app-slug>` places the app, the same as `/app <slug>`.
   - `/` alone shows the 12 verbs. From the first letter, the dropdown adds the Apps section.
   - `?` opens Shortcuts.
4. **Aliases.** These are accepted and autocompleted but hidden from the `/` menu, so nothing breaks:
   - `/weather`, `/calculator`, `/clock` and `/note` → `/widget <x>`;
   - `/folder` alone → the folder picker;
   - `/help` → `/commands`;
   - the numbers `/card 3.2` and `/app 3`.

   `/expense $86 Nobu, Sarah approves` behaves exactly as before.

## Acceptance tests

- [x] `/` lists the 12 verbs in four groups and no addresses. A first letter adds the Apps section. `?` opens Shortcuts.
- [x] `/card expense.ledger` and `/card 3.2` place the same card. `/widget weather` and `/weather` place the weather widget. A bare `/expense` places the Expense claims app. `/expense $86 …` files a claim as before.
- [x] Library → Widgets lists Weather, Calculator, Clock, Note and Folder. The Folder row expands to the member's folders. On-canvas dots and "n of m cards" update on every add and remove through `canvas:changed`.
- [x] Docs (GUIDE.md, SCENARIOS.md) teach the one-sentence grammar.
