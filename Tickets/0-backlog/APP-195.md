## Why

The APP-193 spike measured what happens when 25 members have a shared page open and its owner saves once.
- Every page refreshes within milliseconds, each refresh taking several data-role acquisitions.
- The data pool admits 8 callers per role (2 running and 6 waiting) and refuses the ninth at once (`storage/pool.rs`, `admitted`). Most refreshes therefore answer 503.
- The live-event delivery check runs on the same role. When it is refused, the next event's hint is dropped for the whole organization, until each page's 30-second poll.

This affects every shared app at this size, not only a quiz.

## What changes

**Client.** Refreshes triggered by a live hint start after a uniform random delay of 0–3 s:
- the document, the canvas board's live groups, the record components and `stream.lagged` re-reads;
- the page that committed the command skips the delay;
- a failed refresh (503 or network) retries once after a random 1–3 s before showing "Updates are unavailable";
- refreshes the member starts (the Refresh button, a visibility change) are unchanged; coming back `online` is treated like a hint (0–3 s, one retry), because every device on one network comes back at the same moment (review m6, 2026-11-14);
- a hint for a hidden page schedules nothing: the page is marked stale and reads once when it is shown again (the work document's own `visibilitychange` refresh; the canvas resumes the stale keys). The spread stays 0–3 s and the single retry stays.

**Server.** The bounded waiter set per role goes from 8 to 32: 2 running and 30 waiting, within the unchanged absolute 5 s acquisition deadline. The 33rd is still refused at once. This keeps ADR-004's rules (a bounded waiter queue and one absolute acquisition deadline, including queue), and ADR-007's frozen numbers (two connections per role, eight in all, the 5/3/2 s deadlines) are unchanged. The qualification expectation follows: 30 waiters, the 33rd refused.

**Campaign.** `refresh-herd` runs in the tenant-foundation workflow as a gate. The gate is 15 open pages, the measured sustainable load. The 26-page run is a recorded measurement under `GENUI_REFRESH_HERD_FULL=1 just campaign refresh-herd`. This was decided by the Fable advisor in lieu of Victor on 2026-11-14, after APP-197; the quarantine it replaces is removed.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] A new `refresh-herd` campaign, HTTP-level through the router on the disposable Postgres, in the tenant-foundation workflow:
  - **Setup:** 1 owner and 25 members, with an organization-wide contributor share. The page has a shared one-row list, an `own` list, a form, the record components and one typed view. *(As built, without W4: a per-member contributor share 25 times, and a second shared list in place of `own`; see the evidence.)*
  - **The CI gate: run A at 15 pages** (the owner and 14 members), jittered with one retry, three herds 10 s apart, in the test profile:
    - no terminal refresh failure;
    - at least 90% of refreshes answer 200 on the first try;
    - pages are current within 6 s at the 95th percentile, and every page within 10 s (revised 2026-11-14);
    - hint delivery reaches every page on each herd;
    - the owner's command completes under 1.5 s;
    - no `admission_saturated` for the command or the delivery read.
  - **And run C** (all pages at a barrier, no jitter, `admitted` at 8): reproduces the finding, at least 10 × 503.
  - **The 26-page run is a measurement**, recorded under `GENUI_REFRESH_HERD_FULL=1`. It covers runs A, B and C at 26 pages, plus the page-count and cadence searches.
  - **Reported:** the query worker's `Busy` count for the typed view, the shortest commit cadence at which run A still passes, the catalog verifications per run and the per-transaction p50.
- [x] Client unit tests: the jitter window, the committer skipping it, a single retry, user-started refreshes unchanged, and a hidden page scheduling nothing and reading once when shown (the visible committer unchanged).
- [x] Each new guard is broken and restored, with the record in `evidence/APP-195/`.
- [ ] An independent Tier A review of the pool change, and a review of the client change, find no blocker.
