## Why

The APP-193 spike measured what happens when 25 members have a shared page open and its owner saves once.
- Every page refreshes within milliseconds, each refresh taking several data-role acquisitions.
- The data pool admits 8 callers per role (2 running and 6 waiting) and refuses the ninth at once (`storage/pool.rs`, `admitted`). Most refreshes therefore answer 503.
- The live-event delivery check runs on the same role. When it is refused, the next event's hint is dropped for the whole organization, until each page's 30-second poll.

This affects every shared app at this size, not only a quiz.

## What changes

**Client.** Refreshes triggered by a live hint start after a uniform random delay of 0–3 s:
- the document, the canvas board's live groups, the record components and `stream.lagged` re-reads;
- the page that committed the command skips the delay;
- a failed refresh (503 or network) retries once after a random 1–3 s before showing "Updates are unavailable";
- refreshes the member starts (the Refresh button, a visibility change, `online`) are unchanged.

**Server.** The bounded waiter set per role goes from 8 to 32: 2 running and 30 waiting, within the unchanged absolute 5 s acquisition deadline. The 33rd is still refused at once. This keeps ADR-004's rules (a bounded waiter queue and one absolute acquisition deadline, including queue), and ADR-007's frozen numbers (two connections per role, eight in all, the 5/3/2 s deadlines) are unchanged. The qualification expectation follows: 30 waiters, the 33rd refused.

**Pre-approved fallback.** If the load test fails its criteria with both changes, raise the data role to 4 connections (10 in all), with an ADR-004 amendment recorded in `docs/architecture`, and the campaign constants follow.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] A new `refresh-herd` campaign, HTTP-level through the router on the disposable Postgres:
  - **Setup:** 1 owner and 25 members, with an organization-wide contributor share. The page has a shared one-row list, an `own` list, a form, the record components and one typed view.
  - **Run A** (jittered, one retry), three herds 10 s apart:
    - no terminal refresh failure;
    - at least 90% of refreshes answer 200 on the first try;
    - every page is current within 6 s;
    - hint delivery reaches 26/26 on each herd;
    - the owner's command completes under 1.5 s;
    - no `admission_saturated` for the command or the delivery read.
  - **Run B** (all 26 at a barrier, no jitter): measured only.
  - **Run C** (run B with `admitted` at 8): reproduces the finding, at least 10 × 503.
  - **Reported:** the query worker's `Busy` count for the typed view, and the shortest commit cadence at which run A still passes.
- [x] Client unit tests: the jitter window, the committer skipping it, a single retry, and user-started refreshes unchanged.
- [x] Each new guard is broken and restored, with the record in `evidence/APP-195/`.
- [ ] An independent Tier A review of the pool change, and a review of the client change, find no blocker.
