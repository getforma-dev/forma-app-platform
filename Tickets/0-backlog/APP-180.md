## Why

Under `independent_review`, the person who drafts a revision and the person who approves it should be different people. Today nothing records or checks that:

- `publish_once` always writes the app's owner as `author_id` (`app/src/storage/definitions.rs`, the publication insert near line 1213), and `genui.definition_revisions` records no drafter.
- The operator can grant both `edit_definition` and `review_publish` on one app to one member (`app/runtime/admission_operator.py`, `GRANTABLE`, line 169).

So a non-owner who holds both grants can draft a revision and approve it, and the publication names the owner as its author. Separation of duties rests only on the operator never granting both to one member. This was present before APP-085. The APP-085 review recorded it and did not grade it against APP-085's boxes.

## What changes

To be designed in this ticket. Choose one approach and record it as an amendment to the owning ADR (the ADR-007 publication rules):

- record the drafter on the definition revision and refuse an approval by the same principal;
- refuse to grant `review_publish` to a member who holds `edit_definition` on the same app (operator side, D7.3);
- or both.

Keep owner publication (`owner_publish`) unchanged.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] The ADR records the chosen rule, and why the alternative was not taken.
- [ ] A principal who drafted a revision cannot approve it under `independent_review`. The test fails with the rule removed.
- [ ] The publication's recorded author is the principal who drafted the revision, not always the owner. Older publications keep their recorded author, and the migration, if any, says so.
- [ ] An independent review of the change (Tier A if it touches grants, publication or a migration) finds no blocker.

## Verification

The definition and composer campaigns, plus the new test with its rule removed once. If the operator grant path changes, the operator's Python suite runs too.

## Migration and rollback

A drafter column, if chosen, is additive and nullable for older revisions. Rollback follows the migration's reviewed window.
