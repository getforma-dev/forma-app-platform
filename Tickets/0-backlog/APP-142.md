## Why

Unite already assembles an app's cards into one frame, and it looks right, but the app and its cards still feel disconnected. Victor wants that united frame adopted as the app itself: a page the member can share, work inside, and resize. Focus is the view he wants to keep. Stacks stay an arrangement of cards. They are not the page.

## What changes

1. The united app is a page. Its cards are parts of that page, not a pile of peers. The existing Unite motion can bring them together. Polish of that frame is in this direction and comes after the page is the thing being shared, not before APP-141's creation step.
2. Focus opens one part for work, the way APP-106 already opens a card. Leaving focus returns to the page. Fan-out is a separate action that places the parts back on the board as cards.
3. The page can grow and shrink like a window. The size is layout for this member. It is not a new stored grant.
4. Sharing shares the page, the app. APP-139 still owns the case where the recipient has no account yet. This ticket does not redo that.
5. A stack remains what APP-104 and APP-120 say it is: cards piled so the member can leaf through them. Uniting is not stacking. Do not remove stacks.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Uniting an app opens it as one page. Its cards are parts of that page. Break-apart is fan-out: the parts return to the board and the page is gone.
- [ ] Focus from the page opens one part. Closing focus returns to the page, not to a scattered board, unless the member fans out.
- [ ] The page can be made larger and smaller. A reload keeps that size for this member in this browser.
- [ ] Sharing the page shares the app. A card that was only a part is not the thing that was shared.
- [ ] An existing stack still leaves, fans, and spreads. Unite does not turn a stack into the page by itself.

## Verification

Read `canvas/unite.ts` and `canvas/focus.ts` before changing them. Receipts and form drafts must survive focus and fan-out, as APP-106 already requires. One fresh-browser check on a worktree port. Ports 4001–4003 and 4010 stay untouched.

## Migration and rollback

No migration. Page size stays in the member's browser layout, with the placed set. Removing the page returns Unite to today's frame and break-apart.
