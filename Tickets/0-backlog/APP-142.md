## Why

Unite already gathers an app's cards into one frame (`canvas/unite.ts`, `/unite`, the U key). Focus already opens one card large (`canvas/focus.ts`, the F key, `#focus=`). Break-apart (`/split`, Shift+U) sends every card back to where it was. The frame looks like an app, but the cards still behave as separate board objects. Victor wants the united frame to be the app: a page. Focus stays. Stacks stay. The polish of that frame comes after the page is the thing the member works in and shares.

## What exists today

- Unite computes a layout from the app's declared views and commands: a header, a row of numbers, the main chart, the table, and the form or records. No model call. Cards keep their identity through the animation, so a receipt or a half-filled form survives. Cards of that app that are not yet on the board are brought in. The frame moves as one and is remembered in this browser (APP-104, DS-06, DS-67). It is not remembered on the server, and a new browser does not restore it.
- Focus is a modal over the board, about 80 percent of the viewport. Left and right move through that app's cards, or through the stack the card came from. Escape returns to the board with the card unmoved.
- A stack is a pile of cards the member leafs, fans, and spreads. It is an arrangement. It is not an app. APP-104 still owes hover-scrub and viewport-nearest loading. This ticket does not take those.

## What changes

1. Uniting an app opens its page. The parts are the cards, shown inside the page in the admitted order, not as peers scattered on the board. The existing fly-in may be the entrance. The page has the app's name and its retrieved, adapted, or generated label.
2. Focus, from the page, opens one part for work. Forms submit through the existing command route. Receipts stay visible. Closing focus returns to the page. It does not scatter the parts back onto the board.
3. Fan-out is a separate action, the successor of break-apart. It is the only action that puts the parts back on the board as cards and dismisses the page. Each part returns to the position it had before the page opened, when it had one.
4. The page can be dragged larger and smaller, like a window, down to a size that still shows the header and one part, and up to the viewport. The size is this member's layout in this browser. It is not a grant, not a tenant setting, and not written to the server.
5. Sharing from the page shares the app, the saved work, through the existing share path. It does not share one card. APP-139 owns the recipient who has no account yet. This ticket does not change `share_work`.
6. A stack is left alone. Uniting a stack does not happen. Uniting an app does not turn its page into a stack. Leaf, fan, and spread still work on a stack.

Polish of spacing, type, and motion inside the page is in this direction and is not the first slice. The first slice is the page, focus returning to it, fan-out, resize, and share of the app.

## What this ticket does not do

- It does not create the app. That is APP-141.
- It does not add the standalone HTML address. That is APP-144. This page is the on-canvas page.
- It does not remove folders, and it does not decide whether a card can leave the app and keep its data. Those questions are on APP-141.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Uniting an app opens it as one page. Its cards are parts of that page. Break-apart is fan-out: the parts return to the board and the page is gone.
- [ ] Focus from the page opens one part. Closing focus returns to the page, not to a scattered board, unless the member fans out.
- [ ] The page can be made larger and smaller. A reload keeps that size for this member in this browser.
- [ ] Sharing the page shares the app. A card that was only a part is not the thing that was shared.
- [ ] An existing stack still leaves, fans, and spreads. Unite does not turn a stack into the page by itself.

## Verification

Read `canvas/unite.ts` and `canvas/focus.ts` before changing them. Receipts and form drafts must survive focus and fan-out, as APP-106 already requires. One fresh-browser check on a worktree port. Ports 4001–4003 and 4010 stay untouched.

## Migration and rollback

No migration. Page size stays in the member's browser layout, with the placed set. Removing the page returns Unite to today's frame and break-apart.
