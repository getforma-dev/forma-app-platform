## Why

A reviewer must control exactly what ships, while saved work retains its meaning and current access. This ticket delivers: implement versioned publication policy and lifecycle rules.

## What changes

Define administrator-owned independent-review and low-risk owner-publish policies, lifecycle transitions and exact approval inputs. The following ticket owns the atomic publication transaction and concurrent stale-approval enforcement.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes production deployment and real customer data; this is a synthetic qualification. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] An administrator configures versioned independent-review and low-risk owner-publish policies with separate edit/review/publish/policy permissions.
- [ ] Authors and model tools cannot change policy or choose grants/review authority; owner publication is allowed only under the explicit authorized policy.
- [ ] Lifecycle rules expose Draft/In review/Published/Retired and define exact approval inputs, current publication pointer and previous-version availability without mutating records.
