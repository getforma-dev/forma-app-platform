## Why

Real members must agree to the beta notice before an account exists, and the agreement must be stored and audited. Today `/invite/validate` accepts exactly one field, and no consent is stored anywhere.

## What changes

Migration 0023 adds an insert-only `genui.member_consents`. The pending admission carries `notice_version` from `/invite/validate` through the fAuth round trip. `genui_private.admit_redemption` records the consent and appends the audit kind `admission.consented` in the same transaction. The invitation page shows notice version 1, and the form posts exactly `code` and `consent=1`. Anything else gets the neutral refusal.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] `/invite` shows notice version 1. A post without `consent`, with a wrong version or with any other extra field is refused neutrally before any work.
- [ ] A redemption stores one consent row with the version, and appends `admission.consented` in the same transaction as `admission.redeemed`. The admission audit chain verifies.
- [ ] Migration 0023 is additive and pinned (catalog v23, manifest 23). The invite campaign proves consent end to end, and removing the check fails it.
- [ ] An independent Tier A review finds no blocker.
