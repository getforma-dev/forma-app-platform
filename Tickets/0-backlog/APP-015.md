## Why

The managed workflow must remain bounded, recoverable and auditable under real failure conditions before it handles customer data. This ticket delivers: implement durable jobs, leases and cancellation.

## What changes

Transactional enqueue/claim with sequence fencing, lease renewal, bounded queue, per-org/global slots, visible states, idempotent creation; crash/reclaim refuses stale worker writes; cancellation semantics clear before/after commit.

Keep this a reviewable slice: L means approximately 3–5 focused days. Re-estimate before selection and split the ticket if it exceeds five focused days. The estimate is a planning bound, not a delivery date.

Scope excludes production deployment and real customer data; this is a synthetic qualification. Upstream framework/auth changes remain with the FormaStack team.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Enqueue and claim a durable job atomically with a finite lease and claim sequence; crash/reclaim followed by an old worker result is refused by the fence.
- [ ] Per-organization/global slots and queue caps admit bounded work, expose queued/running/failed/done states and return an actionable busy response without losing jobs.
- [ ] Cancel before irreversible work and after a recorded outcome; the UI reports stopped versus completed truthfully and frees slots without erasing committed history.
