## Why

The current single workspace session and routing hint do not provide a safe multi-workspace account switcher. A chooser must represent authenticated access, not infer it from identical email addresses.

## What changes

After deliberate authentication/linking, show authorized workspaces with a clear active workspace and switch action. Preserve each provider identity, membership, entitlement and authentication policy. Define multi-tab behavior, data/draft separation, expiry, logout-current/logout-all, revoked membership and returning to exact invitation/shared-app destinations. Shared-app-only accounts do not appear as workspace members. Readable names and restoring a retained tenant's served configuration remain APP-117 concerns.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] One/multiple-workspace chooser opens only a freshly authorized destination and requests its authentication when required.
- [ ] Matching emails with different issuer/tenant/subject identities do not silently link accounts or grant authority.
- [ ] Concurrent tabs, browser navigation, stale hints and switching cannot display or submit another workspace's data/drafts.
- [ ] Revocation, expiry, logout-current/logout-all and provider failure have qualified deterministic behavior.
- [ ] Membership invitations and app-only shares retain distinct destinations and permissions.
- [ ] Independent security review, genuine multi-workspace browser proof and mobile/keyboard acceptance are retained.

## Verification

Review the session model before adding durable state or cookies. Test real isolated tenants and distinct subjects, session races and current authority on every destination. Keep synthetic fixtures separate from genuine hosted account evidence.

## Migration and rollback

Any account linking is explicit, audited and reversible under a reviewed contract. No email-only merge or tenant recreation. Preserve the workspace-first path as recovery if the chooser is unavailable.
