## Why

Focus already opens one card large. The F key and the focus button do it, and the left and right keys walk the app's cards (`canvas/focus.ts`, APP-106). Victor wants the pointer to do the same by zooming: middle-click on the app zooms in to that focus, and Tab moves through the app's parts. One focus, not a new mode beside it.

## What changes

1. Middle-click on an app, or on one of its components, zooms the view into the existing focus for that component. The canvas behind is the same dimmed focus APP-106 already uses. Escape still leaves it. After APP-142, leaving focus returns to the app page rather than scattering the cards.
2. Tab moves to the next component of that same app. Shift-Tab moves to the previous one. The order is the admitted order, the same order the left and right keys already walk. Tab does not leave the app to a widget or to another app.
3. Middle-click on a widget does not open app focus. A widget has no components to tab through.
4. The zoom is a view change. It does not write a record, grant, or share.

This waits until the app page exists. It does not replace the F key.

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] Middle-click on an app's component opens the existing focus for that component. Escape closes it.
- [ ] Tab and Shift-Tab move only through that app's components, in admitted order.
- [ ] The F key and the arrow keys still do what APP-106 says.
- [ ] Middle-click on a weather, clock, or calculator widget does not open app focus.

## Verification

Read `canvas/focus.ts` before adding the pointer path. One fresh-browser check on a worktree port. Ports 4001–4003 and 4010 stay untouched.

## Migration and rollback

No migration. Removing the middle-click and the Tab binding leaves focus on the F key and the arrows.
