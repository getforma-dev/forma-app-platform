## Why

The pitch needs the canvas where the member already is: the signed-in workspace, with no organization picker.

## What changes

Mount the GenUI runtime canvas as an island on the workspace page, in the clone. The page is rendered under the session's tenant scope; the canvas receives only the data and definitions that scope authorizes; the organization comes from the session, never from the client. Measure the island (size, load) without gating on a budget (ADR-011 §4).

## Acceptance tests

- [ ] A signed-in member sees the canvas on the workspace page; an anonymous or non-member request gets the uniform 404 and no canvas assets.
- [ ] The canvas never offers or accepts an organization selection; the tenant is the session's.
- [ ] The island's measured size and load time are recorded as pitch debt for APP-097, not gated.
- [ ] Canvas content shows no value without its receipt where a governed number is displayed (see APP-094).

## Verification

Browser check in installed Chrome on the clone, plus the negative requests; sanitized screenshots and measurements only.

## Migration and rollback

Additive page change in the clone; removing the island restores the SSR workspace.
