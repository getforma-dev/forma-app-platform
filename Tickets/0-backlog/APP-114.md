## Why

Victor, 2026-10-30: "Create a ticket to make a landing page, and [the distinction between your agents, our AI and the Forma app] should be there, along with anything else you think." Investors, YC and the first pilot teams need one public page that explains Forma clearly and honestly, and collects interest.

## What changes

A landing page at getforma.dev. The existing site repo is `~/dev/Forma/getforma-site`, built with Forma Stack itself so the product is used on its own site. Sections:

1. **Hero.** One line ("Describe the app your team needs. Get it in seconds, safe by construction, shared and live."), the demo video, and the call to action "Join the beta".
2. **How it works.** Four steps: describe, then an app picked or built, then shared, then live. Visuals from the canvas: library pane, focus mode, Unite.
3. **Workflows teams run.** Expenses, time off, purchase requests (two-level approval), vendor invoices, access requests, deal desk, each with a one-line outcome.
4. **Folders and formation.** Drop files, `formation`, then an app plus a cited brief, with every number traced back to its file.
5. **Works where you work.** The Forma app (web and Slack), your agents (Claude, OpenAI, Cursor or your own, through MCP), and Forma's AI. Covers "bring your own agent", "use ours" and BYOK (enterprise, planned), copy taken from [docs/pitch/CONNECTIONS.md](../../docs/pitch/CONNECTIONS.md).
6. **Safe by construction.**
   - AI output is data, never code.
   - Database-enforced tenant isolation.
   - Receipts on every number.
   - Audited approvals.
   - Agents never exceed the person's rights.
   - Plain language, with links to a security page.
7. **Pricing (hypothesis, marked "early access").** Team, Pro and Enterprise, as in APP-110 and CONNECTIONS.md: included AI credits, then larger documents, then BYOK, SSO and audit export. No prices until pilots, or "contact us".
8. **Open source.** Forma Stack (MIT) and links to its docs, npm and crates.
9. **Beta list form.** Email, company and team size, stored with consent and no third-party trackers. A thank-you page. Data handling stated.
10. **FAQ.**
    - Is my data used to train models?
    - Which AI do you use?
    - Can I bring my own?
    - Where is data stored?
    - What does the demo show versus what is live?
11. **Footer.** Company, contact, privacy and terms pages (placeholders until counsel reviews them).

Constraints:
- No claim beyond what is built, following PITCH-SCRIPT.md Appendix B: no customer logos, no certifications, no speed rankings.
- Accessible (WCAG AA), fast (static where possible), strict CSP.
- Analytics privacy-preserving or none.
