### Item 1

- *Tenant-leading primary/foreign keys:* migration 0002 lines 21–210 put `organization_id` first in every primary, unique and foreign key. Case #61 (`qualify_tenant_schema.py:482–509`) asserts on the real database that all 26 tables are owned by `genui_owner` with RLS and FORCE, that the leading column of every index is `organization_id`, that both sides of every foreign key lead with `organization_id`, and that every policy has USING and WITH CHECK.
- *Reject cross-organization relationships:* #143 "real FK refuses foreign-only app" inserts work for org …0001 that references the Spend Review app and publication existing only in org …0002 and receives 23503; #144 "owner tenant policy refuses cross-organization write" receives 42501 from WITH CHECK; #142 shows the NOLOGIN owner is itself subject to FORCE RLS; #146–#149 show further composite-key and CHECK refusals.
- *Reject cross-organization reads under each application role:* #63, #82, #101 and #119 ("actual own-tenant positive and missing/foreign scope denial", `:523–530`) prove for workspace, control, data and worker that the scoped organization is visible and the other organization's ID returns zero rows. #64, #83, #102 and #120 ("RLS on every granted real relation", `:531–543`) prove, for every relation each role may read, that only the scoped organization's rows appear. Native lines 8–9 (`qualification_cases.rs:52–117`) repeat this through the production transaction API over TLS for all four pools.
- *Including a missing tenant context:* the same per-role cases read first with no scope (zero rows) and then with a blank organization setting on every granted relation (zero rows); native line 9 shows a reset lease without scope sees zero rows in every pool. Malformed scope fails closed with 22P02 (#79, #98, #117, #135) and `row_security=off` is refused (#78, #97, #116, #134). The canonical catalog is enforced at every admission, recycle and readiness (`catalog.rs:46–72`, `pool.rs:302–308`), and drift that disables RLS or FORCE or loosens a policy is refused (#2, #37–#39; native line 29).

- *Transaction-local scope resets:* scope is installed with `set_config(…, true)` inside `BEGIN ISOLATION LEVEL READ COMMITTED READ WRITE` (`authorization.rs:124–129`). `Lease::finish` commits or rolls back, then `reset` refuses replica mode, runs `DISCARD ALL` and reinstalls the blank baseline, and `verify_baseline` requires role `none`, `row_security=on`, `search_path=pg_catalog, pg_temp`, blank organization/principal/epoch and `origin` (`pool.rs:426–451, 500–558`). ## Limits

- Local only: PostgreSQL 16.13 in Docker with local trust/SCRAM authentication and a throwaway CA. RDS IAM/TLS, managed catalog compatibility, same-connection reuse, the two-order races and recovery on RDS are APP-075/077/078's; deployed identity and token traces are APP-074/076's.
- RLS is keyed to a transaction-local setting that an application role can itself change; isolation against arbitrary SQL from a compromised application role is not claimed (design AR2). The controls are the closed storage SQL surface and the non-constructible `TrustedRequest`.
- Timing side channels were not assessed.
- Item 3's write-denial verdict is bound to migration 0002's grants. Later write-grant migrations must re-prove it; the ADR-007 draft added at cff5d6f, pending its own review, keeps workspace without publication or record writes.
- `Lease::finish` reports a cleanup or configuration failure after a successful COMMIT as `Deadline`, `Unavailable` or `Configuration` (`pool.rs:426–451`; exercised by "two-second cleanup deadline discards uncertain reset"). This is harmless for these reads; a future write owner must treat those results as ambiguous outcomes, never as "not committed".
- The integration review's author also wrote the storage module, so it is not used here as independent evidence for storage behavior. The managed-entry draft's independent review is INCOMPLETE; the draft is cfg(test)-only, its single test is ignored, and it is evidence for no item.
- CI: the ticket records no CI for the acceptance source. a0a75a8 was pushed; its CI state was not queried by this reviewer. Done still requires exact-source CI at the final revision and landing; `tenant-foundation.yml` re-executes both campaigns there.
