## Contracts

Share: `POST /v1/work/{app}/{work}/share`, `Content-Type: application/json`, `Sec-Fetch-Site: same-origin` (or the exact `Origin`), the workspace session cookie; body `{"grantee_principal_id":"<uuid>","record_write":false}` (`record_write` optional, default false; no other member). 200 `{"app_id":"<uuid>","work_id":"<uuid>","grantee_principal_id":"<uuid>","granted":[{"scope":"app"|"work"|"source","resource_id":"<uuid>","permission":"discover"|"run"|"record_read"|"record_write"|"source_read"}, …]}` (forma-json-v1, `no-store`): the sharer's subset the teammate now holds, the same body whether this call inserted rows or found them (review m1). 403 `cross_site`; 404 `not_found` (uniform: no or ended session, a work the sharer cannot open, no authority to share, a `record_write` the sharer does not hold, a grantee who is the sharer, revoked, unknown or of another organization, a nonexistent work); 413 `payload_too_large`; 400 `malformed_share`; 503 `unavailable`. A share that inserted a row announces `app.shared`.

Members: `GET /v1/members` → 200 `{"members":[{"principal_id":"<uuid>","label":"Member <8 hex>"}, …]}` (the other active members of the caller's organization, at most 100; labels only), else the uniform 404.

Events: `GET /v1/events` with the session cookie → 200 `text/event-stream`, `Cache-Control: no-store`; frames `event: record.committed|app.shared|app.placed` / `data: {"kind":"<same>","app_id":"<uuid>","work_id":"<uuid>","at":"YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SSZ"}`, and `event: stream.lagged` / `data: {"kind":"stream.lagged","at":"…"}` after the stream dropped a hint (the client refetches what it shows); comment heartbeat `: heartbeat` every 15 s. The uniform 404 without a live session; 429 `too_many_requests` (`Retry-After: 2`) past the bounds. Every event is judged once for the organization (`Data::openable_principals`: current subscribers and which of them can open the work at that moment) and delivered only to a subscriber the judgment names as able to open it; an event of another organization is dropped before any lookup; a member the judgment or the 30 s re-check (`Data::member_current`, unfenced) finds no longer current (sign-out, operator revocation: any epoch change; lapsed entitlement) has its stream ended. A newer connection of the same session takes over the stream; the stream ends after one hour; the client reconnects.

Composer hook: `genui::events::hub().app_placed(organization, app, work)` after the saved work exists.

## Authorization rules as implemented

1. Sharer: a live workspace session (`resolve_session`), current membership at its epoch with a current entitlement, `record_read` on the work by the standard clause (unrevoked creator or `record_read` grant, `run` on the app, live `source_read` on every dependency), the `genui_lock` fence, then inside the definer: unrevoked creator **or** `share_work` on the work, and `run` on the app. `record_write` is shared only when requested and held (re-read after the fence and in the definer).
2. Grantee: an active membership in the **same** organization (the trusted request's; RLS scope), not the sharer.
3. Granted: the intersection of {`discover`, `run` on the app; `record_read`, optionally `record_write` on the work; `source_read` on the work's source dependencies} with what the sharer holds now, **copied** onto the teammate (the teammate then opens the work under those permissions; `source_read` is organization-wide and survives the share — ADR-011 amendment, review M3). Never `edit_definition`, `review_publish`, `share_work`, `export`, `admin_policy`; never a row in another organization. A grantee cannot pass grants on (no `share_work`, not the creator). Repeat shares write nothing, append no event and leave no record. Each share that wrote a row is recorded in `work_shares` with exactly the inserted set, referenced by its event.
4. Delivery: an event reaches a subscriber only if the organization-wide judgment made for that event names the subscriber as current and able to open the work at that moment (`discover` on the app and `record_read` on the work, the listing clause); ids of works a member cannot open never reach the wire; the judgment is one lock-free read per event, however many subscribers (review M2).

## Decisions in this slice (for review)

1. **Definer, not an application INSERT grant.** ADR-007 D7.1 makes `genui_admit` the authority writer; an INSERT grant on the permission tables to `genui_workspace` would have widened the grant set the schema campaign asserts literally. The definer keeps the application roles read-only on authority and computes the subset itself (D7.1: no permission names from callers, no bypass).
2. **`permission.granted` reused on the admission chain**, not a new kind: the operator `verify` already recomputes every chain generically and the operator channel's kind means the same thing; D7.8's closed list is amended by one RESTRICTIVE policy and recorded as debt.
3. **Owner-level = unrevoked creator or `share_work`.** No Phase 1 operator subcommand grants `share_work` (`GRANTABLE` excludes it); the campaign writes that row by fixture SQL as the operator stand-in.
4. **Epoch change closes the stream.** ADR-004 advances the epoch on every membership change (an operator revocation included), so a stream authorized at the earlier epoch ends at the next event and the client reconnects under a fresh session resolution; a sign-out ends it the same way. The judgment carries each current subscriber's epoch, so this needs no per-subscriber read.
5. **The hub is in-process** (ADR-007 D7.18 single task); the hook is a process-global `hub()` so the command route needed one line and no constructor change.
6. **One judgment per event, shared** (review M2). The announcement carries a once-cell; the first pump of the organization to reach it runs the one scoped read for every subscribed principal and the others read the same cell; a failed read drops the hint for everyone and closes nothing. The stream's reads take no `genui_lock` fence: they write nothing and must never make a command or an operator wait.
7. **The pump never waits on the client** (review M1). `try_send` into the 32-slot queue; a full queue drops the hint and marks the stream lagged, and the `stream.lagged` frame goes out as soon as there is room so the client re-reads what it shows. The same session's re-registration takes over the slot rather than adding a stream, so the per-member and the process bounds are bounds.
8. **Share semantics stated as built** (review M3): copied from the sharer; `source_read` organization-wide and surviving; every share recorded in `work_shares` with exactly the inserted set; no unshare route (debt).

## Limits

- Local password authentication over TLS, not RDS IAM; synthetic identities; sessions minted by the `cfg(test)` constructor after the seeded membership (the session campaign owns the fAuth path).
- No browser: the stream is read as a body stream through `tower::ServiceExt::oneshot`; the canvas (`pivot/canvas`) subscribes to `/v1/events` with `EventSource`.
- The members list shows neutral labels (no display name exists in 0002); the sharer picks a principal id.
- An open stream does not refresh the session's idle clock and a session's absolute expiry does not close it before the one-hour lifetime.
- No image was built; the image contexts were edited (allowlists) but not exported.
- Residual amplification (ADR-011 debt 6): one lock-free data-pool read per announced event per organization with a subscriber, plus one unfenced read per stream per 30 s.
- Independent review done (changes requested, fixed on `pivot/fix-095`); nothing merged.
