## Findings

### Major

**M1. The event stream's per-member bound is bypassable and its liveness guarantees (lifetime, epoch re-check, replacement) depend on the client reading.** `app/src/events/mod.rs:333` — `sink.send(event).await` inside the `select!` arm. When the per-connection queue (`SINK_CAPACITY` 32, `mod.rs:60`) is full because the SSE body is not being polled (a client that stopped reading; TCP backpressure), the pump parks inside that arm and no longer polls `lifetime` (`mod.rs:316`), `closed.notified()` (`:317`) or the re-check `ticker` (`:319`). Consequences: (a) the 1-hour lifetime and the 30 s `member_current` re-check do not run, so a signed-out or revoked member's stream is not closed as documented (`mod.rs:14-22`, evidence README "Events"); (b) `Registration` is never dropped, so `unregister` never runs, and (c) `Hub::register` (`mod.rs:216-219`) exempts a re-registration of the **same session** from `MAX_STREAMS_PER_MEMBER` (`replacing`), so one member with one cookie can register again and again while the earlier pumps are stalled, climbing past 4 to `MAX_STREAMS` (256) and answering 429 to every other member in the process. Exploit shape: insider with a session; opens N streams on the same cookie, does not read them, generates ~33 deliverable events on its own work (own commits produce `record.committed`) or enough bytes to close the receive window; each replacement bypasses the member bound. No data is disclosed; the realtime channel is denied until the sockets die or the task restarts.
Probe (scratch `app/src/events/probe_tests.rs`, both pass, i.e. both claims refuted): `probe_stalled_consumer_pins_the_pump_past_lifetime_recheck_and_replacement` — sink unread, 40 deliverable events, `current=false`, same-session re-register; the pump does not end within 2 s although lifetime 300 ms / re-check 50 ms / replacement have all elapsed, and `open_streams()==2`. `probe_replacement_bypasses_the_per_member_bound` — 256 `register(ORG, PRINCIPAL, "same-session")` all succeed; the next member gets `GlobalLimit`.
Fix (small): in `pump`, replace `sink.send(event).await` with `sink.try_send(event)`: `Err(TrySendError::Full(_)) => {}` (an event is a hint; the broadcast `Lagged` arm already accepts gaps), `Err(TrySendError::Closed(_)) => break`. That removes the parked state, so the lifetime, re-check and replacement arms are always polled and `Registration` is always released. Keep the `replacing` exemption only if the pump can no longer stall; otherwise count the replaced registration against the member until it is actually dropped. Add the two probes as tests.

**M2. The delivery gate multiplies every event into one fenced data-pool transaction per subscriber, on the same two-connection pool that serves commands and queries.** `app/src/events/routes.rs:59-61` → `storage::Data::can_open` (`app/src/storage/sharing.rs:165-181`): per event × per same-org subscriber, `Transaction::begin_until` on `stores.data` (ADR-004: `max_size(2)` per role, `pool.rs:653`; acquisition 5 s, `pool.rs:25`) running two `precheck`s (each `current_member` + `entitlement` + `permissions`: ~8 round trips). The command route (`commands/routes.rs`, `stores.data.commit_record_until`) and the query routes (`query/routes.rs:368,480,703,751`) use the same pool. With S subscribers in an organization, one committed record costs S gate transactions before the next command can get a lease; at the stated bound (256 streams) a single commit occupies the pool for seconds, and a burst of commits by any member turns commands/queries into `Error::Deadline`/503 for everyone on the task. The 30 s idle re-check adds `fence_member` (which takes `genui_lock` row locks `FOR SHARE` on `memberships`) ≈ 8 tx/s at 256 streams, steady state. The gate also runs for subscribers who obviously cannot open the work (every same-org event is judged for every same-org stream). Not probed (needs a sized fixture; the campaign opens two streams).
Fix: judge once per event, not once per subscriber: on each event run one scoped transaction that lists the principals who can open `(app, work)` now (the `openable_works` clause inverted: `discover` on the app and `record_read`/unrevoked creator on the work, `run`, live `source_read`), then deliver to the subscribers whose principal is in that set; keep `member_current` on refusals and ticks. If that is too much for November 2, at minimum (a) a per-stream judgment budget (token bucket) and (b) a short-TTL (≤ `RECHECK`) verdict cache keyed `(principal, app, work)`, and record the amplification factor in ADR-011 §Consequences (it is not among the five debt items).

**M3. The share model is "copy the sharer's grants onto the teammate", not "the teammate opens it under the teammate's own permissions", and the evidence cites an ADR-011 amendment that does not exist at `b704274`.** ADR-011 §Decision 1 (`docs/architecture/ADR-011-NOV-2-EMERGENCY-PIVOT.md:13`): "shares it with a teammate, who opens it under the teammate's own permissions"; APP-095 "Why": "the teammate sees the app only under the teammate's own grants." The definer (`0008_sharing.sql:93-120`) inserts `discover`/`run` on the app, `record_read` (and `record_write`) on the work **and `source_read` on every source dependency** for the grantee. `source_permissions` is keyed `(org, principal, source)` (`0002:205-210`): the copied `source_read` is organization-wide, not scoped to the shared work, so a grantee may afterwards read that source through any app they can `run` (and they now `run` this app). The ticket's acceptance 1 therefore passes trivially (the teammate's "own grants" are the sharer's, copied) and acceptance 4 ("sharing grants no access the definitions do not carry") holds only for the shared work, not for the source. There is no unshare route (acceptance 3 "revoking the share" is operator `revoke-permission`, one row at a time, and `source_read`/`run` cannot be distinguished from grants the teammate held before: `inserted` is returned to the sharer but not stored, and the audit event records only `sha256(grantee ‖ granted list)` (`0008:123-124`), so the set cannot be reconstructed from the chain). `evidence/APP-095/README.md` ("Controlling text") cites "the 2026-10-30 amendment ('sharing with a teammate under the sharer's own permissions; real-time updates (a server-sent event stream re-authorized at delivery)')" — `grep` finds no such text in ADR-011 at `b704274`; the ADR's only APP-095 text is the five debt items.
Fix: either amend ADR-011 §1 (and APP-095 "Why") to say what is built ("under the sharer's permissions, copied; `source_read` is organization-wide and survives unshare"), or scope the copy (grant `source_read` only when the grantee already holds it, i.e. drop lines 110-120 and let the work's `source_read` clause deny the teammate who lacks it — that is the "teammate's own grants" reading). Persist the granted set (a `work_shares` row keyed `(org, sharer, grantee, app, work)` with the inserted list, or the list beside the digest) so an unshare can reverse exactly what the share added. Correct the README citation.

### Minor

**m1. `inserted:false` tells the sharer which permissions the grantee already held.** `app/src/sharing/mod.rs:176-179`, `storage/sharing.rs:44`. A sharer learns a teammate's prior `run`/`discover`/`source_read` holdings from the response. Same-organization, low value; still a permission-table read the sharer does not otherwise have (`/v1/members` deliberately shows labels only). Fix: return `granted` without `inserted` (keep `changed()` server-side for the hub hook), or document it as intended.

**m2. Session-resolution store errors answer 404 on the new routes but 503 on the query routes.** `app/src/sharing/mod.rs:57` (`Ok(Lookup::Absent) | Err(_) => None` → uniform 404) vs `app/src/query/routes.rs:191-204` (`Err(error) => storage_refusal(&error)` → 503 for non-`Denied`). Fails closed, so not a security issue; inconsistent with design §9 (503 reserved for store outage) and with the sibling routes. Fix: map `Err(Error::Denied)`/`Absent` to 404 and other errors to `http::unavailable()` as the query routes do, or state the deliberate choice in the module notes.

**m3. The grantee's membership is read without a lock.** `0008_sharing.sql:75-76` (`SELECT m.status … WHERE principal_id=p_grantee`, no `FOR SHARE`) while the sharer's row is `FOR SHARE` (`:71-72`). An operator `revoke-membership` of the grantee (member `FOR UPDATE`, `admission_operator.py:1043-1055` order) interleaving after that read commits permission rows for a now-revoked member (the FK takes `KEY SHARE`, which does not conflict with the operator's non-key `UPDATE`). Harmless under status gating (`current_member`, `fence_member`), and the rows cannot be used, but they persist. Fix: `FOR SHARE` on the grantee row too (lock order unchanged: `organizations` → sharer → grantee, both members; no path locks members in the opposite order), or accept and note.

**m4. `GET /v1/members` takes the `genui_lock` fences for a read.** `storage/sharing.rs:87` (`fence_member` → `lock_member_authority` → `organizations FOR SHARE`, `memberships FOR SHARE`). A listing needs `current_member` + `entitlement` (what `openable_works` uses); the row locks only add contention with operator `FOR UPDATE` and make a read answer 409-class `busy`/404 under lock waits. Fix: use the listing's precheck (no fence).

**m5. Idle re-check bursts after a stall.** `events/mod.rs:313` `interval_at` default `MissedTickBehavior::Burst`: after any pause longer than `RECHECK`, several `member_current` transactions fire back-to-back. Set `MissedTickBehavior::Skip`. (Moot once M1 is fixed, cheap regardless.)

**m6. Ticket log command cannot run as written.** `Tickets/0-backlog/APP-095.md:49` quotes the campaigns by short name with `--exact`; `--exact` requires the full module path (0 tests run otherwise). Copy the README's spelling.

## Claims I failed to refute

- **No escalation through the definer.** `0008_sharing.sql:65-91`: scope must equal `(p_org, p_sharer, current epoch)`; organization and sharer membership active and locked `FOR SHARE`; grantee active in the same organization (RLS `tenant_scope` applies to `genui_admit`: FORCE RLS, not owner, `NOBYPASSRLS`); sharer must be the unrevoked creator or hold `share_work`, hold `run`, hold `record_read` (or creator), and `record_write` only when requested and held. Writes (`:93-120`) are exactly `discover`/`run` the sharer holds, `record_read`, optional `record_write`, and `source_read` the sharer holds on the work's dependencies; `share_work`, `edit_definition`, `review_publish`, `export`, `admin_policy` are never written and no caller input names a permission (`storage/sharing.rs:133` passes ids and a bool). A grantee is neither creator nor `share_work` holder and cannot re-share (campaign check 5). Cross-organization: `p_org` must equal the session scope, inserts carry `p_org`, RLS `WITH CHECK` applies, and the Rust layer uses the trusted request's organization only (`storage/sharing.rs:133`). Rust prechecks the same `record_read` clause as every reader (`authorization.rs:403`: creator-or-grant, `run`, live `source_read` on every dependency) before the fence and re-reads `record_write` after it (`storage/sharing.rs:124-132`).
- **Definer hygiene.** `SECURITY DEFINER STRICT VOLATILE`, `SET search_path=pg_catalog,pg_temp`, every relation schema-qualified, `PUBLIC` revoked, EXECUTE to `genui_workspace` only (schema campaign asserts control/data/worker cannot execute), owner `genui_admit` via the same temporary `CREATE` as 0003 (`:143-147`). PL/pgSQL OUT names `scope`/`permission`/`resource_id` are never ambiguous: every column reference is table-qualified and INSERT column lists are not substituted. No dynamic SQL; the Rust side binds parameters and validates ids (`valid_id`, `valid_identifier`). `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` relies on the four permission tables' primary keys (`0002:191-209`); a repeat writes nothing and appends no event (`:121`, campaign check 4).
- **Lock order (D7.9).** Rust: precheck → `fence` (`lock_member_authority`: `organizations FOR SHARE` → member `FOR SHARE`) → definer: `organizations FOR SHARE` → sharer `FOR SHARE` → permission inserts → `audit_heads(admission) FOR UPDATE` (`:125-126`). Same prefix and tail as `advance_member_epoch` (`0003:454-463`) and the command order; no path takes the admission head before a member row, so no cycle. The head row lock serializes appenders; `UNIQUE (org, writer_role, previous_hash)` backs it.
- **Audit chain integrity.** Body (`:129-133`) is byte-for-byte the `advance_member_epoch` shape (`0003:466-470`): keys in forma-json-v1 order, whole-second UTC `occurred_at`, `sha256:` hex, `previous_hash = head_hash` read under `FOR UPDATE`, head advanced in the same transaction. Campaign check 12 recomputes the chain with `verify` and finds the kind on no other chain. The `admission_chain` policy is widened in place and the schema campaign's `CHAIN_KINDS` literal matches it.
- **Grants.** A4 extension is column SELECT only (`:39-44`); INSERT remains 0003's; no UPDATE/DELETE added. `qualify_tenant_schema.py` `ADMIT_GRANTS` lists exactly these columns and the set equality assertion passed (362). `FOR SHARE` on `organizations`/`memberships` is enabled by the pre-existing lock-enabling UPDATE columns.
- **Catalogs and pins.** v1–v7 and 0001–0007 and `catalog-query.json` are byte-identical to `9cf52c2`; `manifest.json` hashes match the files; `check-schema-pins.py` agrees at 8; the native campaign's unsupported/restored literals moved to 9/8.
- **Uniform 404.** `http::not_found()` on every refusal path of `/v1/members`, `/v1/work/{app}/{work}/share` and `/v1/events` (no cookie, ended, absent, store error, `Denied`, malformed ids via `UniformPath`, no store). Campaign check 8 compares bodies byte-for-byte across existing/nonexistent works and no-session; `events::tests` asserts the exact body. Timing/branching before the session is the same for existing and nonexistent works (ids validated syntactically only, then session, then storage).
- **CSRF / fetch metadata.** The share POST applies `csrf::same_origin` before anything else (`sharing/mod.rs:131`), identical to the command route (`commands/routes.rs:357`), and requires `application/json` (no simple-form CSRF). The two GETs (`/v1/members`, `/v1/events`) follow the project's GET convention (no fetch-metadata check, like `/workspace` and `/w/{app}/{work}`); the session cookie is `SameSite=Lax` (`cookies.rs:83`) so a cross-site `EventSource`/`fetch` carries no cookie, and no CORS headers are set, so nothing is readable cross-origin.
- **SSE tenant isolation and delivery.** Events of another organization are dropped before any lookup (`mod.rs:328`); a deliverable event needs `can_open` **now** for the subscriber's own `TrustedRequest` (`discover` on the app, `record_read` on the work, `current_member` at the stream's epoch, live entitlement); `Denied` while current is `Skip`, `Denied` while not current is `Close`; a store error is `Skip` (nothing disclosed). The document carries ids and a timestamp only (`mod.rs:100-107`). Sign-out and operator permission/membership revocation advance the epoch (`admission_operator.py:1051`), so the next event or tick closes the stream (campaign check 11). The one-stream-per-session key is a domain-separated digest, not the cookie (`routes.rs:42-51`).
- **Recorded debt is honestly recorded** for items 1–5 of ADR-011 §Consequences (seventh definer, kind on two chains, labels, in-process hub, stream vs session lifetime; the idle sweep deletes the session without an epoch advance, so the stream can outlive a destroyed session by up to the hour — that is debt item 5).
