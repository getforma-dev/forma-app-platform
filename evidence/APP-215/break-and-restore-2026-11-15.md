## What changed, in one paragraph

**1.** The three service-wide sign-in windows are raised out of a 25-person trial's reach (`SIGNIN_GLOBAL` 6,000/hour, `CALLBACK_GLOBAL` 8,000/hour, `INVITE_GLOBAL` 2,000/day; every per-address window unchanged). **2.** The audited command route takes the member's existing `MEMBER_WRITES` window (120 an hour) after the request is shaped and answers 429 `too_many_requests` with `Retry-After` over it. **3.** The hot parser path (`boxes::inspect::run`) takes one of two slots before it spawns the parser host, waits at most 10 s and refuses `parse_busy` past that. **4.** Every model call made for a member first takes one slot of that member's own daily allowance (80 a day, `GENUI_MODEL_DAILY_CALLS_PER_MEMBER`) at the one choke point (`Anthropic::admit`), before the process cap of 1,000, which stays; a member whose allowance is reached reads the same 429 `daily_limit` scoped `member` and the canvas says it is theirs. **5.** The shared page's records card labels creators with the neutral handle for a guest (`directory::creator_label`, the function the record components already used). **6.** A guest share no longer requires the sponsor's membership epoch to equal the pinned one, so a sponsor's sign-out no longer kills every friend link; membership status, entitlement, `record_read` and share authority still fail closed, and removal ends the share. **7.** `Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000; includeSubDomains` on every answer, from the outermost router layer. No migration, no deployment-contract change.

## Limits

- **No independent review yet.** Fix 6 is an authorization change (the clause a guest's every read passes) and gets a focused review; the reviewer should read `sponsor_clause_for` and confirm each remaining predicate ends on removal (`admin_remove_member` sets `memberships.status='revoked'` and `entitlements.status='revoked'`, both in the clause) and that `check_guest` still pins the share row's `sponsor_epoch`.
- **The allowance is per process.** Like the process cap (ADR-007 D7.5, D7.18), the member table lives in the one task's memory; a restart starts every count at zero. A member's allowance is 80 calls, not 80 compositions: a composition is one router call plus up to three generation attempts, Ask and Shape one each, a folder brief one per PDF plus one.
- **The member-scoped body is judged on the live counts.** `daily_limit_for` says "member" when the member's own allowance is reached at answer time, whatever the process cap says (nothing of theirs is sent today either way); a member under their allowance who is refused reads the workspace's words, which is the case.
- **`parse_busy` on the upload path stores the file.** The preview says the readers were busy; the member tries the preview again. The two-slot bound is per process; the original-file viewer's two slots (`SHEET_READERS`) and the editable import's one are separate, so up to five parser hosts can still run at once (two here, two viewer, one import), each under its own 2.5 GiB kill line.
- **HSTS is sent on every answer, including plain-HTTP dev listeners,** where browsers ignore it; the header is not conditioned on `X-Forwarded-Proto`. fAuth's host (`auth-staging.getforma.dev`) is the infrastructure review's own item and not this release.
- **The runbook interim appends no audit event.** The exact SQL marks the row revoked under Victor's word; the migration-bearing fix (sharing M2) will append `permission.revoked` and let `admin_remove_member` do it.
- **Browser look.** No browser ran here; the member-scoped sentence renders through the existing refusal paths. Victor's QA sees it when a member's allowance runs out.
- **CI was not observed** by the builder; the PR carries the exact head.
