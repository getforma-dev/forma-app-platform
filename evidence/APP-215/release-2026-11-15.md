## What changed for members

All seven fixes are below; the [ticket](../../Tickets/0-backlog/APP-215.md) and the PR have the details.
1. The global sign-in, callback and invite windows are 10× larger, so junk traffic can't lock everyone out.
2. Commands have their own window of 300 an hour per member (`COMMAND_WRITES`). The other row-creating routes keep 120 an hour.
3. Heavy file parsing runs at most 2 at a time, with a bounded wait.
4. Each member gets a daily AI allowance of 80 calls, under the existing organization cap of 1,000.
5. Guests see neutral names.
6. Guest shares survive the sponsor's sign-out. The residual risk is recorded in ADR-014.
7. HSTS is sent on every response.

These limits are compiled defaults for now. Making them per-organization plan data is follow-up A13 in [POST-CLASS-FOLLOW-UPS.md](../../docs/planning/POST-CLASS-FOLLOW-UPS.md).
