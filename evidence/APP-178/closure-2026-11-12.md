## The decision behind box 1

The first revision (`a251076`) asserted that a suspension removes a guest's connected link for good. The independent review (B1) found that the result depended on whether the guest happened to look during the suspension. It also found that this contradicted box 1 ("restoring the organization restores access"), the "paused" label in management, and ADR-014's rule that a refusal must not silently delete placement.

Victor delegated the product call on 2026-11-12. The decision is recorded in [ADR-014, addendum 2026-11-12](../../docs/architecture/ADR-014-LIVE-APP-SHARING.md#addendum-2026-11-12-a-suspended-organization-pauses-connected-links): **a suspension pauses connected links.** It is implemented in `app/src/storage/shared_access.rs`:
- `linked_apps` asks `link_paused` before unlinking. The question is whether the grant is live in every respect apart from the sponsor organization's state (active or suspended). Accepting either state keeps a link whose organization is restored in the middle of a listing; this was re-review r1, reproduced by the reviewer with a widened window.
- `live_clause_for` and `sponsor_clause_for` take the organization's state as a parameter, so the paused question and the live question share one predicate.
- Revocation, version or placement changes, expiry and sponsor loss still unlink.

## Limits

- The campaign uses synthetic verified identities and the native PostgreSQL fixture. It makes no hosted or AWS claim.
- "Does not reveal" is proved at the store level, where `accept_live_share` and `shared_auth_target` each refuse expired and unknown keys identically. The HTTP layer maps `Denied` to the uniform 404, per APP-154; this campaign does not assert that over HTTP.
- A paused link counts toward the guest's placement cap while it is hidden. The guest cannot remove it from the canvas until it shows again, but can remove it by its key (ADR-014 addendum).
