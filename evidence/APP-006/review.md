## Resolved pre-freeze finding

**P2 — final key freshness/generation needed a delivery fence.** Initial inspection found that `key()` returned only copied key bytes; final token-expiry/deadline checks could release an identity after the key's short freshness expired or another refresh invalidated/replaced the cache. Before the reviewed candidate froze, the implementer introduced a key lease with generation/expiry. `verify_inner` now acquires the final cache mutex, then rechecks token time, overall deadline and the lease while retaining the guard through the return decision (`src/lib.rs:234`, `src/lib.rs:379`). Independent delayed-expiry and concurrent-refresh probes pass. There is no unresolved finding on this boundary.

## Limits and remaining acceptance

The public access JWK has no per-key retirement timestamp. Local source compatibility and injected rotation do not prove hosted KMS permissions, custody, fleet drain, real rotation/outage or key rollback.

The candidate does not perform PKCE/state/code exchange, register the intended tenant/client/callback, mint a browser session, enforce application membership or qualify an unknown app principal. These remain APP-006's hosted/consumer gates and the APP-009 integration seam. No fake organization picker or alternate sign-in service was introduced. Real deployment/image/health receipts and integrated hosted recovery/rollback remain absent, so APP-006 must remain Blocked regardless of this limited verdict.
