# APP-104 closure, 2026-11-12

Lead mapping by Claude (Opus 5.5), part of the [closure pass](../closure-2026-11-12/README.md).

## What happened to this ticket's behavior

- **October 30–November 1: built and landed.** The library pane, the clean board, the slash commands, the Figma-style controls, card stacks and library drag were built on `pivot/pitch-mvp` (commits `3a3d7c6` and `d9c4a4a`, among others) and on `pivot/app-104-library-drag`. That branch is now `main`. The October 30 browser walk in the log checked each box with synthetic sessions.
- **November 2: partly replaced on purpose.** [ADR-018](../../docs/architecture/ADR-018-WHOLE-APP-VIEWS.md), delivered by APP-168 (Done, PR 48), made an app one canvas object. Its components cannot be placed or removed loose from the Library, and per-card stacks became the whole-app Stack view. ADR-018 states that it "supersedes older fan-out/per-app-subcard UX instructions without rewriting their historical evidence."
- **Fences** were never built. That box moves to [APP-177](../../Tickets/0-backlog/APP-177.md) instead of being ticked.
