## Findings

### MAJOR-1: "Stop sharing with everyone" can answer success while the organization-wide share stays live

**Where.**

- Root cause, W4's definer: `app/migrations/0024_private_entries_and_roles.sql:271-272`. The live share is read `FOR UPDATE`, and :283 returns `('none', false)`, meaning nothing was live.
- How it surfaces:
  - `app/src/storage/sharing.rs:349-351`: `revoke_organization_share` maps that to `Ok(false)`;
  - `app/src/sharing/mod.rs:397-404`: the route answers 200 `{"role":"none"}`;
  - `app/admin/src/sharing/dialog.ts:256`: the dialog then shows "Stopped sharing with everyone."

**Failure scenario, reproduced deterministically by probe P3.**

1. The organization-wide share is live as viewer.
2. The owner changes it to contributor, and that transaction has not yet committed.
3. Hal, a `share_work` holder, presses "Stop sharing with everyone". His call blocks on the viewer row's lock.
4. The owner commits. In READ COMMITTED, Hal's `FOR UPDATE` re-checks the old row, finds it revoked, and skips it. His statement's snapshot cannot see the new contributor row, so the definer answers "nothing to revoke".
5. Hal is told the share stopped. The live share is `contributor`. Nia, who has no grant of her own, can still open the work (`can_open` true).

Probe P2 drove the same pair through the route 12 times and always got the benign order. The window is a few milliseconds. In a 25-person test only Victor and his helper share, so it is unlikely, but it is a revoke that silently does nothing.

Probe P1 shows the same root cause from another side. Two concurrent first-time "Share with everyone" calls ended in a 503 for one caller in 6 of 8 rounds (the partial unique index `work_shares_everyone_live`). Integrity held each time: one live row and exactly one `permission.granted` event.

**Suggested fix (W4).**

- In `set_work_everyone`, serialize writers per work before reading the live share. For example, take the admission `audit_heads` row lock that the function already takes later, or lock a stable per-work row, before the `SELECT … FOR UPDATE` at :271. Then the next statement sees the other writer's committed row. This turns the lost revoke into a real revoke, and the 503 into "unchanged" or "replaced".
- Release A's committed record (`ops/promotion/schema-release.json`) ends at database version 22, so 0024 is in no reviewed release yet. It can still be corrected before release B.
- **W2b's part:** add P3 as a regression check in `share-routes`, through the route (a held owner transaction, then the route's `none`, then assert no live row).

### MAJOR-2: one person's share cannot be undone in the product, and the operator path leaves the dialog wrong

**Where.**

- `app/src/sharing/mod.rs:169`: `MEMBER_REVOKE = false`.
- No `revoke_work_share` implementation exists on any trial branch; the only mention is W2b's own comment. Checked with `git grep` on `trial/w1`, `w2`, `w2b`, `w3`, `w4` and `plan/trial-25-readiness`.
- The operator's `revoke-permission` only gains `record_review` on `trial/w3-ops` (`GRANTABLE`). It deletes permission rows but leaves the `work_shares` row live.
- `Workspace::work_sharing` (`app/src/storage/sharing.rs:468`) lists people from `work_shares.role`, so after such a revoke the dialog keeps showing the old access.

**Failure scenario.** Victor shares an app with a member as "Can review all entries" by mistake. Selects default to "Can view", but nothing confirms the reviewer choice. That member now sees every member's private entries, the point of D7. The only undo is:

- removing the member entirely (W2's `/admin`); or
- an operator `revoke-permission` after W3 lands, which signs them out. After it, the dialog still lists them as a reviewer.

**Suggested fix.** Before the codes go out, do one of these:

- **(a)** W4 lands `revoke_work_share`, W2b adds the route, and the gate flips (the client function exists; see NIT-1 on its body); or
- **(b)** the trial runbook records the operator undo, it marks the `work_shares` row revoked as well, and Victor accepts in writing that the dialog has no per-person remove.

Both MAJOR-2 and MINOR-7 would also be eased by a confirmation step whenever the reviewer role is chosen.

### MINOR-1: `contributor_not_allowed` has no end-to-end test

**Where.** The route-level check rests on `work_sharing`'s roles (`app/src/storage/sharing.rs:454-455`) and `role_refusal` (`app/src/sharing/mod.rs:153-163`). The campaign's only non-owner sharer, Hal, holds `record_write`.

**Failure scenario.** G1 shows a regression that offers "Can add and edit their entries" to a sharer without `record_write` passes every test. The dialog would offer a role the server then refuses with the uniform 404 and "That share was refused", instead of the plain 422.

**Fix.** In `share-routes`, give a `share_work` holder `record_read` only. Assert roles `["viewer"]`, then 422 `contributor_not_allowed` (byte-identical whoever the grantee is) for both POSTs, with nothing written.

### MINOR-2: the dialog's behavior is not tested, only its helpers

**Where.**

- `app/admin/src/sharing/dialog.ts:180` (`render` takes roles from `offeredRoles`) and :193 (the stop control's `hidden`).
- `app/admin/test/team-sharing.test.ts:156-161` checks the CSS fix with a regular expression over the stylesheet, not computed visibility.

**Failure scenario.** G2 and G3 show that a dialog offering reviewer to every sharer, with an always-visible "Stop sharing with everyone", passes all 314 canvas tests. Only the one-off smoke guards it.

**Fix.** Test `teamSection` against a small DOM, as the existing canvas tests build one. Render the sections for `roles: ["viewer","contributor"]` and `organization.role: null`, and assert the option values and the stop control's `hidden`.

### MINOR-3: on a store outage the organization section silently disappears

**Where.** `app/admin/src/sharing/dialog.ts:267`. When the first read fails for any reason other than 404, `section.hidden = sharing === null` keeps the section hidden. The plain refusal copy is written into that hidden section.

**Failure scenario.** A transient 503 or timeout. The sharer sees only "Someone outside your organization" and may email a private link to a colleague instead.

**Fix.** Hide the section only for the 404, the "you cannot share this" answer. For any other failure, show the section with the status message and a retry.

### MINOR-4: concurrent organization-wide shares, and the copy members see

**Where.** `app/src/sharing/mod.rs:398-400` announces `app.shared` only when a role is set. A revoke or downgrade announces nothing. The canvas handler (`app/admin/src/canvas/board.ts:1518`) tells every recipient "An app was shared. Open its private link to review access."

**Failure scenarios.**

- **P1:** a double submit from two tabs, or owner and helper at once, answers "Sharing is unavailable right now" although the share was made. The cause is the same as MAJOR-1.
- **Revoke:** after "Stop sharing with everyone", members' open canvases are not told. The server refuses them correctly, but the screen is stale until a reload.
- **Copy:** every organization-wide share, and every role change of one, sends the "private link" sentence to all 25 members. There is no private link for an organization share.

**Fix.** The MAJOR-1 fix also removes the 503. Announce something when access ends or narrows. Give `app.shared` copy that fits a member share, for example "An app was shared with you. It is in your apps."

### MINOR-5: "Can add and edit their entries" is accurate only for private (`own`) collections

**Where.** `app/admin/src/sharing/api.ts:76`. Per 0024's header, only `visibility: "own"` collections limit a member to their own entries. In a shared collection, a contributor's `record_write` edits everyone's entries.

**Failure scenario.** A member gives contributor access on an app with a shared collection, expecting the grantee to touch only their own entries.

**Fix.** Use "Can add and edit entries". Or compute, in Rust, whether the work has `own` collections and pass the wording as data (`app/admin/AGENTS.md`: compute flags in Rust).

### MINOR-6: test gaps outside the dialog

None of the following is asserted:

- An organization-wide share against a member of a second organization, at the route. The guard is structural: session scope, RLS `tenant_scope`, and `organization_share`'s `os.organization_id={SCOPED_ORG}`. Neither `share-routes` nor `private-entries` uses a second organization.
- MCP's `forma_share` success path, and `record_write: true` mapping to contributor. `mcp_campaign.rs` calls `SHARE` only for refusals (:1025, :1040, :1296).
- 429 and 413 on the two POSTs and the new GET.

**Fix.** Add one canary-organization member to `share-routes`: a byte-identical 404 on all three routes, and `can_open` false while the share is live. Add one successful `forma_share` with `record_write: true` to the MCP campaign, asserting `record_write` copied.

### MINOR-7: reviewer for everyone is one click

**Where.** `app/admin/src/sharing/dialog.ts:251`. The "Access for everyone" select offers "Can review all entries" whenever the sharer may grant it.

**Failure scenario.** One click makes every member a reviewer, so everyone sees every private entry. D9 allows it, and it is undone by "Stop sharing with everyone" (subject to MAJOR-1). Exposure is immediate.

**Fix.** Confirm before granting reviewer to everyone, naming the consequence in plain words. Or leave reviewer out of the organization select for the trial.

### NIT-1: the gated revoke pins a guessed contract

`app/admin/src/sharing/api.ts:139-142` and `team-sharing.test.ts:58-63` fix the revoke body as `{"grantee_principal_id","role":"none"}` on the person route. Today the server refuses that body with 400 (`parse_share` refuses `none`). Flipping `MEMBER_REVOKE` without a matching route would ship a control that always says "That request was not understood". Make the client follow W4's route when it exists, and treat the current test as a placeholder.

### NIT-2: the stop control is hidden whenever the work is person-by-person only

`app/admin/src/sharing/dialog.ts:186` hides the whole organization form when `organization.available` is false, including "Stop sharing with everyone". No current writer can add a source dependency or folder file to an existing work, so this cannot happen today. Showing the stop control whenever `organization.role` is set costs nothing.

### NIT-3: the box share call in a campaign carries a role it does not have

`app/src/storage/box_campaign.rs:2222` sends `"role":"viewer"` to `POST /v1/boxes/{box}/share`. That route has no role and ignores unknown members (`app/src/boxes/routes.rs:2011-2017`). Restore the original body.

### NIT-4: misplaced comment

`app/src/storage/mod.rs:211` puts the APP-192 comment above `mod qualification`. `mod share_routes_campaign` is at :225.

### NIT-5: each POST builds the whole dialog answer to judge standing

`standing` (`app/src/sharing/mod.rs:256-266`) calls `work_sharing`, which reads the member list (up to 100), the live organization share and the person-only test on every POST. A narrower read of roles plus availability would do.

### NIT-6: copy

The `organization_share_unavailable` text says "your own data sources" (`app/admin/src/sharing/api.ts:147`), but the sharer may be a `share_work` holder whose sources are not theirs. "This app uses data sources or folder files…" is accurate.

## Verified as sound

- **Enumeration.** A caller without share authority cannot tell an existing work from a nonexistent one, or learn who it is shared with:
  - the refusal order is 403, then the 404 for ids and session, then 429 (per member, not per work), then 413/400 (body syntax only), then standing;
  - `work_sharing`'s precheck and its authority query both answer `Denied`, so the 404 is uniform;
  - the campaign asserts byte-identical 404s for anonymous callers, Nia, Rev (including Rev's reviewer request), a nonexistent work, and a grantee who is nobody, revoked or the sharer;
  - B5 confirms the GET's guard.
- **The 422s.** `role_refusal` and `organization_share_unavailable` are judged from `work_sharing`, which reads only the sharer's standing, before any grantee is parsed into storage. The campaign shows identical bytes for five grantees. `organization_share_unavailable` reveals only a property of a work the sharer can already open.
- **`work_sharing` and the definers.**
  - It uses the same authority clause: the unrevoked creator or `share_work`, with `run` from `app_permissions`.
  - Its contributor rule (`record_write` via `work_clause`) and reviewer rule (also `reviewer_clause`) match the definers' `v_write`/`v_review` and storage's `Permit::also`.
  - It runs in one `REPEATABLE READ READ ONLY` transaction with no `lock_member_authority` fence.
  - Labels come from `directory::label`, so no provider identity appears; the campaign asserts no `usr_`.
- **`principal_id` to sharers.** Acceptable. `GET /v1/members` already gives every active member each colleague's `principal_id`. The dialog shows only labels, and the test asserts no `principal_id` reaches `textContent`.
- **Organization isolation.** The organization comes from the session, `work_shares` is under `tenant_scope` RLS, and `organization_share` requires active membership in the scoped organization. A route-level test is missing (MINOR-6).
- **Same-origin.**
  - Both POSTs go through `csrf::same_origin`. It allows only `Sec-Fetch-Site: same-origin`, or no metadata with an exactly matching `Origin`, and refuses `same-site`, `none`, duplicates and a missing header (unit test and campaign).
  - The GET has no fetch-metadata check and needs none: the session cookie is `__Host-`, `SameSite=Lax` and `HttpOnly`, there is no CORS layer in `app/src`, and responses are `no-store`, `nosniff` and `no-referrer`. A cross-site page cannot read it, and a same-site subdomain gets no CORS grant.
- **Contract drift.** Every sender of the share body moved to a role. Checked with `git grep grantee_principal_id` and `record_write`:
  - canvas `actions.ts:172` (contributor) and `page.ts:38` (viewer default);
  - the dev seed;
  - the `actions`, `pitch`, `workflows`, `box` and `sharing` campaigns.

  Slack and MCP's draft flow call `share_work(…, Contributor)` directly. No document, script or operator tool posts the old body. The old encoding is refused with 400 (unit test, `share-routes`, `sharing`).
- **MCP.** `forma_share`'s published input schema (`app/src/mcp/tools.rs:170-177`) and its output are unchanged, and `record_write` maps through `ShareRole::from_record_write`. Refusing `record_write: true` from a sharer who lacks it predates W2b (W4's route already answered 404).
- **Audit and idempotency.** The definers still write one `permission.granted` per grant, and one `permission.revoked` plus one `permission.granted` per role change. A replay writes nothing. The campaign counts the rows.
- **Client.**
  - Roles are offered only from the server's list.
  - The request bodies are closed.
  - The answer is parsed strictly.
  - No classroom words appear (asserted by test and by reading).
  - Copy is plain and never names another member.
  - The CSS fix wins on specificity: `.share-team [hidden]` (0,2,0) beats `.share-danger` and `.share-everyone-actions` (0,1,0).

### The five focus areas

1. **`/share/add`.**
   - **Same front as `/share`.** It goes through `share_person` and so `share_request`: fetch metadata, ids, session, write limit, then the body.
   - **Closed body.** It refuses `none` and `record_write` with 400. `none` is refused before any standing is read, so the answer does not depend on the work.
   - **Same refusals afterwards.** Standing (uniform 404), then the 422 role refusals, then storage. The campaign asserts 400, 400, 422, 429, 404 and 403 (`:1066-1112`). CSRF is the shared `csrf::same_origin` check, and the cross-site 403 is asserted.
   - **No escalation.** `Ensure` copies only the sharer's held subset and never deletes (B31).
   - **The contributor question.** A member without share authority (the creator, or a `share_work` holder) gets the uniform 404, as Rev does. A `share_work` holder with `record_write` can give contributor, exactly as on `/share`. That fits D9 and ADR-011: copied, never conferred, and share authority unchanged.
   - **Enumeration.** None added.
2. **The mode split.**
   - `Set` is used only by the dialog's `/share` (`sharing/mod.rs:362`) and by the organization route (`:478`), which storage requires (`storage/sharing.rs:336`).
   - Every automatic path uses `Ensure`:
     - `/share/add`;
     - the composer tailor (`composer/routes.rs:1010`);
     - MCP's draft share (`mcp/tools.rs:1188`) and `forma_share` (`:1390`);
     - Slack (`slack/flows.rs:718`);
     - the adaptation (`sharing/mod.rs:601`).
   - The HTTP automatic callers all post to `/share/add`: the canvas approver step (`actions.ts:170`), the dev seed (`seed.rs:126`), and the `actions` and `workflows` campaigns.
   - The dialog's person share is `Set`, so an exact demotion works (campaign: `/share` as contributor lowers Fay).
   - One unused `Set` caller remains (NIT-9).
3. **Busy.**
   - Sharing produces `Error::Busy` only in `definer()`, on 55P03 and 40P01 (`storage/sharing.rs:158-172`). The only other producer, record snapshot capacity, is unreachable from these routes. `standing()` keeps mapping every other error to 503.
   - The answer is `{"code":"busy","status":429}` with `Retry-After: 2`. That is distinct from the write limit's `too_many_requests` (`http/mod.rs:117`), and the body names nothing.
   - On the removal path, though, a non-owner can reach the busy answer (MINOR-8).
4. **`owner_principal_id`.** Acceptable. It is returned only after `standing_in`'s share-authority check. Every member is already listed in `/v1/members`, so this adds only "who owns this app", which a sharer of that work can reasonably know. The picker needs it.
5. **Personal revoke.**
   - **Owner-only:** the definer checks `v_owner`, and `can_revoke_members` equals "the caller is the owner".
   - **Idempotent:** a repeat, or a person who holds nothing, answers 200 with the same body and writes nothing (campaign `:957`).
   - **Uniform 404:** for a `share_work` holder, a member without authority, a member of another organization, the owner naming themselves, and a nonexistent work, asserted byte-identical (B24 redone).
   - **Exception:** the lock ordering in MINOR-8.

### New findings

#### MINOR-8: a removal takes locks before it judges the caller

**Where.**

- `app/src/sharing/mod.rs:396-400`: `role: "none"` goes to `remove_access` before any standing is read.
- `app/src/storage/sharing.rs:426`: `revoke_work_share` uses `fence_member()`, with no work precheck.
- In 0024's `revoke_work_share`, the grantee's membership row is taken `FOR UPDATE` (:535) and the per-work advisory lock (:538) before the owner check (:540) and the work-existence check.

**Failure scenario.** Reproduced through the route.

- **P4a.** Nia has no access to the work, and the work does not exist. She posts `{"grantee_principal_id": Ana, "role": "none"}` to `/v1/work/{app}/{nonexistent}/share`. While that request is in flight, Ana's membership row is held `FOR UPDATE`: a concurrent `FOR SHARE NOWAIT` on it is refused with 55P03. Nia then gets the 404.
- **P4b.** While Ana's row is held, as any of Ana's fenced transactions holds it, Nia's same request answers `429 {"code":"busy"}` with `Retry-After: 2`, after 1133 ms, instead of the uniform 404. Released, it answers 404. A grantee who is nobody answers 404.

**Impact.**

- **No disclosure about the work.** It does not reveal whether the work exists, who owns it, or who has it.
- **A presence side channel.** Any member can learn that a colleague's row was held past `lock_timeout` (1000 ms, `storage/pool.rs:613`).
- **A nuisance lever.** Any member can make a colleague's fenced actions wait, bounded by `lock_timeout` per request and the 120-writes-per-hour member limit.

Low impact for a 25-person test.

**Suggested fix.** Either side fixes it:

- **W2b:** in `remove_access`, before the definer, read with no lock whether the caller owns the app and the work exists. Answer the 404 at once otherwise.
- **W4:** judge the owner and the work with plain reads before the `FOR UPDATE`, and re-check after locking.

Then add a campaign check: a non-owner's removal while the grantee's row is held answers 404 within `lock_timeout`.

The list already shows the higher role, so a plain sentence ("Only someone who can give that access can lower it") would cost nothing.
