## A gap found and closed

The verification asks for unit tests of the calculator parser, and the fourth box is the parser's refusal. No such test existed on `main`, and none tested the Tokyo clock either. Two tests were added on the test-gaps branch (`tests/closure-gaps`, commits `5ffd4a5` and `b2ba6b7`), in `app/admin/test/widgets-calculator-clock.test.ts`:
- **Calculator:** `12*(3+4)` = 84, precedence, power, and the keypad symbols. It refuses letters, eval-shaped input, other punctuation, exponent and hex literals, malformed and non-finite input, and expressions past the length and depth bounds. A tokenizer that skips an unknown character instead of refusing fails it.
- **Clock:** `zoneOf('Tokyo')` is `Asia/Tokyo`, and `clockParts` at a fixed UTC instant gives Tokyo's time and date.

This ticket is Done only once that branch is on `main`.
