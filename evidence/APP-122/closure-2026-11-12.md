## A gap found and closed

No test drove Shape's preview, Apply, Revert and Cancel on a card (box 3). `app/admin/test/shape-apply-revert.test.ts` (commit `f3505dd` on `closure/acceptance-gaps`) runs the real `runShape` path. It uses the real renderer, view evaluation and listing parser, with spies for placement, the dock host and the Library lookup, and `fetch` as the transport. It asserts:
- the preview state;
- the shaped label and Revert button after Apply;
- the restored card after Revert or Cancel;
- that the only requests are the listing read and `POST /v1/shape`.

Removing Revert's restore or Apply's label fails it. This ticket is Done only once that branch is on `main`.
