## Findings and fixes

1. **Narrow page did not reflow.** Resizing a page to 320 by 280 on a desktop
   kept the desktop columns and header. The app name and provenance consumed
   nearly the whole page, leaving the parts unusable. Layout now follows the
   page's width, including restoration, without replacing or moving its card
   nodes. The narrow header keeps its actions on a separate row, and the full
   app name remains available through its title attribute.
2. **A tall page could hide its resize corner.** New pages previously took the
   full natural height of all their parts. Initial pages now have a bounded
   window within the viewport; their contents scroll from the first open.
   Existing stored sizes remain honored, subject to the existing viewport clamp.
3. **The share list could be clipped by a short page.** Its maximum height now
   uses the space remaining inside the actual page, with canvas zoom accounted
   for. The list scrolls so its last member remains accessible.
